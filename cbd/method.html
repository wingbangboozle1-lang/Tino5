What the model does not do

  • It does not estimate the probability of a crash, a recovery, or any future rate.
  • It does not model state tax on Treasury interest, or California conformity with the home-sale exclusion.
  • It does not model the trust's own income tax, or the estate's tax position beyond the filing threshold.
  • It uses the cbe model's property numbers unchanged. Those are owner records, rounded, and unverified (flag F01).
