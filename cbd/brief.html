For the attorney

  1. Confirm the trust is an inter vivos or testamentary trust that passes present beneficial ownership (R&TC 63.2(e)(9)). Source: R&TC 63.2.
  2. Confirm no interest in a legal entity holds the home (R&TC 63.2(e)(8)). Source: R&TC 63.2.
  3. Confirm the home is the transferor's principal residence and that it stays one for the transferee within one year (R&TC 63.2(a)(1)(A)).
  4. Confirm the claim deadline in R&TC 63.2(f)(1)(A) and who will file it.
  5. Confirm the notice duties for the trustee and any notice of supplemental or escape assessment.

For the tax adviser

  1. Confirm the federal filing threshold for the year of death and whether Form 706 is needed (IRS FAQ).
  2. Confirm California treatment of Treasury interest on the parked proceeds. Not modelled here; flagged F04.
  3. Confirm California conformity with the federal home-sale exclusion. Not modelled; conservative for path F; flagged F05.
  4. Confirm the filing status, which changes the home-sale exclusion from $250,000 to $500,000 (IRS Topic 701).
  5. Confirm the basis of inherited property and the depreciation method for any letting period (IRS Topic 409).
