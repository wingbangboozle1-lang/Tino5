Break-even rent for Cupertino (Path B): Tax $41,390 + insurance $1,660 + water $1,480 + electricity $360 + maintenance 1% ($31,000) = $75,570/yr (≈ $6,300/mo) before vacancy/management/income tax. With Prop 19 (if it applied — it does not for rentals) it would be $61,630/yr. Any rent below break-even loses money on a cash basis. Decision checklist

  • Will you actually move in within 1 year and stay? If no, Prop 19 is gone — Path A is unavailable.
  • What is the bound insurance quote for 95014 HO-3 and 94122 landlord policy? Replace $1,660 planning figures.
  • What is the real Cupertino rent (lease quote) vs $75,570/yr break-even?
  • What is your housing cost elsewhere if you sell? Subtract it from Path C before comparing.
  • What is the cash amount (privately)? Does the estate exceed the indexed $15M/$30M threshold in the transfer year?
  • What is the solar tariff vintage? NEM 2.0 grandfathered vs Net Billing changes the electricity line by hundreds per year, not thousands — it does not drive the decision.
