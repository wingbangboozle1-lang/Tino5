The Inheritance Process, Step by Step (Trust-Held Real Estate, Death c. 2040–2050)

Revocable living trust → no probate for trust assets → but many deadlines still apply. Statute references: federal IRC, CA Probate Code, CA R&TC, BOE forms. This is a checklist for the family's attorney/CPA — not legal advice.

Golden rule: almost every deadline below runs from the date of death, not from when the family "gets around to it." Put a master calendar together in week 1. Missing the 1-year occupancy test forfeits Prop 19 forever — it is the single most expensive unforced error in this plan.
  1. Week 0 — First 72 hours. Secure both properties (locks, alarm, water/electric on). Order 10–15 certified death certificates from the county clerk/recorder (Santa Clara & SF). Notify the attorney who drafted the trust and the CPA. Locate: trust agreement (+ amendments), pour-over will, deeds (verify both homes are titled in the trust — anything outside needs the pour-over will / probate petition), insurance policies, most recent property-tax bills, solar true-up agreements, rent-roll and leases for Property B, mortgage/HELOC statements.
  2. Week 0 — Freeze the money picture. Inventory assets & liabilities at date of death (DOD). Pull FMV evidence: (a) comparative-market-analysis or broker price opinions for sanity, (b) retroactive date-of-death appraisals by a licensed CA appraiser for both houses — these set your §1014 basis and protect you on every later sale. Price Property B's units individually if the assessor might treat portions differently.
  3. Week 1 — Trustee formally accepts. Trustee signs the Acceptance of Trust and Certificate of Trust (CA Probate Code §18100.5) — banks/title companies want this, not the full trust. Obtain a trust EIN (IRS SS-4) if the trust earns income post-death (rent from Property B); trust income is reported on Form 1041 (+ CA Form 541). File Form 56 (Notice Concerning Fiduciary Relationship) with the IRS.
  4. Day ≤ 60 — Notify trust beneficiaries. The trustee must serve statutory notice of the trust administration (and of the trust's existence) on beneficiaries and heirs — CA Probate Code §16061.7 — typically within 60 days of the settlor's death. It starts the 120-day contest window; do not skip it. (Statute: leginfo.legislature.ca.gov — link follows standard pattern; content may change — confirm current text with counsel.)
  5. Day ≤ 150 — File BOE-502-D (Change in Ownership Statement — Death of Real Property Owner) with each county assessor (Santa Clara for Property A; SF Assessor-Recorder for Property B). Missing it risks penalty + an unmonitored reassessment. Attach death certificate copies; in probate, tie it to the inventory & appraisal filing. (BOE; R&TC §480(b); forms: BOE-19-P guide below)
  6. Month 1–2 — Valuation lock. Retroactive DOD appraisals final; basis file compiled (purchase records + DOD FMV + post-death capitals). For Property B: compute building vs land split and accumulated depreciation per unit — needed for future depreciation recapture math (≤25% federal unrecaptured §1250).
  7. Month 1–2 — Property tax strategy gate (the big one). For each property, decide within weeks — not months:
    (a) Property A: will an heir occupy it as principal residence within 1 year? If yes → plan BOE-19-P claim (file within 3 years; occupancy + BOE-266 homeowners' exemption within 1 year). If no → accept full reassessment and list the property for sale.
    (b) Property B: occupy one unit (assessor-confirmed Prop 19 treatment for that portion) or accept full reassessment (≈$21,240/yr at 2026 FMV, growing). Run the keep-vs-sell math in Decision Analysis with real numbers in hand.
  8. Month 2–3 — Insurance & risk transfer. Switch Property A to an HO-3 primary-residence policy (cheaper than landlord); Property B to a DP-3 landlord policy with loss-of-rents coverage (SF: consider earthquake endorsement — SF is high seismic risk; separate EQ policy required). Verify FAIR Plan fallback quotes if admitted carriers decline. Update loss payees, add trust as additional insured. See Insurance.
  9. Month 2–3 — Income & tenant operations (Property B). Notify tenants of the change of ownership/agent; re-register security deposits per SF Rent Ordinance; set up trust bank account; forward rent to trust EIN account; comply with rent ordinance notice rules (no rent change just because of an inheritance); confirm rent-board registration (SF Rent Board) where applicable.
  10. Month 3 — Utilities, solar & services. Transfer PG&E accounts; re-paper the solar interconnection/net-energy-metering true-up to the trust or heir; update water/sewer accounts; HOA (if any) changes; alarm/utility vendors.
  11. Month 3–6 — Tax filings (final/1041/541). Final Form 1040 for the decedent (income to DOD) due Apr 15 following year; trust Form 1041 + CA Form 541 for post-death income (rent, interest, dividends) annually until distribution completes; Form 1041-ES estimated payments if income > threshold; consider distribution of distributable net income (DNI) to beneficiaries (K-1s) to keep trust-level tax at the 37%-over-$16,000 trap rate (Rev. Proc. 2025-32 §4.01 Tbl 5).
  12. Month 6–9 — Federal estate tax check. Compute tentative gross estate (both houses at DOD FMV + cash + portfolios + retirement accounts + life insurance owned by decedent). If > filing threshold → file Form 706 within 9 months of death (or Form 4768 for a 6-month extension). If below threshold but surviving spouse exists → file Form 706 anyway to lock in portability ($30,000,000 combined shelter); Rev. Proc. 2022-32 grants a simplified late-election (≤5th anniversary) for smaller estates. Alternate valuation (IRC §2032, 6 months) available if values fell. IRS — Form 706 instructions · Rev. Proc. 2022-32
  13. Month 9–12 — Distribution & title. Once debts/taxes settled, distribute per trust: either (a) deed Property A to occupying heir (reassessment excl. if Prop 19 claimed), (b) deed both for sale, proceeds per formula, or (c) keep in trust. Record grant deeds with county recorders; update title/CLTA policies; change tax bills to new base year value as assessed.
  14. Month 12 — Prop 19 paperwork closes. If occupied: BOE-266 homeowners' exemption already filed within 1 yr; diarize BOE-19-P (3-year) filing with: death certificate, trust pages, deed into trust/heir, DOD appraisal, proof of occupancy. Confirm reassessment or exclusion on the next secured tax bill — errors are common; appeal within the county's assessment appeals window if invites/incorrect.
  15. Month 12–18 — Reinvest & rebalance. Execute the sale (if chosen): list → close → pay trust debts → distribute/reinvest proceeds per the target allocation (Treasury ladder + index core). Coordinate with CPA on year-of-sale reporting: DOD basis vs sale price → minimal/no federal gain; CA ordinary on any post-DOD appreciation; recapture analysis for Property B.
  16. Ongoing (annual). April 15: Form 1041/541 until trust terminates; ±2% property-tax growth cap monitoring; insurance re-marketing every 2 years (CA market volatile); annual trust review (beneficiaries, successor trustees, disclaimer/alternate-valuation powers); watch S.4196 / SALT / Prop 19 cap / federal indexation — see Pending legislation.
