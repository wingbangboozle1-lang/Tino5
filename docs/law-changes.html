Sensitivity of the decision to law change

The two rules that matter most are §1014 stepped-up basis and Proposition 19. Repeal of the step-up would make an immediate sale taxable on decades of appreciation. Tightening or repeal of Prop 19 would remove even the partial property-tax relief. Conversely, a restoration of broader parent-child exclusions would improve the hold case.

Because the transfer is 14–24 years away, the correct procedure is to re-verify every rule in the year of transfer rather than to assume the 2026 law persists. The site’s Source Registry is designed for exactly that re-check.
