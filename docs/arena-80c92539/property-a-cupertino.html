A.5 · What to do with Property A — recommendation

Recommend (in order of certainty):
  1. If the heir/household will occupy Property A as a principal residence within one year: keep it, claim the Prop 19 exclusion (BOE parent-child claim filed within 3 years; occupancy within 1 year), switch utilities, get a homeowner's exemption ($7,000 off assessed value), reprice insurance as primary residence (cheaper than landlord policy), and diarize the biennial cap adjustment.
  2. If the heir will not occupy it: list the property at death or as soon as probate/trust administration allows — the §1014 step-up resets basis to ≈ sale price, so the entire pre-death appreciation is never taxed (federal or CA). Reinvest net proceeds per the target allocation (index equity sleeve + T-bill ladder + I-bonds for the cash component).
  3. Do not let it drift as an accidental rental in year 1–2 without a decision gate; every month of delay forfeits Prop 19 (occupancy deadline) and enlarges the tax delta (≈$17,100/yr, indexed).
