T.5 · California Proposition 13 — the inherited-tax foundation

  • 1% base rate on factored base-year value + local voter-approved bonded indebtedness (typically lands at ≈1.1–1.35% in Santa Clara; ≈1.18% composite in SF for FY2025-26). BOE Publication 29 (California Property Tax: An Overview)
  • FBYV growth ≤ 2%/yr (Cal. Const. art. XIII A) — reassessed only on (a) change in ownership or (b) new construction. Market declines can be recognized (Prop 8). Same source.
  • Change in ownership at death counts (transfer to trust/children/estate) — the default outcome is full reassessment to FMV. Exclusions below.
  • Supplemental property-tax bills issue when a property is reassessed (the difference between old and new base, prorated); expect 1–2 catch-up bills ≈ (new − old) tax for the balance of the fiscal year + following year. BOE — Property Taxes home (supplemental rolls: RTC §§75–75.80; the dedicated BOE publication URL could not be verified 2026-11-10 — see Flagged for Review #site-links) + the county tax collector.
  • Homeowners' exemption: $7,000 off assessed value for an owner-occupied principal residence — claim it the moment the heir moves in (Prop 19 requires it within 1 year). BOE — Property Tax (search: Homeowners' Exemption)

T.7 · California income tax on the inherited assets

  • No CA inheritance, estate, or gift tax. California's former pick-up tax equals $0 for deaths after 2004 (federal credit repealed; CA never conformed since). FTB analysis, SB 726 (estate "pick-up" tax = $0)
  • Capital gains are taxed as ordinary income in CA — no preferential rate. Top CA rate 12.3% above the highest income-tax bracket (indexed annually; bracket threshold not restated here because the 2026 figure was not independently verified this session — see Flag F-03 note on unverified figures; current-year table: FTB tax-rate schedules) + 1% Mental Health Services Tax (Prop 63) on taxable income over $1,000,000 → 13.3% effective top rate. FTB — Capital gains and losses ("California does not have a lower rate for capital gains")
  • Federal estate tax paid on income in respect of a decedent is not deductible for CA (Schedule CA, Line 16). FTB Schedule CA (540) instructions
  • CA taxes worldwide income of residents; part-year and trust sourcing rules apply for estates/trusts (Form 541). FTB — Forms
  • CA does not conform to the federal SALT cap or to QBI/pass-through mechanics (check current-year instructions each filing season).

T.9 · Local charges to budget at transfer (not "taxes" but bills)

  • Supplemental secured tax bills (Santa Clara & SF): 1–2 catch-up bills after reassessment; due dates printed on the bill; failure to pay adds 10% penalties.
  • Documentary transfer taxes apply to purchases/sales — not to transfers by death/inheritance (no sale). A later sale triggers them on the seller's proceeds only in specific cities (SF Prop W / Santa Clara County rates) — consult the county recorder for the exact schedule (SF Treasurer & Tax Collector; Santa Clara County Tax & Treasurer-Tax Collector) — flagged: exact current DTT schedule not independently verified in this review.
  • Parcel taxes & direct assessments (SF: parks, libraries schools bonds; Santa Clara: school parcel taxes) appear on the secured bill — these are why the observed SF bill exceeds 1.18% of FBYV (see Flag #B-rate).
  • Homeowners' exemption ($7,000 assessed-value reduction) must be re-claimed by the heir. BOE — Property Tax
