Methodology & Sources — How Every Number on This Site Was Produced

M.1 · Verification standard

  • Laws, rates, and thresholds are cited to official sources: irs.gov (Rev. Proc., Pubs, Form instructions), congress.gov (statutes), law.cornell.edu (codified IRC), boe.ca.gov (Prop 13/19, LTAs), ftb.ca.gov, cbo.gov, fhfa.gov, insurance.ca.gov. Statutory sources verified by direct retrieval 2026-11-10 where feasible; two BOE deep links had moved (404) and were replaced with index pages or specific LTAs — see Log M.6.
  • If a value could not be confirmed from an official source, it is either shown as a flagged estimate, or left blank (e.g., earthquake premium, 2026 Property A tax bill, Cupertino market rent). No value was invented.
  • All dollar figures are rounded to the nearest $10 (derived rates like 1.34% are not rounded). Statutory and regulatory figures (e.g., the Prop 19 cap of $1,044,586; IRS bracket thresholds) are published verbatim because they are law, not personal data; rounding applies to financial estimates and user-provided figures only. Projections (2026→2060/2065) are assumption-based planning estimates — never presented as appraisals, quotes, or predictions.
  • Every table row on this site carries an inline source link or an explicit UNVERIFIED flag. The full citation register is M.4 below.

M.2 · Privacy protocol (top priority — applied before any content was written)

  • Omitted everywhere on this site: owner names, street addresses, ZIP codes, assessor parcel numbers, recorded-owner data, financial-institution details, trust names/dates, family members' identities, exact year-by-year DOD balances.
  • Properties are referred to only as "Property A — Cupertino (Santa Clara County)" and "Property B — Outer Sunset, San Francisco (San Francisco City & County)", exactly as framed in the engagement brief.
  • Year-by-year raw tax/assessment history (user-provided) is NOT published. Only derived, decision-relevant aggregates are shown: derived effective rate (≈1.34% Property A / user-reported Property B), projected reassessment deltas, and the verified ≤2%/yr FBYV growth rule. The raw history remains with the owner/CPA file; it was used only to derive those aggregates. Per Protocol P-4, the raw tables in the brief were treated as personal data contained in research material → internal use only.
  • meta robots noindex is set on every page; the site is not intended for search-engine discovery. If the owner later forks this into a public-marketing site, remove noindex consciously.
  • This public repository contains nothing else personal: COORDINATION.md is a process log (task board), and contains no personal or property-specific data beyond what is already public law/market data and city-level labels.
  • Nothing on this site is tax, legal, or investment advice. Before acting: retain a CA-licensed attorney + CPA; this document is their briefing book, not their substitute.

M.5 · Reproduction recipe

  1. Take user-provided current figures (rounded to $10).
  2. Project market value: V(t) = V₀ × 1.045^(t−2026).
  3. Project FBYV without transfer: B(t) = B₀ × 1.02^(t−base); tax = B(t) ÷ FBYV × rate.
  4. Reassessment at death: new base = FMV; tax = FMV × county rate; re-grows ≤2%/yr.
  5. Prop 19: taxable = max(FMV − E(t), FBYV), E(0) = $1,044,586 growing ≈2.15%/2yr.
  6. Portfolio: V(t+n) = V(t) × (1+r)^n; deltas vs keep shown net of 5.5% sale cost.
  7. Exclusion path: $15,000,000 × 1.023^(t−2026).

Computation performed with python3 (scripts in the session workspace, not committed). No spreadsheets with personal data exist on this site.
