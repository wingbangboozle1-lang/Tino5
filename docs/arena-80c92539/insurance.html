I.4 · Premium-saving levers (both properties)

  1. Shop at every renewal — the 2026 admitted-market reopening (new "Net Cost" commitments) means re-marketing 60–90 days before renewal each year.
  2. Wildfire hardening documentation — Class A roof, ember vents, defensible space → CDI "Safer from Wildfires" discounts; can be worth 5–15%. CDI — insurance.ca.gov (search “Safer from Wildfires”)
  3. Water-shutoff + leak sensors (smart valve) — commonly 2–5% discount + claim avoidance; high ROI given $4,380/yr water bills already paid to the utility.
  4. Raise deductibles thoughtfully — $2,500–$5,000 all-peril; keep the delta in a T-bill ladder.
  5. Umbrella — $2M personal umbrella covers both properties + future rentals; cheap relative to limits (often $200–$400/yr; unverified quote needed).
  6. Bundle A + auto if same carrier makes sense; compare against monoline specialists for high-value homes (Chubb/PURE/AIG-type markets via surplus lines).
  7. Annual replacement-cost recalc — insure to replacement cost (not market); CA reconstruction costs for high-spec homes have risen sharply and vary by region; no official per-sqft figure exists — get a carrier replacement-cost estimate; underinsurance = coinsurance penalties at claim time.
