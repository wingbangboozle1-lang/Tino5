Methodology of this recheck

  • Cadence. Annual, plus event-driven (new revenue procedures, new board letters, elections). This cycle runs 2026-11-17; the next full cycle is due November 2027, with a targeted check after the board's February 2027 letter (flag R4).
  • What counts as official. The statute, the agency's own publication (revenue procedure, letter to assessors, adopted rate schedule, fee schedule, survey page, index page, legislation file), read directly. Search engines and commentary are used only to find the official document and are never a value source.
  • Verdicts. CONFIRMED (read or re-checked against the named document), UPDATED (figure changed or newly published), OPEN (blank cell + flag + discovery action), PROPOSED (not law), PRIOR READ (line-read by the 2026-11-13/15 sessions and re-checked for consistency).
  • No inference. Where a value could not be read (flag R1), the cell is blank; components are published instead of a reconstructed total.
  • Rounding. Money to the nearest $10 unless the figure is an agency-published exact amount (tariffs, statutory caps, thresholds), which is quoted exactly and marked EXACT.
  • Privacy. County-level anonymisation: Property A (Santa Clara County) and Property B (San Francisco County). No names, street addresses, ZIP codes, parcel or account numbers on any page. Owner-supplied figures appear only as bands labelled owner-supplied.
  • Movement test. After the register update, the ranking of the paths is recomputed on the rebuild page; a change in any input that cannot bind within the modelled range is recorded as NO MOVE with its arithmetic.
