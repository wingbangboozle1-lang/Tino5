What this register is

Every decision-critical figure published on the live desks was re-checked on 13 November 2026 against the official document named in its own source link. Each document in the tables below was opened and read in this session unless the Verdict column says otherwise. Verdicts:

  • CONFIRMED — the official document was opened in this session and says what the desks publish.
  • CORRECTED — a published row disagreed with, or linked wrongly to, its source; the row or link was fixed and the fix is listed under Corrections applied.
  • OPEN — could not be confirmed from an official source in this session; the value stays flagged on the owning desk and nothing was inferred.
  • Referee-confirmed — the independent referee opened the document on 12 November 2026 (register referee-abae/constants.html); this session did not re-open it.

Privacy: this register uses Property A (the future trust inheritance) and Property B (the already-owned building) only. No names, street addresses, ZIP codes, parcel numbers or account numbers appear. Owner-supplied figures are referenced at $10 rounding, never at source precision.

Why this section exists: the owner side of this project is located in Japan. Under the scope rows above, a Japanese-resident heir can owe Japanese inheritance tax on the US house and on the cash in the same inheritance, at rates far above the effective US estate-tax rate once the US basic exclusion is sheltered. The decision between occupying, renting and selling should not be finalised until each heir's tax residence at the expected date of death is known (see the Questions pages linked from the hub).
