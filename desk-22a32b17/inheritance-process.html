Chronological 12-Step Trust Inheritance, Assessor & Tax Execution Checklist

Because Property A passes via trust rather than formal Superior Court probate, title administration happens privately through the successor trustee—but statutory tax and assessor deadlines run strictly from the date of death, not the date a deed is recorded (BOE LTA 2026/026; CA R&TC §480(b); Probate Code §16061.7).

Pre-Transfer (Now – 2040) 1. Audit Trust Instrument & Prior Spousal DSUE Confirm Property A is held in a revocable living trust included in the settlor's gross estate (preserving IRC §1014 step-up and avoiding Rev. Rul. 2023-2 carryover trap). Verify whether any predeceased spouse filed Form 706 for DSUE portability (5-yr late relief under Rev. Proc. 2022-32). Unverified trust clause (Flag F-03); losing §1014 step-up costs $2,280,000+ in 2045 sale tax. Verified — Official Rev. Rul. 2023-2 · IRS Rev. Proc. 2022-32
Days 1–30 Post-Death 2. Death Certificates, Insurance Binder & Trust EIN Obtain certified death certificates; notify Property A's homeowner insurer immediately to maintain vacancy/trustee dwelling coverage during administration; apply online for a Trust Employer Identification Number (EIN, IRS Form SS-4) once the revocable trust becomes irrevocable at death. Uninsured vacancy exclusion if carrier is not notified promptly after settlor's death. Verified — Official CDI Res. Guide · IRS Estate Tax
Within 60 Days of Death 3. Mandatory Statutory Trustee Notice (Probate Code §16061.7) Successor trustee must serve formal statutory Notification by Trustee on all trust beneficiaries and legal heirs within 60 days after death (starting the 120-day trust contest limitation window). Personal trustee liability and open-ended contest window if statutory 60-day notice is omitted. Verified — Official Prob. Code §16061.7 · CA Courts Probate
Within 90–120 Days 4. Certified Date-of-Death Appraisal (Real Estate & Non-Cash Assets) Commission a qualified MAI/certified residential date-of-death appraisal of Property A (and consider IRC §2032 6-month alternate valuation date if market values drop after death and estate is taxable) to substantiate IRC §1014 stepped-up basis. Unsupported §1014 basis upon IRS/FTB Schedule D audit if sold without date-of-death appraisal. Verified — Official IRS §1014 FAQ · IRS Pub 551
Within 150 Days of Death 5. County Assessor Death Notification (CA R&TC §480(b)) Trustee must file Form BOE-502-D (Change in Ownership Statement — Death of Real Property Owner) with the Santa Clara County Assessor within 150 days of date of death—even when title remains inside a trust and no deed has yet recorded! Statutory 10% penalty of taxes applicable to the new base year value under R&TC §482. Verified — Official R&TC §480(b) · SCC Assessor
Months 4–8 (Decision Fork) 6. Execute Strategy Fork: Sell Under §1014 (S3) vs. Move In (S1) vs. Lease (S2) Remember: under BOE LTA 2026/026, date of death (NOT deed distribution date) is the statutory transfer date! Decide well before Month 9 whether to list/sell Property A via the trust/beneficiary or move in as principal residence. Missing the 1-year occupancy window permanently forfeits Proposition 19 family-home relief. Verified — Official BOE LTA 2026/026 · R&TC §63.2
Within 9 Months (+6-Mo Ext.) 7. Federal Form 706 Estate Return & Form 8971 Consistent Basis If gross estate + prior taxable gifts exceed the exclusion ($15.0M in 2026; $15.48M in 2027; ~$24.13M projected in 2045), file Form 706 and pay any 40% estate tax within 9 months (Form 4768 grants automatic 6-month filing extension). Distribute Form 8971 Schedule A to beneficiaries within 30 days after Form 706. Late-filing/late-payment penalties plus IRC §6035 inconsistent-basis penalties. Verified — Official IRS Form 706 · IRS IRC §6035
Within 1 Year (If Occupying A) 8. Move Into Property A, File BOE-266 & Terminate Property B HOX Only if choosing Strategy S1a/S1b: establish physical principal residence in Property A within 1 year of death, file Form BOE-266 ($7,000 Homeowners' Exemption) in Santa Clara County, and notify the San Francisco Assessor in writing (by Dec 10) to terminate the Homeowners' Exemption on Property B. Dual Homeowners' Exemption escape assessment + 25% penalty; loss of Prop 19 eligibility. Verified — Official Form BOE-266 · BOE HOX Rules
At Deed Recording / Sale 9. Affidavit of Death of Trustee, Trust Distribution Deed & R&TC §11930 Exemption Record Affidavit of Death of Trustee + Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (BOE-502-A). If distributing to beneficiary, claim documentary transfer tax exemption '$0 — R&TC §11930 transfer by reason of death'. If selling directly from trust to buyer, county transfer tax is $0.55/$500 (R&TC §11911). Avoidable county/city transfer tax collection if §11930 statutory exemption is not cited on deed. Verified — Official R&TC §11930 · R&TC §11911
April 15 Following Death/Sale 10. Final Form 1040/540, Fiduciary Form 1041/541 & Schedule D Sale Reporting File decedent's final individual returns (Form 1040 & CA Form 540), trust fiduciary income returns (Form 1041 & CA Form 541 for post-death income), and report any sale of Property A on Form 8949 / Schedule D showing §1014 FMV basis less selling expenses (typically a small deductible capital loss!). IRS automated 1099-S gross-proceeds mismatch notice if Form 8949 / Schedule D is omitted. Verified — Official IRS §1014 / Sch D · CA FTB 540/541
Months 1–36 Post-Sale 11. Reinvest Net Proceeds via U.S. Treasury Ladder Into Global Equities (S3b → S3a) Place net sale proceeds (~$6.40M in 2045 baseline) into direct U.S. Treasury bills/notes (interest 100% exempt from CA 13.3% state income tax) and tranche systematically over 24–36 months into low-cost total-market equity index funds. Holding cash in ordinary bank CDs/HYSA unnecessarily forfeits 13.3% CA state tax on interest. Verified — Official IRS Pub 550 · TSP G/C Funds
Within 3 Years (If Occupying A) 12. Final Deadline for Form BOE-19-P Retroactive Prop 19 Relief If occupying Property A under Strategy S1a/S1b, file Form BOE-19-P (Claim for Reassessment Exclusion for Transfer Between Parent and Child Occurring on or After Feb 16, 2021) within 3 years of death (and before transferring to a third party) to receive retroactive relief back to date of death. Filing after 3 years yields prospective relief only—losing 3+ years of supplemental tax refunds! Verified — Official BOE Pub 801 · SCC BOE-19-P
