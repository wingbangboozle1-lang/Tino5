Executive Verdict & Strategic Reasoning

Primary Recommendation — Retain & Occupy Property B Unit 3 + Sell Property A Under IRC §1014 Step-Up + Reinvest via 24–36 Month U.S. Treasury Ladder Into Global Equities (Strategy S3b → S3a) Because Property B is already owned by the beneficiary—delivering $85,800/yr across two leased units, an ultra-low Proposition 13 tax bill ($2,350/yr baseline), and a dedicated 3rd unit for owner occupancy—housing is already solved at near-zero marginal cost. Conversely, Property A arrives via revocable trust at death with a full IRC §1014 date-of-death fair-market-value basis step-up (wiping out decades of embedded appreciation tax-free) while simultaneously losing most of its Proposition 13 shield under Proposition 19 (CA R&TC §63.2). Selling Property A promptly after transfer captures $6,401,480 net of ~5.5% transaction costs in the 2045 central case with $0 federal and $0 California capital-gains tax, outperforming renting Property A by $10,879,960 and outperforming moving into Property A (with B Unit 3 unleased) by $16,912,670 over 25 years.

Four Critical Guardrails Before Executing Any Option 1. Verify Revocable vs. Irrevocable Trust Status Now (IRS Rev. Rul. 2023-2): If Property A was placed into an irrevocable grantor trust excluded from the federal gross estate, IRC §1014 step-up is denied—creating a $2,282,200+ tax trap upon sale (Flag F-03).
2. Never Sell Property B Instead of Property A: Property B receives no basis step-up at the settlor's death and faces San Francisco's tiered transfer tax (0.75%–2.25%) plus ~37.1% combined capital-gains tax.
3. Stage Sale Proceeds via U.S. Treasuries (24–36 Months): Our Black Swan Engine proves that a post-sale 2000/2008 equity bear market (Regime R4) cuts a lump-sum equity investment by ~54%; staging via CA-tax-exempt U.S. Treasuries eliminates sequence-of-returns risk.
4. Audit Liquid Estate Headroom & DSUE Portability: If the settlor's 2026 liquid portfolio exceeds ~$5,740,000 (single, no DSUE portability) at 6%/yr growth, combined house + cash crosses the projected 2045 federal 40% estate-tax threshold (Estate & Cash Tax).
