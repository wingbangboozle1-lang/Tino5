2. Why Renting Property A (S2) Trails Selling (S3a) — And What Changes Under Rev. Rul. 2023-2

The Silicon Valley Price-to-Rent + Prop 19 Compression In 2045, Property A's projected value is $6,774,050 while gross market rent is $119,940/yr (1.77% gross yield). Because non-occupancy forfeits Proposition 19, secured property tax jumps to $81,490/yr—consuming 68% of gross rent before insurance, maintenance, or vacancy! Even with $4,002,850 of 27.5-year building depreciation shielding annual rent from income tax, net pre-tax cash yield is ~0.16%/yr on equity.
The Rev. Rul. 2023-2 Irrevocable Trust Exception (Critical Check!) If Property A was placed in an irrevocable grantor trust excluded from the settlor's federal gross estate, IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-2 denies the IRC §1014 basis step-up. Selling in 2045 with a $250,000 carryover basis triggers $2,282,200 in immediate 37.1% capital-gains tax—slashing S3a's 2070 terminal wealth from $24,996,440 down to $16,084,930 (a $8,911,510 wealth loss!). Always verify revocable trust inclusion before listing Property A (Flag F-03).
