Questions and information gaps

What is known, what is missing, and how to find each missing item. Every row states the answer if there is one, the source if there is one, or the action that would produce one.

QuestionAnswerHow to discover what is missingSource
What is Property A's tax rate area, and what is its published rate?not answeredThe county publishes a compilation of tax rates each year with the rate for every tax rate area; the code is printed on the property tax bill. Read the code off the bill and look up that rate.scc_trb
What is Property A worth at the date of death?not answeredObtain a written valuation as at the date of death. It sets the income tax basis and any estate tax.irs_p551
What would Property A let for?not answeredObtain three written rental estimates. Until then the letting paths rest on an assumed gross yield of 2.0% to 3.2% and are a floor, not a ranking.census_acs hud_fmr
What does insurance cost on each property?not answeredObtain quotes for both. The Department of Insurance publishes a comparison tool under Insurance Code section 12959; its figures are sample premiums, not quotes.cdi
Will the estate exceed the federal filing threshold?The threshold for a 2026 death is $15,000,000Prepare an estate liquidity plan and ask now about portability and prior taxable gifts.irs_estate irs_706
Does California charge an estate tax?not confirmed this sessionConfirm with the Franchise Tax Board before relying on any answer.ftb_540_2025
Is the Proposition 19 exclusion available?Only if Property A continues to be the transferee's principal residenceDecide the occupation question first; the exclusion follows from it, not the other way round. The claim must be filed within one year of the transfer.boe_nr2502 boe_pub801
Is a like-kind exchange wanted?For Property A, almost certainly not — the basis steps up at deathTake advice before selling. Any exchange idea belongs to Property B, where the gain has been building for years.irs_p544
What are Property B's leases and the local rules that bind them?not answeredObtain the two written leases and check them against the local rent rules before relying on the rental income in any path.irs_p527
What is Property B's income-tax basis?not answeredFind the purchase records. Until then Property B's terminal value is left out of the path comparison, because it is the same in every path and cancels out.irs_p551
What are the deadlines after the transfer?The Proposition 19 claim and the Homeowners' Exemption must be filed within one year of the transferSee the timeline on the process page.boe_nr2502 boe_pub801
How often should this be re-run?Every year, and after any input changesRebuild the model and the site and check whether the recommendation moved.fhfa_fred
