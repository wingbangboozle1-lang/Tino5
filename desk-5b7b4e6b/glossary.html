Glossary

TermMeaningSource
Ad valoremCharged in proportion to value. The property tax is an ad valorem tax; a parcel tax is not.boe_prop13
Assessed valueThe value the county taxes. Under Proposition 13 it starts at the purchase price and rises by no more than the lesser of 2% and the change in the California Consumer Price Index.boe_prop13
BasisThe amount a gain or loss is measured against. Inherited property takes a basis equal to its fair market value at the date of death.irs_p551
Depreciation recaptureTax on the depreciation already claimed when a let property is sold. Unrecaptured section 1250 gain is taxed at a maximum of 25%.irs_p527
Factored base year valueThe Proposition 13 assessed value, rolled forward by the annual cap. The Proposition 19 exclusion is measured against it.boe_nr2502
Homeowners' ExemptionA small reduction in taxable value for an owner-occupied home. Claiming it within a year of the transfer is a condition of keeping the Proposition 19 exclusion.boe_nr2502
Imputed rentThe rent a home would command, counted as the benefit of living in it. Every path on this desk counts housing as a benefit in whatever property is occupied, so that living rent-free is not mistaken for a free gain. this desk's convention
Like-kind exchangeSection 1031 lets gain be deferred when real property held for investment or business use is exchanged for other real property.irs_p544
Net investment income taxA 3.8% tax on net investment income above the threshold amounts.irs_niit
PortabilityThe election that lets a surviving spouse use the unused estate tax exemption of the spouse who died first.irs_estate
Proposition 13The 1978 measure that set the 1% maximum levy and the cap on assessed value growth.boe_prop13
Proposition 19The 2020 measure that changed the parent-child transfer of a principal residence or family farm, and ties the exclusion to the transferee's occupation.boe_nr2502
ReassessmentSetting the assessed value to market value, which happens on a change in ownership unless an exclusion applies.boe_prop13
Section 121The exclusion of gain on the sale of a principal residence, up to $250,000 or $500,000, after two years of use out of the last five.irs_p523
Tax rate areaThe code that determines which local agencies and bonds apply to a parcel, and therefore its exact property tax rate. It is printed on the tax bill.scc_trb
Total returnPrice change plus income. The equity return on this desk is the change in the published index plus its dividend-price ratio, which is an approximation.erp2026_b56

Desk built 2026-11-17. Money is rounded to the nearest $10; statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published and marked. Properties are identified by county only — no name, street address, parcel number or postcode appears anywhere on this desk.
