Reinvestment risk, beside the headline

Parking the proceeds for 24 months and phasing in over 12 costs $1,960 per $10,000 invested if the crash never comes — 10.0% less than going straight in over ten years at a 7% market return. That is the price of the cushion, and it is paid in every future in which nothing goes wrong. The other side of the trade is on the black swan page.
