Property tax and assessment

California's Proposition 13 sets the levy at 1% of assessed value plus voter-approved debt, and caps the assessed value's growth at the lesser of 2% and the change in the California Consumer Price Index. Proposition 19 changed what happens when a home passes from parent to child. The two rules pull in opposite directions here, and which one wins depends on whether the heir moves in.

What the bill does if the property is reassessed

If Property A is let rather than occupied, the Proposition 19 exclusion does not apply and the assessed value is reset to market value at the transfer. At the fitted rate that turns a $9,160 bill into roughly $45,670 a year on the same day. The exclusion is worth more than any other single input in this model — and it is conditional on occupation, which is why the brief says to claim it only if the heir will occupy the home long term.

How to find the exact rate for the parcel

The county publishes a compilation of tax rates every year: the 1% maximum levy, the countywide retirement levy and the rate for each tax rate area. The tax rate area code is printed on the property tax bill. This desk does not publish a rate for either parcel because the code has not been supplied — see F01.
