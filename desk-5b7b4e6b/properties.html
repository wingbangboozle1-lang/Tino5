Property facts

Property A is the inheritance. Property B is already owned by the same person, with two of its three units let and the third occupied — so the household can live at Property B without giving up any rent. That asymmetry runs through every number on this desk: living in Property A does not save rent, it frees a unit at Property B.

Property B's fitted fixed charge comes out negative, which is not a real thing. The reason is visible in the household's own bills: B's bill grew about 4.3% a year over 2016-2025 while its assessed value grew about 1.9% a year, so no constant ad-valorem rate plus a constant fixed charge describes it. B is therefore projected from its own observed growth and the split is not published for B. The parcel's own tax rate area has not been confirmed for either property — see flagged item F01.
