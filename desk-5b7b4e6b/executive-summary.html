Read these as: keeping Property A is the right call only if you expect its house prices to beat 16.1% a year, or shares to return less than 4.7% a year, for the next thirty-six years. Both are far from the 1977-2025 averages. The honest conclusion is not "sell" — it is that the decision rests on those two rates, and the household should write down what it believes before the figures arrive.
