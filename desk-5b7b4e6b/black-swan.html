The phased-investing path, replayed against the two great drawdowns

Endpoints are those published by the Federal Reserve and the Economic Report of the President. The 1929 drawdown is the Dow's fall from 381 in September 1929 to 41.22 in the summer of 1932, 89% below the peak, with the pre-crash level not regained until November 1954. The 2008 drawdown is the S&P composite falling from 1,468.36 at the end of 2007 to 903.25 at the end of 2008, recovering along the path the same official table records.

Read this as a mechanism, not as a strategy. These replays know the crash is coming. A mechanical rule that happens to be waiting when the crash starts will always look brilliant in hindsight. The honest counterweight is the cost of the cushion when nothing goes wrong, which is shown immediately below and again beside every headline.
