# Research: inheriting real property held in a trust (step-by-step, verified sources)

1. Obtain certified death certificates; locate the trust instrument.
2. Successor trustee takes over: record **Affidavit of Death of Trustee** + certified death certificate per property; **Certification of Trust** under CA Probate Code §18100.5. Source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PROB&sectionNum=18100.5.
3. Serve **Probate Code §16061.7 notice** on every beneficiary and heir (required even without probate). Source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PROB&sectionNum=16061.7.
4. File **Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (BOE-502-A)** with the county recorder together with the affidavit. Source: CA BOE forms https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/forms.htm
5. Obtain a federal **EIN** for the trust; retitle financial accounts via Certification of Trust.
6. Inventory and appraise all assets at **date-of-death fair market values** — these drive the §1014 step-up. Source: IRS Pub 559 https://www.irs.gov/publications/p559
7. Pay debts and taxes. File **Form 706 within 9 months** of death if gross estate + adjusted taxable gifts > exclusion ($15,000,000 for 2026 deaths), or to elect **DSUE portability** for a surviving spouse. Source: IRS Form 706 instructions https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706
8. **Property tax decision**: occupy the Cupertino house as primary residence within **1 year** + claim homeowners' exemption, and file **BOE-19-P within 3 years** of transfer (or 6 months after a supplemental/escape notice). Otherwise full reassessment to market value. Sources: Santa Clara County Assessor https://sccassessor.org/index.php/tax-savings/transferring-your-assessed-value/parent-to-child-2 and FAQ https://www.sccassessor.org/edocman/Prop19ParentChildFAQ.pdf ; BOE LTA 25-015 https://boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25015.pdf
9. Distribute or sell per trust terms. Sale soon after death ≈ no taxable gain (step-up). If kept as residence 2 of 5 years, §121 exclusion also applies. Sources: IRS IRM 21.8.5 https://www.irs.gov/irm/part21/irm_21-008-005r ; IRS Topic 701 https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc701
10. Final accounting to beneficiaries and termination; retain all filings and appraisals.
