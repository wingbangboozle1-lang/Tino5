## Property tax law
- Prop 13: 1% base + max 2%/yr inflation while ownership unchanged — CA Const. art. XIII A §2; BOE.
- Prop 19 (RTC §63.2): parent→child family-home exclusion requires (a) parent's principal residence,
  (b) child makes it principal residence within 1 yr, (c) HOX/DV exemption filed within 1 yr.
  Value limit = FBYV + indexed amount. Indexed amount 2/16/2025–2/15/2027 = $1,044,586 (BOE LTA 2025/009).
  Excess above limit is added to FBYV. Claim BOE-19-P within 3 yrs or before sale.
  URL: https://boe.ca.gov/prop19/
- Trust: RTC §61(h) — revocable trust becoming irrevocable on death is the change-in-ownership date;
  BOE FAQ #9 confirms date of death = date of change in ownership. Same URL.
- SF secured rate FY2025-26 = 1.18268325% (SF Treasurer & Tax Collector).
- Santa Clara County: 1% base + voter debt; county-wide avg ad valorem ≈1.193%; Cupertino city avg 1.16%
  (SCC Assessor Annual Report 2025-26).
- Homeowners' exemption: $7,000 of assessed value (max ~$70/yr saving) — BOE.

## Inheritance tax
- Federal basic exclusion 2026 = $15,000,000/person (IRS "Estate tax" + "What's new — estate and gift tax").
  Top rate 40%. Form 706 filing threshold 2026 = $15,000,000.
- California: no inheritance tax, no state estate tax (RTC §13302 is a "pick-up" tax tied to a repealed
  federal credit → collects $0).
