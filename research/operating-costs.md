## San Francisco rental rules (SF triplex, ZIP 94122)
- SF Rent Ordinance covers most multi-unit buildings with certificate of occupancy **before June 13, 1979**; owner-occupying one unit of a 3-unit building does NOT exempt the rented units.
- Annual allowable increase = **60% of Bay Area CPI, max 7%**: **1.4%** (3/1/2025–2/28/2026), **1.6%** (3/1/2026–2/28/2027). Source: SF Rent Board https://sfrb.org/topic-no-051-years-annual-allowable-increase
- Just-cause eviction protections apply. AB 1482 statewide cap (5% + CPI, max 10%) **expires Jan 1, 2030**. Source: SF Rent Board https://sfrb.org/article/summary-ab-1482-california-tenant-protection-act-2019
- Prop 33 (Nov 2024) rejected → Costa-Hawkins remains; vacancy decontrol still allowed on turnover.
