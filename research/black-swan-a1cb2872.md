## Decision reading (conditional, not a certified ranking)
- Live in Property A: most exposed to cost/insurance/earthquake paths, least to portfolio sequence; housing benefit is not spendable cash — needs a liquid reserve sized from *verified* actual costs (currently blank/unverified).
- Rent Property A: adds vacancy/tenant/law shock on top of the same cost paths; a single no-rent year plus a major repair is the binding test, not average yield.
- Sell A and reinvest: most exposed to *timing/sequence* (crash-yr1 path roughly halves the yr-50 portfolio vs smooth baseline on identical later returns); diversification — not a single asset — is the SEC-stated mitigant.
- Property B is already owned: held constant in every path; inheriting A does not reset B's basis or assessment (IRS Topic 703 / BOE Prop 19 general rules, as on the Tax page).
- No probability is assigned to any path. CBO's smooth 2% path is a projection convention, and the 1979–81, 2008, and Dec-2022 records show real paths are uneven in level, timing and composition.
