# Research: California property tax law (verified from official sources, Nov 2026)

## Proposition 13 (1978)
- Tax rate: **1% of assessed value + voter-approved bonds/special assessments**. Reassessment to market value only on change in ownership or new construction. Source: CA BOE Pub 29 https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/pub29.pdf and Pub 800-10 https://boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub800-10.pdf
- Assessed (factored base year) value may grow at most **2%/yr** while ownership unchanged. Source: https://boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub800-10.pdf , LTA 25-023 https://boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25023.pdf
- Homeowners' exemption: **$7,000** off assessed value of principal residence (~$70–$80/yr saving). Source: Santa Clara County Assessor Annual Report 2025-26 https://files.santaclaracounty.gov/exjcpb1521/2025-11/annual_report_2025-2026.pdf
- Santa Clara County "typical tax rate area" example: **$1.1488 per $100** of assessed value (1% base + county/school/water bonds). Source: SCC Assessment Information Booklet https://files.santaclaracounty.gov/exjcpb1521/2024-11/assessment_information_booklet.pdf

## Proposition 19 (operative Feb 16, 2021) — intergenerational transfer
- Parent→child exclusion applies ONLY to a **family home** that is the principal residence of the transferor AND continues as the **principal residence of the transferee** (heir must occupy within **1 year** and claim homeowners'/disabled veterans' exemption). Sources: BOE LTA 25-015 https://boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25015.pdf ; SCC Assessor https://sccassessor.org/index.php/tax-savings/transferring-your-assessed-value/parent-to-child-2
- Value test: new taxable value = **lesser of market value or (factored base year value + inflation-adjusted $1,000,000)**. If market value exceeds that sum, the excess is reassessed. If the heir does NOT occupy as primary residence → **full reassessment to market value**. Source: BOE Property Tax Rule 462.520 https://boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/rules/Rule462-520.pdf
- The $1M is adjusted every 2 years (each Feb 16) by the CA House Price Index (FHFA): **$1,044,586 for Feb 16, 2025 – Feb 15, 2027**. Source: BOE LTA 25-015.
- Claim: file **BOE-19-P** within **3 years** of transfer (or within 6 months of a supplemental/escape assessment notice); prospective relief only if late. Source: SCC Assessor Prop 19 FAQ https://www.sccassessor.org/edocman/Prop19ParentChildFAQ.pdf
- Base-year-value portability (55+/severely disabled/wildfire victims) allows transferring a low taxable value to a replacement home anywhere in CA (up to 3 times; others once). Source: SCC Assessor.
