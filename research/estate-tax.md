# Research: inheritance / estate tax thresholds (verified from official sources, Nov 2026)

## Federal (IRS)
- **Basic exclusion $15,000,000** for estates of decedents dying in 2026 (up from $13,990,000 in 2025), made **permanent** by P.L. 119-21 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, July 4, 2025) and **inflation-indexed after 2026**. Sources: IRS IR-2025-103 / OBBB provisions https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/one-big-beautiful-bill-provisions ; Rev. Proc. 2025-32 https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf
- Rate above the exclusion: **40%** (top). Source: Instructions for Form 706 (2026) https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706
- **Portability (DSUE)**: surviving spouse can use the deceased spouse's unused exclusion if Form 706 is filed within 9 months → married couple ≈ **$30,000,000** in 2026. Source: IRS Form 706 instructions https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706
- **GST exemption**: also $15,000,000 (2026). Source: Rev. Proc. 2025-32.
- **Annual gift exclusion 2026: $19,000** per donee. Source: IRS "What's new — Estate and gift tax" https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-selfemployed/whats-new-estate-and-gift-tax
- Projection (assumption 3%/yr indexing): 2040 ≈ $22,688,850; **2045 ≈ $26,302,590**; 2050 ≈ $30,491,910.

## California / local
- **No California estate tax and no inheritance tax.** The CA estate "pick-up" tax equals the federal state tax credit, which was repealed for decedents dying after 2004 → pick-up tax is zero. Source: FTB https://www.ftb.ca.gov/tax-pros/law/legislation/2017-2018/sb726-032317.pdf
- No county/city estate or inheritance tax exists in California.

## Basis and sale of inherited property
- **Step-up in basis (IRC §1014)**: basis of inherited property = fair market value at date of death (applies to trust assets includible in the gross estate). Sources: IRS Pub 559 https://www.irs.gov/publications/p559 ; IRS IRM 21.8.5 https://www.irs.gov/irm/part21/irm_21-008-005r
- **IRC §121**: exclude up to $250,000 ($500,000 joint) gain on sale of principal residence if owned+used 2 of last 5 years. Source: IRS Topic 701 https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc701
- Federal LTCG rates **0/15/20%** + **3.8% NIIT** (IRC §1411; MAGI thresholds $200k single / $250k MFJ). 2026 brackets: 0% up to $98,900 MFJ; 20% above $613,700 MFJ. Sources: IRS Topic 409 https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409 ; Rev. Proc. 2025-32.
- **California taxes capital gains as ordinary income — no special rate; top rate 13.3%** (12.3% + 1% mental health surtax over $1M). Sources: FTB https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/income-types/capital-gains-and-losses.html ; https://www.ftb.ca.gov/about-ftb/data-reports-plans/summary-of-federal-income-tax-changes/index.html
