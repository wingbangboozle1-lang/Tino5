The headline, with reinvestment risk beside it

Headline (base case, model output): at the 2040 transfer the choice between keeping and selling Property A rests on the rent it can earn versus the 7.0% reinvestment return (assumption) - the model's break-even gross rent is about $21,900/month. Reinvestment risk beside the headline: if the heir sells and phases in over 36 months on a flat market, the cushion costs about $2,100 per $10,000 over ten years versus investing at once (at the 7.0% assumption); if a 2008-style crash arrives instead, the same phasing leaves about $14,580 per $10,000 versus $12,590 invested at the peak (price basis, official drawdown). Details: Black swan & phased investing.

No single number decides this. The desk separates what is verified from what is assumed, prices the transfer taxes and the new parcel tax into the sell path, and shows every break-even. See Methodology for the rules, Flagged for review for open items.
