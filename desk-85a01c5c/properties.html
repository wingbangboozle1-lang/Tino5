Property facts

Two properties, two different tax positions. Property A is inherited (step-up in basis; Prop 19 exclusion available only if occupied). Property B is already owned with long-held Prop 13 protection (very low assessed value) and rental income; it is not inherited, so nothing about its ownership changes at the transfer. The desks in this repository that treat both as one portfolio are flagged; here they are analysed separately and only combined for estate-size questions.
