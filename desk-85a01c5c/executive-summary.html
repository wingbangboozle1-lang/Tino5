Executive summary

Break-evens instead of a single forecast; estate headroom; the decision log.

The decision, stated as break-evens

The desk compares live in Property A, rent it, and sell and reinvest - plus mixed paths (live-then-sell, rent-then-sell, sell-then-phase) - at the same transfer dates (2040, 2045, 2050) and the same horizon end (2076), with the transfer taxes, recording fees, occupancy effects and liquidity differences priced in. Because the market rent of Property A is unknown (flagged), the rent path is expressed as the break-even rent at which renting matches selling, rather than as a forecast.

The cash portion of this estate is the deciding unknown: it is not on this desk and is requested on Questions.

What this summary does not do

  • It does not assert a market rent, a confirmed insurance premium, a confirmed water provider, or a square footage for Property B - each is a blank row or an owner-supplied band on the relevant page.
  • It does not model the income-tax return of the whole estate; the 1% California tax above $1,000,000 of income is noted on Inheritance & estate tax and is a CPA question.
  • It does not claim the phased-investing path is superior: the black-swan page shows it winning in both official crash replays and losing about $2,100 per $10,000 over ten years on a flat market at the base-case return. That trade-off is the owner's call.
