Step-up in basis (IRC sec. 1014)An inherited asset's tax basis resets to its fair market value at the date of death; selling soon after usually realises little taxable gain.verified IRS - Topic No. 403, Basis of Assets (inherited property: fair market value at date of death, IRC sec. 1014)
Sec. 121 exclusionUp to $250,000 ($500,000 joint) of gain excluded when selling a principal residence owned and used 2 of the last 5 years.exact (statutory) IRS - Topic No. 703, Sale of Home (sec. 121 exclusion $250,000 / $500,000 married filing jointly; ownership and use 2 of the last 5 years)
