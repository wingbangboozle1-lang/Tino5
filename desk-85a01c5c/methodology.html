Methodology & privacy

Rounding and figure rules

  • Money is rounded to the nearest $10 unless it is a statutory or agency-published amount, which is quoted exactly as published and tagged exact (statutory).
  • Every table row carries an official source link, or is blank and tagged unverified, or is a disclosed assumption / owner-supplied input. No value is inferred into a blank.
  • Model outputs are derived values: their method and every input are shown on the pages that use them.

Crash-replay method

  • Drawdown paths are monthly interpolations between official anchors (Federal Reserve History endpoints; the 1929 path also uses the official mid-November 1929 point). The 2008 recovery follows the official ERP end-of-year index levels for 2009-2013, then holds flat.
  • Price indexes only: dividends are excluded (understating the all-in strategy's recovery), and every table that shows replay numbers says so.
  • Parking yields: official FRED annual average bill rates for the 2008 replay; a labelled 2.0%/yr assumption for the 1929 replay; today's published Treasury curve for the "what the park earns now" line.
  • Series values (S&P annual levels and dividend ratios; bill rates) are transcriptions of official publications whose provenance was established and audited in this repository (see the swan data README on main); the official URLs are on Sources.

Privacy

  • No names, street addresses, ZIP codes, city names, parcel or account numbers appear anywhere on this desk. The two properties are described at county level only.
  • Exact per-year assessed values and tax bills are withheld (banded anchors used instead) because assessed values are public records that can be matched to a parcel.
  • No personal information appears in the final published product; the owner's exact figures remain in the owner's private records.
