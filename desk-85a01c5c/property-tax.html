The exact rate-area total is deliberately blank. The parcel's tax rate area code is printed on its property tax bill; publishing the exact total rate with the banded assessed value would narrow a public-record search. What is published: the countywide components above (verified from the book) and the model band 1.04% - 1.25% (assumption, covering the reviewed neighbouring tax-rate-area totals). The discovery action is on Questions: read the rate area and the itemised rate off the owner's own bill - no parcel identity is needed on the site.

Owner-implied reconciliation: the owner's 2026 bill implies roughly 1.3% including flat parcel charges and possible supplemental amounts; the ad-valorem band above is lower, and the difference is the fixed charges. This reconciliation is owner-supplied and unverified.
