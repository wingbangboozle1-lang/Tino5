The headline

California has no inheritance tax. The federal estate tax will very likely never apply to this family: the exemption is $15,000,000 per person for 2026 deaths and is indexed from 2027. The tax that actually bites on inheritance is the property tax reassessment under Proposition 19 — and it bites hardest on exactly the options that do not involve living in the house.

The second-largest tax item is the opposite: the step-up in basis on death erases roughly $2,549,460 of built-in gain on Property A. That benefit is only worth having if the trust is drafted and funded so the house is inside the parents' estate (flag F-14).

Why the ranking is closer than it looks

Selling wins on these assumptions because it converts an asset that grows at 4% into a portfolio assumed to grow at 6%, and because it avoids a 37.1% federal-plus-California capital-gains bill at the end. The result flips if Property A appreciates faster than — a year. Over the last ten years the San Jose metro index rose about 5.0% a year, so this is genuinely a close call — which is why the options page shows three cases rather than one answer.

How to read this site

Status labels

verified secondary owner-provided derived assumption unverified
  • verified — read from an official source, linked in the row.
  • secondary — credible non-official reporting, or an official value whose page could not be retrieved here.
  • owner-provided — supplied by the owner on 10 Nov 2026; not independently confirmed.
  • derived — arithmetic on the above, with the arithmetic shown.
  • assumption — a named model parameter (A1–A29) with a sensitivity.
  • unverified — not confirmable; the value is left blank and listed on Flagged for review.

What this is not

This is a decision-support model, not legal, tax or investment advice. It contains no names, addresses, parcel numbers or account numbers — the two properties are called A and B throughout, and every owner-supplied figure is clearly marked. Ownership, debt, trust drafting, condition and insurance are unknown (see the flagged list); each of those can move the answer.

Statutory values are quoted exactly as published. Anything computed or projected is rounded to the nearest $10.
