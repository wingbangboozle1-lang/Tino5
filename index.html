Highest-priority decision lever

Protect the Prop 19 deadline if eligible

For a qualifying parent-to-child family-home transfer, the current rule requires the heir to occupy the home as a primary residence within one year and file the homeowners’ or disabled-veterans’ exemption within one year to obtain relief from the transfer date. The BOE-19-P claim generally has a three-year deadline (and must be filed before transferring to a third party). Verify eligibility and deadlines with the county assessor and a California estate/property-tax professional at the actual transfer date.
