Make the inheritance decision with fewer unknowns.

A source-linked guide to the current California and federal rules, the costs to verify, and the steps that matter before deciding whether to live in, rent, or sell an inherited home.

✓Privacy is built in

Names, street addresses, ZIP codes, exact asset values, rent, and household utility details are intentionally excluded from this public site. Homes are referred to only as Property A and Property B.

!
Bottom line: no defensible “best” choice can be calculated yet.

Property-by-property carrying costs, debt, basis, title/trust terms, lease details, insurance quotes, occupancy eligibility, and the future death-year tax rules are not confirmed. The page leaves those amounts blank rather than inventing them. The strongest present action is to preserve the choice, verify the records, and compare after-tax net proceeds with a fully costed hold scenario.

→
Provisional direction: protect the option value; do not make an irreversible choice on gross numbers.

At inheritance, immediately test whether the intended heir genuinely wants and can establish Property A as their primary home within the Prop 19 deadline; model the county’s partial/full reassessment result first. Keep Property B’s existing rental option only if a verified, reserve-adjusted after-tax cash-flow comparison beats selling and reinvesting; do not count gross rent as profit. Do not transfer or sell either property before counsel and a tax professional review the trust, title, basis, county claims, and sale tax. This is a conditional process recommendation—not an unsupported claim that one path maximizes return.

THE TWO PROPERTIES

Different roles, different tax questions

See all decision criteria →
PROPERTY A

Family home · Santa Clara County

Questions: Can the heir make this their principal residence within a year? Will the assessed-value exclusion apply, and what will full reassessment mean if it does not?

  • Energy credits and water costs need utility-bill verification.
  • Primary-residence insurance must be quoted for the actual dwelling and rebuild coverage.
  • Current assessed-value and tax history were not independently matched to an official parcel record.
Cost model & projection status →
PROPERTY B

Multi-unit rental · San Francisco County

Questions: Keep the current rented units, occupy the vacant unit, or sell? A reported rent stream is not net income until operating costs, taxes, vacancy, and capital work are deducted.

  • Rental use can change property-tax exclusion eligibility.
  • Insurance must match multi-unit, rental, and owner-occupancy exposure.
  • Rent, unit count, and utility details have been withheld from this public page for privacy.
