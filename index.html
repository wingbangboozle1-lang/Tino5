EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What matters most

Verified framework
  1. 01
    Do not assume an inherited home keeps its old property-tax basis.

    California generally reassesses on a change in ownership. A narrow Prop 19 family-home exclusion may apply only if its eligibility, value-limit, occupancy, and filing requirements are met. Rental/investment property is not a qualifying family home under that rule. See rule details →

  2. 02
    Trust transfer does not itself settle tax or property-tax questions.

    Trust administration, estate-tax inclusion, basis reporting, and county reassessment are separate issues. A revocable trust can avoid probate administration for trust assets, but should not be assumed to remove those assets from the federal taxable estate. See the sequence →

  3. 03
    Compare after-tax net outcomes—not headline value or gross rent.

    For each hold, rent, occupy, or sell path, include reassessed property tax, insurance, utilities, vacancy, maintenance, capital work, income tax, selling costs, and the after-tax return on reinvested sale proceeds. Exact inputs are unverified and therefore omitted from the public site. See the comparison framework →

  4. 04
    2040–2050 thresholds and market values cannot be verified today.

    Model future outcomes as explicit scenarios only after the transfer date, property facts, and contemporaneous laws are known. This site leaves future dollar values blank rather than presenting a fabricated forecast. See the model limits →
