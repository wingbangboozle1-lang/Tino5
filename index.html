What would change the answer?

These questions come before choosing a path; several are unresolved and can move both the tax outcome and the keep-versus-sell ranking.

  • Will the heir genuinely use Property A as a long-term primary residence, and will the transfer meet the then-current Proposition 19 requirements?
  • What are the date-of-death value, tax basis, debts, sale costs, local tax-rate area and actual property operating costs?
  • What rent is supportable for Property A, and what are Property B’s actual net rent, lease terms, unit legality and occupancy trade-offs?
  • What is the whole taxable estate after prior taxable gifts and any portability election, not just the value of one home?
  • What investment policy, time horizon and loss tolerance will govern proceeds, including the possibility of a market decline during Treasury parking?
!

The recommendation is conditional, not final

Independent valuations, rent opinions, written insurance quotes, inspection-based capital budgets, the trust instrument, tax basis, debt and estate-wide information are not yet verified. Use the dashboard to see what is known, what is assumed, what is blank, and exactly how to close each gap. Do not treat a point projection as a 50-year forecast.

Privacy boundary and remaining exposure

The live Pages product uses Property A / Property B labels and is checked for known name, street, postal, parcel, email, phone, city and neighbourhood patterns; broad county-level jurisdiction references remain where needed for legal context. It does not publish a city, neighbourhood, postal code, street, parcel or account identifier. The root and main desk ask search engines not to index their pages, and robots.txt asks crawlers not to fetch the site; these are crawler requests, not access controls. The GitHub repository is public. Earlier commits and excluded working trees still contain previously exposed household/location material: excluding files from Pages does not erase Git history or remove those files from GitHub. A shared-history rewrite was not performed in this branch, so this repository must not be described as scrubbed. The public-repository history issue remains prominently flagged for a repository-wide, coordinated remediation.
