Attorney & CPA Briefing Sheet — anonymized planning brief. Print this page from the browser; no private identifiers or property address is required.

Decision frame. Property A is expected to transfer to the inheritor through a trust around 2040–2050. Property B is already owned by the same inheritor and has existing rental units. Analyze Property A as a new inheritance while treating Property B as a continuing ownership, housing and rental-income baseline; do not conflate the two transfers.

Questions for counsel and tax adviser before choosing

  • Attorney: review the trust instrument, title, trustee powers, beneficiaries, transfer date, debts/liens, residence eligibility and the exact one-year/three-year claim rules under California law.
  • CPA / estate tax: inventory the entire gross estate, lifetime taxable gifts, prior gift/estate returns, marital deductions/portability, asset ownership and basis; determine whether an estate return, income-tax return or portability filing is required.
  • Property operations: obtain written insurance quotes for an owner-occupied inherited home and a multi-unit rental; confirm lease terms, lawful unit status, actual rents, property tax components, water/power, repairs and reserve needs.
  • Investment decision: compare verified net sale proceeds with hold/rent economics after tax, then test an optional 24–36-month Treasury ladder and staged equity purchases using several return, crash and future-yield assumptions.

Time-sensitive items (relative to actual legal triggers)

60 days: trustee notice duties may apply after a revocable trust becomes irrevocable. 9 months from death: Form 706 filing and estate/GST tax are generally due if a return is required; an extension to file does not automatically extend payment. 1 year from transfer: qualifying occupancy and homeowners exemption deadline relevant to the Prop 19 exclusion. 3 years from transfer (or earlier triggering event): outer Prop 19 claim period. Have the attorney calendar exact dates from the governing documents and official rules.

Official references: California Probate Code §16061.7; IRS Form 706 Instructions; California Revenue & Taxation Code §63.2; California BOE Proposition 19 guidance.

What is still unknown and must be obtained

Executed trust and deed; exact date and beneficiaries; whole-estate inventory and gift history; asset-by-asset basis records; mortgages and liens; county tax bills and rate-area charges; current unit leases and lawful status; independent rent appraisals; insurance bindable quotes; inspection and maintenance history; solar interconnection/credit terms; sale-cost estimates; and investment policy, liquidity needs and tax assumptions. Unknown values are intentionally blank on the decision desk until documented.

Decision to make only after the facts

Compare (A) occupy/claim if eligible, (B) rent/retain, (C) sell and reinvest, and (D) mixed paths such as retaining Property B while parking Property A sale proceeds in short Treasuries before a staged stock-market entry. No path is recommended as certain. Use the questions register, mixed-path comparison, stress tool, and flagged items as the working papers.
