Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

Suggestions only. Nothing on this page has been implemented. Each row is a proposal for you to accept, modify or reject before anything changes. The status column is empty of any decision because none has been taken.

Review before implementing. These are proposals produced by the analysis, not instructions. Several of them — moving in, moving the property into a company, letting the empty unit — have consequences that depend on facts this desk does not have. Read the reason, then decide.
