  • Method and privacy
Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

Year by year to 2076: market value, assessed value and tax for both properties, with the reassessment at the transfer year.

The transfer is modelled in 2045, the midpoint of the 2040–2050 window. Assessed values before the transfer trend at 2% a year under Proposition 13. At the transfer the assessed value is reset under Proposition 19 with the exclusion claimed, then trends at 2% again. Market value grows at the 4.0% assumption throughout.
