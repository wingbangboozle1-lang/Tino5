Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

What is in this repository, what is published, and the irregularities found while reading it. Written so that you can check any of it yourself.

R03An earlier desk overstated the Proposition 19 savingOne earlier desk treated the exclusion as protecting the whole gap between assessed and market value. It protects only the allowance: once market value exceeds the cap, the new taxable value is the market value less the allowance, and everything above the cap is taxed. That desk is excluded from publication.Confirm the correction against the guidance, then decide whether to reinstate or retire that desk.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
R04An estate tax closing letter fee was reported inconsistentlyAn earlier page showed a fee that did not match the published figure.
