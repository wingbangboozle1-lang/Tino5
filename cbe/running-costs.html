Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

The rent cap, exactly as the statute states it

This matters because Property B already has two of three units let, and because the rental income it produces is one of the benefits of moving into Property A. The rule is statewide; a local ordinance can be stricter and none was retrieved.

Does that duplex exemption cover Property B?It is drafted for two units. Property B has three, so on the face of the statute the exemption does not fit — but whether it does turns on how the units are configured and on the tenancy dates, neither of which this desk knows. It is flagged rather than assumed.unverifiedCalifornia Legislative Information

The cap is a ceiling on increases, not a cap on rent. A tenancy that starts at market rent is unaffected until the first increase. This desk models rent as tracking general inflation, which is a national index; the statute uses a regional one, and that difference is flagged.
