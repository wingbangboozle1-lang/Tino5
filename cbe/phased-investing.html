Privacy. Current pages omit names, street addresses, parcel/account identifiers, postcodes and city names; the properties are called Property A and Property B. Owner-specific money figures on current pages are rounded to the nearest $10, but rounding is not a guarantee of anonymity and does not remove earlier versions from Git history. Statutory amounts may be quoted exactly as published. See Flagged for review before treating the repository as fully scrubbed.

A phased sale-proceeds path is available inside the Black swans & phased investing page: park proceeds in a rolling 3-month-to-2-year Treasury ladder for a user-selected 24–36 months, then phase purchases into equities.

What the model does and does not claim. The calculator requires user-entered proceeds, Treasury yields, equity return and hypothetical crash magnitude/timing. It does not prefill unknown rates or treat recession dates as stock-market losses. The Treasury curve link describes current market observations, not a future inheritance-year yield.

Risks to compare

  • Reinvestment risk: each maturity may roll into a different yield.
  • Opportunity cost: equities may rise during the parking period.
  • Sequence risk: a fall before, during or after phasing changes which purchases experience it.
  • Tax and inflation drag: not included in the local calculator; model with an adviser separately.
