  • Method and privacy
Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

Fifty years is long enough that growth and inflation will not be constant. This page replays the real inflation record instead of assuming a smooth line, and shows what each historical environment does to the decision.

What is real here and what is not. The inflation record is the official Consumer Price Index series published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics, transcribed month by month from the series page and linked on every row. The real house price growth and real equity return attached to each historical episode are assumptions: no official long-run series for either was retrieved into this desk. The sampling therefore gives you a realistic shape for the inflation path — persistence, shocks, long stretches of calm — and not a forecast.

A positive gap means keeping wins. The inflation column is measured from the official record; the two growth columns are nominal rates built as inflation plus the assumed real rate for that environment.

The spread is the finding. Across measurable environments the occupy-versus-sell gap runs from −$25,283,660 to $34,702,440 — a range of $59,986,100. That is far wider than the difference between any two of the three options under the baseline. Which decade the transfer happens to fall in matters more than which option you pick, and nobody can choose the decade.

Five mechanisms that only show up in an uneven world

MechanismWhy it only matters when the path is unevenStatusSource
Proposition 8 cuts the bill when prices fallIf market value drops below the trended base year value the county assessor enrolls the lower value, so the tax bill falls with the market. It then rises with the market again until it meets the trend. A house carried through a slump is cheaper to hold than a smooth-line model suggests, which is exactly the cushion that mattered in the deflation row above.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
The Proposition 19 allowance moves with house prices, not with inflationThe allowance is indexed to a California house price index, which rose 2.15% in 2024. In a housing boom the allowance grows quickly, so the protected slice grows quickly too. In a housing slump the allowance can barely move while the rest of the taxable value keeps climbing.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
Federal Housing Finance Agency
The statewide rent cap binds only above 5% inflationThe cap is 5% plus the change in the cost of living, or 10%, whichever is lower. Below 5% inflation it does not bind at all. In a high-inflation stretch it caps rent increases below inflation while insurance, maintenance and water all rise with inflation, so the real return on a letting compresses precisely when the overall price level is moving fastest.verifiedCalifornia Legislative Information
A rental stream is nominal, and so is the tax on itRent is taxed as it is received. In a high-inflation stretch with flat real rents, the nominal rent that clears the thresholds grows and the tax on it grows with it, while the real value of the depreciation shield stays fixed in nominal terms. The shield is worth less every year and nothing in the tax code restores it.verifiedInternal Revenue Service
Internal Revenue Service
An asset held to death is never taxed on its gainAll three options here end with an unsold asset, so no option is charged a terminal capital gains tax that another avoids. If the asset is still held at death its basis is stepped up again. That is why the sell-and-reinvest comparison uses an after-tax portfolio return rather than a gross one, and it is why a high-inflation environment hurts selling twice: it erodes the real return and it leaves a nominal gain that would be taxed on the way out.verifiedInternal Revenue Service

Block resampling: assembling the future out of real five-year stretches

Independent draws from the historical distribution would jump from 14% to −10% and back with no memory. Inflation does not behave like that: it persists. The simulation therefore draws contiguous five-year blocks of real history and chains them, which preserves the way a bad stretch runs for years before it turns. Each block also carries one growth regime with it, so a high-inflation block is also a block in which real returns were poor — the correlation is the point.

1,200 resampled paths per comparison, fixed seed so the numbers reproduce. This is a dispersion study, not a probability forecast: the environments are drawn with equal weight, which is a choice and not an estimate of how likely each one is.

The honest read. Across resampled histories the median favours selling by $327,960, but keeping wins in about 36% of paths and the middle half of outcomes runs from −$1,140,440 to $368,400. A decision this close to the line should not be made on a projection at all. It should be made on the break-even rent, which is observable once you have a letting appraisal, and on whether you actually want to live in the house, which is not a financial question.
