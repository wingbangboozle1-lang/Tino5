Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

Two properties, one decision. Property A is expected to pass through a family trust some time between 2040 and 2050. Property B is already owned, lived in, and has two of its three units let. This desk works out what each way of holding Property A is worth, where the numbers come from, and exactly which missing facts would change the answer.

A row marked unverified has a blank value cell. Nothing on this site fills a gap with an invented number: where a figure is needed but unknown, the model reports the break-even value of that figure instead, so you can compare it against a real quote.
