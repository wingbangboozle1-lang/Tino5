What would change the answer — start here.
  • Whether the inheritor will genuinely occupy Property A within the statutory period: this changes Prop 19 eligibility and materially changes the tax model. Confirm the trust terms and use plan with a California estate/property-tax attorney.
  • Net sale proceeds versus the after-tax return on a diversified portfolio: replace the assumed price growth, portfolio return, sale costs, and taxes with written adviser inputs.
  • Actual rent, insurance, repairs, debt, and Property B unit/legal status: obtain unit-specific quotes and a rental/inspection review; Property B is already owned and remains part of the household comparison.
  • Total taxable estate, ownership, lifetime gifts, and prior spouse portability: have the CPA/estate attorney inventory the whole estate; the home alone does not determine federal estate-tax exposure.

A row marked unverified has a blank value cell. Nothing on this site fills a gap with an invented number: where a figure is needed but unknown, the model reports the break-even value of that figure instead, so you can compare it against a real quote.
