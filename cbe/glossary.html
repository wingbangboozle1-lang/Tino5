Privacy. Current pages omit names, street addresses, parcel/account identifiers, postcodes and city names; the properties are called Property A and Property B. Owner-specific money figures on current pages are rounded to the nearest $10, but rounding is not a guarantee of anonymity and does not remove earlier versions from Git history. Statutory amounts may be quoted exactly as published. See Flagged for review before treating the repository as fully scrubbed.

Mixed pathA strategy combining property occupancy/rental choices with liquidity reserves, Treasury holdings and/or staged market investment; feasibility and tax outcomes depend on facts.scenario labelNo official source — a planning category, not a statutory term
Important distinction: recession dates, inflation history, and stock-market returns are different series. The black-swan tool uses NBER chronology only to identify economic episodes; shock magnitude and timing are explicit user assumptions, not facts inferred from those dates.
