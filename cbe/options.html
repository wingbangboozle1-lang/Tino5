Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

Three ways to hold Property A, compared on the same footing. All three end with an asset that has not been sold at the 2076 horizon, so none is penalised for a terminal tax the others avoid.

What "economic" means here. The cash column counts money only. The economic column adds the market rent of the dwelling the household actually lives in — because living in your own home is untaxed and being paid rent is not, and that asymmetry is the largest single tax effect in this whole decision. The two units of Property B that are already let are common to all three options and are left out of all of them.
