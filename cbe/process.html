Privacy. No name, street address, parcel identifier, account number, postcode or city appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are called Property A and Property B. Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10 so that a published number cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nobody.

What actually happens when a property held in a family trust passes, in the order it happens, with the deadline for each step and the document that imposes it.

Two dates decide the tax outcome. You must occupy the home within one year of the transfer and file the homeowner's exemption within one year. Miss either one and the Proposition 19 exclusion is lost, and the assessed value resets to full market value — on the projections used here that is the difference between a $67,320 bill and a $87,250 bill in the first full year, or $19,930 a year, and it then compounds for as long as you hold the property.

In the first sixty days

#StepWhy it matters, and the deadlineStatusSource
5The trustee serves the statutory notificationWithin 60 days of the death the trustee must serve a notification on every beneficiary of the trust and every heir of the deceased settlor, in the manner provided by Probate Code section 1215, to the last known address.verifiedCalifornia Legislative Information
6Obtain a date-of-death valuationThe value at the date of death sets the income tax basis of what is inherited, and it sets the new assessed value if the exclusion turns out not to apply. A valuation obtained later is harder to defend than one obtained at the time.verifiedInternal Revenue Service

Property tax filings

#StepWhy it matters, and the deadlineStatusSource
7Notify the county assessor of the change in ownershipA change in ownership statement is required, and the assessor then decides whether to reassess and to what value.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
8Move in within one year of the transferThe law requires the transferee to occupy the family home within one year of the transfer for the exclusion to apply as of the date of transfer. This is the single most valuable date on this desk.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
9File the homeowner's or disabled veterans' exemption within one yearFiled on the form the Board prescribes with the county assessor. The exclusion is not available without it, and if it is filed after the year has passed the exclusion cannot be applied retrospectively once the property has been sold.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
California State Board of Equalization
10File the exclusion claim within three yearsOr before the property is transferred to a third party, whichever is earlier.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
11Expect a supplemental or escape assessmentAfter a change in ownership the assessor may issue a supplemental bill for the part of the year after the transfer, or an escape assessment if the value is determined late. Budget for a one-off bill in the first year.verifiedOffice of the Treasurer and Tax Collector

Federal filings

#StepWhy it matters, and the deadlineStatusSource
12Work out whether an estate tax return is requiredA return is required if the gross estate, increased by adjusted taxable gifts and with the specific gifts listed in the statute, exceeds the threshold for the year of death: $15,000,000 for 2026 and $15,480,000 for 2027. California imposes no estate tax at all, so this is the only death tax to consider.verifiedInternal Revenue Service
Internal Revenue Service
California Legislative Information
13File within nine months, or extendThe return is due nine months after the date of death. An automatic six-month extension of time to file is available on Form 4768 filed by the due date.verifiedInternal Revenue Service
14File even if no tax is due, if portability is wantedA return must be filed to elect portability of a deceased spouse's unused exclusion, regardless of the size of the estate. Where no filing requirement existed, a simplified late election is available under the revenue procedure the Service issued for that purpose.verifiedInternal Revenue Service

Afterwards

#StepWhy it matters, and the deadlineStatusSource
15Tell the insurerA change of ownership is a material change. An owner-occupier policy will not cover a let property, and a landlord policy will not cover an owner-occupier's contents. Sort this before the transfer completes, not after a claim.verifiedCalifornia Department of Insurance
16Transfer the utility accounts and check the rooftop solar tariffThe export tariff was enrolled by the previous account holder and may need to be re-enrolled under yours. This desk does not know which utility serves Property A, or what tariff the system is on.actionNo official source — the utility that serves Property A is not named on this site and no tariff was retrieved, so no official document can be linked
17Diarise the two annual tax datesSecured property tax installments are delinquent after 10 December and 10 April, and the penalty is 10% of the installment. Two dates a year, every year.verifiedOffice of the Treasurer and Tax Collector
18Keep the assessor's notices and the filed claimsIf the exclusion is ever challenged, the notices and the filed claims are the evidence. Keep them with the trust documents.verifiedCalifornia State Board of Equalization
