5 · How to repeat this review manually

  • Open the live landing: this site's root page — every published desk is one click away.
  • Run the three gates locally: python3 scripts/privacy_scan.py · python3 scripts/verify_safe.py · python3 desk-e86/verify.py (and python3 referee-abae/_src/crosscheck.py for the cross-tree view).
  • Spot-check any "verified" badge by opening its source link in the row — all were read on 13 November 2026; re-read them if the page has been updated since.
  • Compare claims across desks with the referee's log: discrepancies · brief-to-page traceability.
  • For anything unresolved, the single intake list is the flagged page; the single decision list is the suggestions page.
