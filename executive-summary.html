3. The single most important number: Prop 19 partial reassessment (Cupertino)

California Prop 19 (parent→child, primary residence only) caps the exclusion at factored base year value + $1,044,590 for transfers Feb 16, 2025–Feb 15, 2027. The child must move in as primary residence within 1 year and file for the homeowners’ exemption within 1 year; BOE-19-P within 3 years. Rental use forfeits the exclusion entirely.
