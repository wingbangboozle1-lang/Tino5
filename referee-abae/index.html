Bottom line. The desk's arithmetic follows from its own rules, and nearly all its statutory figures match official sources (two small errors, X04 and X05). Its ranking Sell > Rent > Occupy is robust to tax-rate choices, insurance, rent ±20%, a 27% price fall at transfer, and the terminal-tax convention. It is not robust to the return on the proceeds: reinvested in Treasuries only, selling loses to renting in every historical window, and if home prices grow about 6.1% a year renting ties selling. The ranking is therefore a bet on owning equities for 25 years, with inputs (rent, tax-rate area, transfer date) still unverified. The two published desks differ in level mostly because they assume different after-tax returns; in the historical record (approximation) no 25-year window earned less than 4.5% after tax and 5 of 25 earned less than 5.6%. Nothing here is a recommendation.

Not done by this referee

  • No property-specific input is verified here: rent, tax-rate area, insurance, repair costs and values are the desks' or the owner's.
  • No legal, tax or investment advice, and no forecast. Ratios describe a model.
  • No check of other sessions' pages beyond those listed; the cross-check script is ready to scan them once merged.

Scope. This page contains no names, addresses, ZIP codes, parcel numbers, household bills, rents, values or account details. It shows official figures, rates, ratios, counts and pass/fail results. Property A is the future trust inheritance; Property B is already owned. Not advice: rules and thresholds may change before a transfer, and every figure here is only as good as the source linked in its row.
