The index has fallen for years at a time (1989–1994 and 2006–2012). Over the whole record, from the fourth quarter of 1976 to that of 2025, it grew 6.7% a year.

Rolling 25-year windows

25 windows start between 1976 and 2000. Home-price growth ranged 4.1% to 9.1% (median 5.9%); consumer prices 2.1% to 4.6% (median 2.7%); the S&P approximation 7.4% to 13.5% (median 9.6%). The desk assumes 4.0% home growth, below every historical window, and a 4.5% after-tax return, inside the 3.3% to 7.7% range of returns that would make Sell tie Rent across the windows. Windows overlap heavily, so they are not 25 independent trials: fifty years of data hold about two separate 25-year periods. Each window is run as a transfer in 2026 followed by 25 years at that window's growth, inflation and returns; the desk's own comparison uses 2045, and the break-even return differs by about 0.15 points between the two.
