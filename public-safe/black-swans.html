Black swans & uneven regimes

When growth and inflation stop being well behaved

A 50-year plan built on a single growth rate is a plan built on fiction. This page keeps the same arithmetic but changes the regime: crashes, inflation, insurance shocks, rent caps and a portfolio that disappoints.

What the evidence says about smoothness. The most recent official CPI release shows monthly all-items changes of +0.6% in September 2026 and +0.4% in October 2026, energy +5.2% then +0.7%, gasoline up 37.4% over the periods BLS could compare, and electricity +0.9% in one month (BLS · Consumer Price Index). BLS also could not publish a 12-month change for October 2026 at all, because October 2025 data were not collected during a lapse in appropriations. That is the texture of real data: gaps, spikes and revisions. A flat 2.5% line is a modelling convenience, not the world.

The five shocks that matter most

1. A crash at the wrong moment

Sequence, not average, decides the outcome. A 30% fall at the transfer date cuts every path, and at the horizon the sale path ends $5,535,430 lower than in the baseline ($12,916,010 versus $18,451,440). Property tax does not fall with the market unless the assessed value is above market and the county enrolls a Prop 8 decline-in-value assessment (BOE · How property is assessed (Pub 800-10) · SCC Assessor · Decline in value).

2. Insurance withdrawal

The state's insurer of last resort already carries 677,449 homeowner policies, and 662 ZIP codes are designated distressed (CDI · Sustainable Insurance Strategy). Tripling the premium reduces the rental path by about $577,100 and the occupancy path by $577,100. A non-renewal with no replacement is worse: it is an uninsurable asset, not an expensive one.

3. Uninsurable physical loss

A fire or earthquake loss equal to 20% of value is $1,306,250 at the transfer date. The owner of the house absorbs it; the seller of the house does not. Earthquake cover is separate, and FAIR Plan cover is narrow (CDI · Residential insurance).

4. Rent regulation and tenants

Statewide caps under Civil Code 1947.12 allow 5% plus CPI, capped at 10%, and the section is repealed on 1 January 2030 unless extended; local ordinances layer on top (CA Civil Code 1947.12 · SF.gov · Allowable rent increase). A permanent 1.5% rent-growth regime costs the rental path about $2,269,070. Tenant buyouts, eviction limits and habitability claims can cost more than the cap itself.

5. Tax and legal surprises

  • No step-up. If the trust is drafted or funded so that A is not included in the transferor's gross estate, the basis may carry over. On a $6,531,230 sale with a $250,000 basis the capital-gains tax at 37.1% would be about $2,330,340. This is the single most expensive possible surprise (IRS · Publication 551).
  • Prop 19 tolerance. The allowance ($1,044,586 for transfers through 15 February 2027) is adjusted every other year and can be changed by the Legislature or the voters. A smaller allowance raises the occupied-path tax (BOE · Proposition 19 · BOE · Prop 19 fact sheet (Pub 801)).
  • A wealth or inheritance tax. A 2026 statewide measure that would have imposed a one-time tax on certain taxpayers was defeated, 47.4% yes to 52.6% no, in unofficial results; a separate measure prohibiting new state personal property taxes and certain retroactive state taxes passed. Neither outcome is certified yet (CA Secretary of State · Ballot measures).
  • Administration delay. A contested trust or a late appraisal can add a year of carrying costs, roughly $88,620 at 2045 values, before any decision is possible.

How to hold the plan together

  • Keep 24 months of full carrying costs in cash before the transfer, sized from the table on the Costs page.
  • Sell on a documented timetable or a documented trigger, not in a panic and not on a forecast.
  • Confirm insurance replacement cost, exclusions and earthquake cover before occupancy or letting.
  • Do not rely on the solar credit surviving the transfer until the utility confirms the tariff (CPUC · Net energy metering & net billing).
  • Re-run these five shocks whenever a real input changes.
