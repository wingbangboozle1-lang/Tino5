Trust inheritance: step by step

Practical sequence for a trust-held inherited home
StepAction / evidenceWhy it mattersOfficial source
1 · Identify authorityConfirm trust instrument, successor trustee, distribution powers, debts, title and beneficiaries.A beneficiary may not be able to sell, occupy or lease before distribution or trustee approval.California Courts · Probate
2 · Inventory estateDocument assets, liabilities, ownership, trust status, lifetime gifts, marital status and possible filing obligations.Federal estate tax uses the gross estate, deductions, adjusted taxable gifts and elections—not the value of one home alone.IRS · Estate tax · IRS · Form 706 instructions
3 · Establish basis and valueObtain date-of-death valuation and retain basis, appraisal, improvement, expense and any estate-return documentation.Basis affects tax on later sale and must be supported by records.IRS · Basis of inherited property
4 · Protect Prop 19 deadlinesCheck family eligibility, principal-residence occupancy and submit required homeowner/exclusion claims within current deadlines.Current BOE guidance describes a one-year homeowner-exemption filing period and a three-year exclusion claim period, subject to details and exceptions.California BOE · Proposition 19 · California BOE · Proposition 19 fact sheet
5 · Secure property and operationsBind appropriate insurance, confirm utilities, inspect condition, preserve leases and comply with local rental/habitability rules.Coverage and legal status cannot be assumed to continue unchanged through transfer.California Department of Insurance · Home insurance · California Legislative Information · Codes
6 · Compare options after verificationBuild occupy / rent / sell cases from actual taxes, quotes, rental evidence, sale costs, income-tax basis and portfolio risks.Avoid using gross rent, unsupported insurance or a countywide proxy as net economic value.California FTB · Gifts and inheritance · IRS · Basis of inherited property
7 · Recheck law and fileConfirm federal and state law, forms, transfer recording, tax bills and insurance requirements for the actual transfer year.A transfer expected decades out cannot be safely administered from current rules alone.IRS · Estate and gift tax updates · California BOE · Proposition 19

Scope. This public site contains no household names, addresses, ZIP codes, parcel identifiers, household bills, rents, property values, or account details. Property A is the future trust inheritance; Property B is already owned. Those roles are stated without publishing identifying financial inputs.

Not advice. Rules and thresholds may change before a future transfer. Confirm the then-current law and obtain legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, and investment advice for the actual estate.
