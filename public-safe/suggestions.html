Proposed improvements

Suggestions for review

A prioritized, non-binding action list to improve decision quality. Review and choose a path before implementing changes.

Purpose and guardrail: These are proposed improvements for a reviewer to accept, revise, or defer before implementation. Nothing on this page changes the decision model or asserts a financial winner. Keep property-specific documents and figures in a private, access-controlled working file; publish only anonymized, independently supported conclusions.

Reviewer decision record

For each item, choose Accept, Revise, or Defer in a private review record. Record the responsible professional, evidence needed, decision date, and next review date. This static public page does not collect submissions and contains no personal or property-specific information.

Suggested order of work

  1. Resolve the trust, authority, title, basis, and legal status questions listed on Flagged for review.
  2. Build a private, evidence-linked operating-cost file and obtain current independent quotes.
  3. Only then populate an after-tax decision model with traceable inputs and an explicit baseline plus stress cases.
  4. Compare alternatives; document the chosen path, rejected alternatives, sensitivity, and conditions that would trigger reconsideration.

Scope. This public site contains no household names, addresses, ZIP codes, parcel identifiers, household bills, rents, property values, or account details. Property A is the future trust inheritance; Property B is already owned. Those roles are stated without publishing identifying financial inputs.

Not advice. Rules and thresholds may change before a future transfer. Confirm the then-current law and obtain legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, and investment advice for the actual estate.
