Decision rule: estimate annual after-tax net cash flow for rent, total cost of ownership for occupancy, and after-tax investable proceeds for sale. Then stress each option under inflation, insurance shocks, interest-rate changes, and home-price declines. Do not compare gross rent with sale proceeds.

Break-even principle (illustrative)

For a reference property, holding as a rental beats selling when the net rental yield (after tax, insurance, maintenance, management and vacancy) exceeds the after-tax reinvestment yield from the sale proceeds. Because every input is property-specific, no break-even figure is published here; compute it from verified quotes and the actual secured tax bill at transfer time. See the generic formulas in scripts/desk_model.py.

Scope. This public site contains no household names, addresses, ZIP codes, parcel identifiers, household bills, rents, property values, or account details. Property A is the future trust inheritance; Property B is already owned. Those roles are stated without publishing identifying financial inputs.

Not advice. Rules and thresholds may change before a future transfer. Confirm the then-current law and obtain legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, and investment advice for the actual estate.
