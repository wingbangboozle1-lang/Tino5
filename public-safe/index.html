RESEARCH REVIEW · 11 NOVEMBER 2026

A decision plan, not a prediction

One property is expected through a future trust transfer. Another is already owned. Keep those decisions separate.

Privacy comes first. Property A and Property B are placeholders. Actual valuations, rents, bills, dates of family events, locations and assessment histories are intentionally withheld. Rounding alone cannot prevent identification.

Property A

Future inherited home. Compare occupancy, rental and sale after checking trust terms and transfer-year law.

Property B

Already owned, with existing rental activity and accommodation. No new basis adjustment or reassessment is assumed from inheriting A.

Current decision

Preserve flexibility. Do not make an irreversible sale or relocation decision based on a smooth long-run forecast.
