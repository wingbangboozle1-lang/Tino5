Attorney & CPA Briefing Sheet

Purpose: decide how to evaluate a future trust distribution of Property A. Property B is already owned by the heir and is a separate asset—not part of this inheritance. Expected transfer horizon: 2040–2050 (planning assumption; not a confirmed date).

Decisions to document before projections

  • What facts or thresholds would cause the household to occupy, rent, sell, or choose a mixed path? Write criteria now, before receiving modeled outcomes.
  • Is long-term owner occupancy realistically intended? Verify Prop 19 relationship, occupancy, filing, value-cap, and deadlines with the assessor/CPA; do not claim exclusion without confirming eligibility.
  • Should the sale be in the transfer tax year? Compare same-year sale with later dates, including basis, capital gain, residence exclusion eligibility, transfer/recording costs, and any exchange plan.
  • For sale proceeds, approve an investment policy before funds arrive: reserve, allocation, rebalancing, tax lots, and whether a 24–36 month Treasury staging schedule is acceptable.

Attorney / trustee: obtain and confirm

  • Trust instrument, amendments, trustee authority, title, distribution conditions, debts/liens, beneficiary status, date and form of transfer.
  • Estate gross value, deductible liabilities, lifetime taxable gifts, prior gift/estate returns, portability election, and estate liquidity plan.
  • Date-of-death appraisal and supporting comparables; separate property-tax assessed value from income-tax basis and estate-tax valuation.
  • Proposition 19 eligibility, claim procedure and deadlines; local transfer-tax and recording-fee determination.

CPA / tax adviser: calculate and advise

  • Federal estate tax: estate-level computation using law in force at death—not a generic inheritance allowance; assess prior taxable gifts and portability. IRS estate tax
  • Income-tax basis, depreciation history and recapture on Property B, capital gains, residence exclusion, trust income and state tax. IRS Publication 551 · IRS Publication 527
  • California property-tax transfer rules and current claim forms; distinguish reassessment from income-tax basis. California BOE Proposition 19
  • Compare live/rent/sell/mixed paths at identical transfer dates and horizons, with documented costs, tax assumptions, liquidity and sensitivity ranges.

Open evidence required

Current date-of-death valuation; trust and estate data; verified leases and legal unit status; independent rent estimates; insurer quotes; utility and maintenance records; five-year capital plan; parcel-specific tax-rate area and bill; exact tax basis/depreciation; sale costs; household occupancy plan; investment risk tolerance.

Use: bring this blank checklist to independent legal and tax advisers. Do not add identifying details to the public site. Rules may change before transfer; recheck official sources at the time of decision.
