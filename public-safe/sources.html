Verification standard

  • Verified means the stated rule was read on the linked official page on the date shown. It does not mean a private property fact was confirmed.
  • Derived means arithmetic on a banded anchor and/or a verified rule, with the formula shown on the page.
  • Assumption means a named model input chosen by the analyst; sensitivities are on the Decision and Black Swans pages.
  • Unverified means no official source supports it. The cell is left blank, not filled with a guess, and the item appears on Flagged for review.

Why some official pages are not used

  • County assessor property-search results are not cited because looking up a specific parcel would defeat the anonymization this site depends on.
  • Insurer quote engines and utility rate calculators produce property-specific numbers; those are quotes, not published facts, so they are left for the owner to obtain privately.
  • Secondary commentary, blogs and estimator sites were not used for any figure.

Reproducing the numbers

The published pages are generated by scripts/build_desk.py from scripts/desk_model.py. Both are plain Python with no dependencies. Every figure on this site can be recomputed from the assumptions block on the Projections page.
