Black swans & uneven economic paths

Stress tests are scenarios, not probabilities or historical replays. A smooth baseline hides sequence risk and cash shortages.

Decision consequence

Renting concentrates local housing and tenant risk; selling concentrates timing risk unless proceeds are diversified and liquidity is preserved. Occupying creates a housing benefit, not spendable yield. Re-run all three on the same shock path and liquidation date before selecting a winner.
