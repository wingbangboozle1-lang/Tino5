B's taxable value grows at the Prop 13 cap and is not reset by A's inheritance. Rent growth is capped in practice by the local rent ordinance and by state law while it remains in force (SF.gov · Allowable rent increase · CA Civil Code 1947.12); the model uses 2.0%, below market rent growth, for that reason.

How to read these tables

  • Nominal dollars. No inflation adjustment. At 2.5% inflation, $7,946,240 in 2050 is about $4,393,280 in 2026 purchasing power.
  • Net sale proceeds assume 6.0% of price for commission, escrow, title and documentary transfer tax (CA Rev. & Tax. Code 11911).
  • Stepped-up basis means no capital-gains tax on appreciation before death (IRS · Inherited property basis); the projection does not deduct one.
  • Declines are possible. A market crash can lower the assessed value only while market value is below the factored base value (BOE · How property is assessed (Pub 800-10) · SCC Assessor · Decline in value); the Black Swans page tests that.
