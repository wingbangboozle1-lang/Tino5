50 years: transparent, fictional arithmetic

Not the owner’s forecast. To avoid exposing private financial fingerprints, this example starts with a fictional $1,000,000 home, $200,000 assessment and $15,000 operating budget. None describes either property. Actual annual valuation, assessment and bills remain unverified and are not filled in.

Assumptions and limitations

Assume nominal appreciation 3%, general costs/CPI 2.5%, assessment growth 2%, and illustrative base tax 1%. These are analyst-selected sensitivity inputs, not official forecasts or a certified tax bill. Source links provide legal/economic context only. No inheritance reassessment event is applied in this pre-transfer continuity illustration; it must not be used as a post-transfer bill.

For n = year − 2026: value = $1,000,000 × 1.03ⁿ; real value = value ÷ 1.025ⁿ; assessment = $200,000 × 1.02ⁿ; illustrative base tax = assessment × 1%; operating budget = $15,000 × 1.025ⁿ. Real dollars use 2026 purchasing power. Only display values are rounded to $10; intermediate calculations are not rounded. Tax excludes bonds, direct assessments, exemptions and supplemental bills.
