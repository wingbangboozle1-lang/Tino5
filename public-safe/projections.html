Reproducible method for future use

  • Market-value scenario: verified transfer-year baseline × (1 + named annual growth assumption)years. Show several growth paths; do not call one a forecast.
  • California taxable value: start from the assessor's factored base-year value; Proposition 13 generally limits annual assessed-value inflation adjustments to 2%, but ownership change and exclusions can alter the result.
  • Transfer: apply the Prop 19 family-home rules only if transferor/transferee, principal-residence, filing and timing requirements are met.
  • Annual tax: actual taxable value × the actual tax-rate-area levy, plus applicable direct assessments. Use the secured bill, not a countywide proxy.

Generic calculation helpers in the repository contain no household values. They do not output a property forecast without verified inputs.
