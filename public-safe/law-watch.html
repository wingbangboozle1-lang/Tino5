Scheduled to change or expire

  • 1 January 2030 — the statewide rent cap is repealed by its own terms unless extended (CA Civil Code 1947.12).
  • 15 February 2027 — the current Prop 19 allowance period ends and a new adjusted amount applies (BOE · Prop 19 fact sheet (Pub 801)).
  • Every autumn — the IRS publishes the next year's exclusion and the BOE publishes the assessment inflation factor; both are inputs to the model.
  • Every 1 March — the local allowable rent increase is reset (SF.gov · Allowable rent increase).

What would actually change the decision

  • A smaller Prop 19 allowance makes occupying A more expensive and strengthens the case for selling.
  • A lower federal exclusion matters only if the total estate is near or above it.
  • Rent deregulation improves the rental case; tighter caps weaken it further.
  • Higher transaction taxes make selling more expensive but do not change the ranking until selling costs reach about 34% of value — far above today's total.
  • A change in step-up rules would be the most consequential change of all, because it would put a capital-gains tax on the sale of A.
