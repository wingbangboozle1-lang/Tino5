Current inheritance and property-tax rules checked against official sources
RuleCurrent statementScope / caveatOfficial source
Federal estate-tax exclusionFor 2026, the federal basic exclusion amount is $15,000,000 per decedent.Estate-level calculation; not a separate per-heir inheritance allowance. Estate composition, deductions, prior taxable gifts and elections matter. Future amounts must be rechecked.IRS · Estate tax · IRS · Estate and gift tax updates
California estate taxCalifornia says no estate-tax return is required for decedents dying on or after 1 January 2005.This does not eliminate federal estate tax or income tax on later income/gain.California State Controller · Estate tax
California inheritance receiptCalifornia FTB says a gift or inheritance is not included in the recipient's income; income it later produces is taxable.Rental income, interest, dividends and taxable gain are distinct from receipt of the inheritance.California FTB · Gifts and inheritance
Inherited income-tax basisGenerally, basis is fair market value on date of death; alternate valuation and consistent-basis rules can apply.A sale near basis is not automatically tax-free: valuation, trust/estate inclusion, later appreciation and expenses matter.IRS · Basis of inherited property · IRS · Form 706 instructions
California parent-child property-tax exclusionCurrent Prop 19 rules require eligible family transfer and principal-residence requirements; value above the adjusted limit can increase taxable value.Claim timing and eligibility are critical; no exclusion for other real property under the current rule. Verify the applicable-year adjusted limit.California BOE · Proposition 19 · California BOE · Proposition 19 fact sheet
Property-tax assessmentCalifornia assessed value is generally subject to a 2% annual inflation cap; the base property-tax rate is generally 1% plus voter-approved bonded debt, with reassessment and exceptions governed by law.The actual bill also depends on local tax-rate area and direct assessments; no property-specific amount is published here.California BOE · How property is assessed · California BOE · Property tax overview
