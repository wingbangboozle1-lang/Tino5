Year-by-year projections

Market value grows at the modelled appreciation rate; assessed value grows at the Proposition 13 cap of 2% until a change of ownership, then resets. Three assessed-value tracks are shown for Property A: what happens if the transfer never occurs, what happens if the child moves in and keeps the exclusion, and what happens if the property is rented and fully reassessed.

The mechanism, in one line

Because assessed value grows at 2% while market value is assumed to grow at 4%, the gap between the two doubles roughly every generation. That gap is the entire prize in Proposition 13 — and Proposition 19 decides how much of it survives a death.
