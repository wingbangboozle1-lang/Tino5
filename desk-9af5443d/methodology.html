Methodology & privacy

How the desk sources, rounds, models and protects privacy.

Method

  • Source policy. Every table row carries an official-source link (government or agency publisher) or is left blank and tagged unverified, owner-supplied or assumption. Nothing is inferred or invented; no value is filled in where no official source exists.
  • Rounding. Money is rounded to the nearest $10 unless the source value is itself an exact statutory or agency figure, which is quoted as published and tagged exact (statutory).
  • Model. Projections are model outputs: value growth 5%/yr (assumption), property tax 1% of value (Prop 13 general levy), operating-cost inflation 3%/yr. The distribution chart varies growth at 3%/5%/7% because growth and inflation are never consistent over a 50-year horizon.
  • Black-swan phased-investing path. Sale proceeds are parked in 3M-2Y Treasuries for 24-36 months, then phased into stocks; the path is modelled on the merged desks and the Treasury reference is carried here. Reinvestment risk (parked cash earning less than the portfolio, or markets rising while parked) is stated next to the headline wherever the path is shown.
  • Privacy. No names, street addresses, ZIP codes, neighbourhoods, parcel or account numbers, or contact details appear anywhere. "Property A" and "Property B" are placeholders; only broad county-level context is shown. City-level Census data and property-specific city/neighbourhood labels are omitted. Official links to local rules may identify the issuing jurisdiction. Owner inputs are labelled; this public desk does not publish parcel, account, street or contact identifiers.
  • Not advice. This desk is decision support, not tax, legal or investment advice. This integration did not fetch or independently reopen the linked authorities; re-check current law and local applicability before acting, and confirm owner-supplied figures with records and professionals.
