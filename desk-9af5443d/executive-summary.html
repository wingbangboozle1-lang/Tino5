Executive summary

Headline recommendation, the conditions that flip it, and the decisions recorded on 2026-11-15.

Headline (baseline, $10 rounding). The recommendation is unchanged from the merged desks: on the baseline assumptions, selling Property A at inheritance and reinvesting after tax out-keeps holding it as a rental, because a $3,100,000 inherited home largely reassesses to market value and carries a material tax bill, while the Prop 19 allowance shields only about $1,044,590 of the gap — and only if the heir occupies within one year. This desk adds the tax-law detail that sharpens the numbers: the 1% MHST applies in any year CA taxable income exceeds $1,000,000, and a conditional local parcel-tax scenario may affect Property B from the FY2027-28 bill; jurisdiction and class still require private confirmation.
