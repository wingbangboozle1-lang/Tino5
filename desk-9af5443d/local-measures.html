Local measures: Proposition H & related items

Local tax and election measures with conditional applicability and official source links.

Two distinct local measures use the name Proposition H. The 2024 measure concerned firefighter retirement benefits; the 2026 measure is a transit parcel-tax schedule. This page preserves the published terms while leaving parcel-specific jurisdiction and applicability unverified.
