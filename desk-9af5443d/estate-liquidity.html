Estate tax & liquidity plan

Verified federal thresholds, liquidity sources, and the questions to settle before the figures arrive.

Estate liquidity plan. The inheritance includes a "considerable amount of money" that could exceed the federal exclusion. If it does, the estate needs liquid assets to pay the tax without a forced, ill-timed sale. This page sets out the verified thresholds, the portability and prior-gift questions to ask now, and the liquidity sources to map.
