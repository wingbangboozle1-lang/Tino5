CA income tax & Form 540

The 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax read from the official Form 540 instructions, and the updated sensitivity value.

The 1% tax above one million dollars — read from the official Form 540 instructions. California's Behavioral Health Services Tax is 1% of California taxable income above $1,000,000, computed on Form 540 line 62 from taxable income (Form 540 line 19). It is a per-year surtax: it applies in any year taxable income crosses the threshold and disappears in years below it. The updated sensitivity value is below.
