SF measures: Prop H & others

Proposition H (2024 and 2026) with official terms, and the companion measures that touch Property B.

Two different "Proposition H"s — do not confuse them. San Francisco has used the letter H twice in recent elections: Prop H (Nov 5, 2024), a firefighter-pension charter amendment, and Prop H (Nov 3, 2026), the Muni parcel tax. This page models both with their official terms, and separates enacted changes from proposals.
