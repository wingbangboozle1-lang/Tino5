Narrative (economic history)

  • Uneven growth. Prices can fall for several years and then recover. The bust-and-recovery path tests a sharp fall in 2027-2031 followed by a rebound. Check any drawdown against the FRED metro series before relying on it.
  • Inflation shocks. A period of high inflation raises insurance, water and maintenance costs, and can lift nominal sale prices. The stagflation path raises insurance growth to 8% a year to test fixed-cost pressure.
  • Flat decades. A long period with no price growth weakens the sell-and-reinvest case, because reinvestment becomes the only source of return. The flat-decade path tests this.
  • Rates. The rate-shock path lowers after-tax reinvestment returns and favours keeping the home if it is kept.
  • Policy. Prop 19 could change through legislation or a ballot measure. The current repeal initiative did not qualify (S21). No future change is modelled as enacted.
