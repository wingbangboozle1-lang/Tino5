Selling and reinvesting ranks highest in 24 of 27 cases. Living in the home ranks highest in 3; renting in 0.

Each row values three options 10 years after transfer, with the live option set to 100. Live: the heir occupies the home (Prop 19 applies). Rent: the home is let out and reassessed. Sell: the home is sold at transfer; net proceeds are reinvested at the after-tax return. Growth cases are 1%, 3.5% and 5% a year. Reinvestment after tax is 3.2% (Treasuries), 4.0% (balanced) or 5.0% (equities). All are ASSUMED and anchored to the CBO 10-year Treasury forecast of 4.1% for 2026 (S11).

Prop 19 exclusion removed (hypothetical)

If the parent-child exclusion were removed at transfer, an occupied heir would be reassessed to market value. In the base case for 2045, the live option falls to 96.8 (live today = 100), against 100 under current law. The exact text of initiative 25-0017 was not reviewed here, and the initiative did not qualify (S21).
