Under Prop 19 for an occupied home, the taxable value at transfer is the factored base value if the market value is at or below base plus the transfer allowance. Above that limit, it is the market value minus the allowance. The allowance is $1,044,590 until February 2027 (BOE; published to the dollar, rounded here). After February 2027 the model holds it at an ASSUMED 1.1% a year, derived from the last two BOE adjustments (flagged). If the home is rented, no exclusion applies and the taxable value is the market value at transfer. The claim and occupancy rules are in RTC 63.2 (S06). This is a simplified approximation; confirm with the assessor or a CPA.
