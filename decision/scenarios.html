All break-evens are computed at a 2045 transfer with the baseline assumptions and the housing-inclusive comparison. They move when the assumptions move, which is the point: they are thresholds to monitor, not answers to trust.

Why the ranking is not the headline. The order of these three options is produced by a model running on assumptions, several of which are currently unknown. What survives every sensitivity is narrower and more useful: the property tax jump is real and large, the step-up in basis removes any urgency to decide, and renting is dominated unless the property can be let for far more than the assumed yield.

Occupy net cash is negative because it is a cost; the housing value column is what living there is worth at market rent, and is not cash. Rent's depreciation shelter is a non-cash deduction against rental income under the 27.5 year straight line assumption; it comes back as recapture taxed at up to 25% federally when the property is sold. Sell's portfolio column is the value before the tax that would be due on liquidating it.
