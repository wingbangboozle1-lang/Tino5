How the numbers were produced

Method &amp; privacy

Privacy. No name, street address, parcel or account number appears anywhere on this site. Properties are identified only as Property A (Santa Clara County, to be inherited through a trust) and Property B (San Francisco, already owned). All money is rounded to the nearest $10 so published figures cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nothing.

How the model works

The projection engine is deterministic: the same inputs always produce the same output, and the code is in this repository under decision/_src/. It works like this.

  1. Assessed values follow the law, not a forecast

    Property A's assessed value grows at exactly the 2% Proposition 13 ceiling until the ownership changes. That is why the assessed value columns on the projections page are the most reliable figures on this site.

  2. Market values follow an assumption

    4.0% nominal with a band. Every market value figure on the site inherits that assumption and should be read as a scenario.

  3. At the transfer the property is re-priced two ways

    With the Proposition 19 exclusion, the new taxable value is the factored base year value plus any market value above the cap. Without it, the new taxable value is the full market value. Both are shown for every year so the cost of each choice is visible.

  4. Each option is a cash flow stream

    Occupy pays tax, insurance, maintenance and water and receives the solar credit and the rent from the San Francisco unit it frees up. Renting collects rent net of vacancy and management and pays the same costs. Selling turns the property into a portfolio that compounds, with the income portion taxed each year and the growth portion taxed at the end.

  5. Each option is credited with the home it lets you live in

    This is the step most analyses skip. Occupying gives you a house worth several million dollars to live in; renting and selling leave you in your San Francisco unit. Valuing that at market rent is the only way to compare the three fairly, and it is why the options page shows two columns.

  6. The result is a threshold, not a verdict

    The model solves for the portfolio return, the rental yield and the growth rate at which the ranking flips. Those are the numbers to act on.

Privacy method

No identifiers

  • No name, street address, parcel number, assessor's parcel identifier, account number, email address or telephone number appears on any page.
  • The two properties are called Property A and Property B, identified only by county.
  • No ZIP code, city name or neighbourhood name appears on any page.
  • No trust name, trustee name or beneficiary name appears anywhere.

All money rounded

  • Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10, following the instruction that figures be published in a form that cannot be traced back through a public search.
  • Assessed values and tax bills are public record in California. Rounding is what makes publishing them safe.
  • Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published, because they are statewide constants and identify no one.
  • Each figure is rounded on its own. Where two published figures are subtracted the result can differ by $10 from the difference quoted on another page, which is itself computed before rounding.

Verified or withheld

  • Every table row links to the official document it was read from.
  • Where a value could not be confirmed, the cell is blank and marked unverified. No value is ever inferred to fill a gap.
  • Owner-supplied figures are marked as owner input and are never presented as verified.

What could not be fixed here

  • Earlier generations of this project published unrounded figures. They remain in the public git history, in merged branches and in pull request diffs.
  • No commit in any branch can remove them. Repository visibility or a history rewrite is the only remedy, and that is an owner decision — see F15 on the flagged page.

Reproducing this

python3 decision/_src/build.py rebuilds every page. python3 decision/_src/verify.py re-runs the checks: every table row carries an official source link, every cited host is on the official allowlist, all money is rounded to $10, every internal link resolves, and no personal identifier pattern appears anywhere in the output.

Not tax, legal or investment advice. Figures are rounded to the nearest $10 and every table row links to the official source it was read from; rows that could not be confirmed carry a blank value and an unverified marker. Rules and thresholds change — re-check the current law before acting.
