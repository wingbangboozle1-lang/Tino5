Suggestions

Privacy. No name, street address, parcel or account number appears anywhere on this site. Properties are identified only as Property A (Santa Clara County, to be inherited through a trust) and Property B (San Francisco, already owned). All money is rounded to the nearest $10 so published figures cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nothing.
Nothing here has been done. This page exists so that each suggestion can be read, accepted, modified or rejected before anything changes. No commit in this branch implements any of them, and none will be implemented without a decision recorded against its number.

Fifteen suggestions, ordered by the size of what they protect. Each states why it matters, what it is worth, what it costs to do, and what could go wrong.

How to review these

  1. Read the why column first

    Some of these protect something irreplaceable — a filing deadline, a tax base, a piece of privacy. Those matter more than their dollar value suggests.

  2. Group them

    Four of them are pure information gathering with no downside. Do those first and the rest of this site gets sharper.

  3. Record a decision against each number

    Accept, modify, or reject. A rejected suggestion with a reason is worth more than one left open.

  4. Only then change anything

    None of these is implemented here, by design.
