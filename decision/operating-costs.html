Running costs

Privacy. No name, street address, parcel or account number appears anywhere on this site. Properties are identified only as Property A (Santa Clara County, to be inherited through a trust) and Property B (San Francisco, already owned). All money is rounded to the nearest $10 so published figures cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nothing.

Four of the six cost lines on this page are owner estimates with no source. They are shown that way deliberately, because they feed every projection that follows.

Water, electricity and insurance are held flat in real terms in the projections and grown at the assumed inflation rate. That is a simplification: California insurance premiums have been moving much faster than general inflation, and the CDI market data shows direct written premium for the homeowners line rising from $14.5bn in 2024 to $16.4bn in 2025 with a reported 2025 loss ratio of 170%.
