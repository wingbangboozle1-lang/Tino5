Law watch

Privacy. No name, street address, parcel or account number appears anywhere on this site. Properties are identified only as Property A (Santa Clara County, to be inherited through a trust) and Property B (San Francisco, already owned). All money is rounded to the nearest $10 so published figures cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nothing.

The transfer window is 2040 to 2050. Over that horizon Congress could restrict the step-up in basis and California voters could revisit Proposition 13. Neither is on the books today.
