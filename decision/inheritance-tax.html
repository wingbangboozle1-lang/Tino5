Inheritance &amp; estate tax

Privacy. No name, street address, parcel or account number appears anywhere on this site. Properties are identified only as Property A (Santa Clara County, to be inherited through a trust) and Property B (San Francisco, already owned). All money is rounded to the nearest $10 so published figures cannot be matched back to a public record. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published because they are statewide constants and identify nothing.

The inheritance includes cash and investments as well as the house, so the federal threshold matters. California's position does not require any analysis at all: it is prohibited by statute.

This is the cleanest answer on the whole site: California cannot tax the transfer, and no local government in California can either. Only the federal estate tax can apply, and only above a threshold that is higher than most estates.
