Homeowners insurance — why there is no single “official” ZIP price

California does not publish a ZIP-specific HO-3 quote. Two official series measure different things and must not be joined into a trend:

Planning figure used on this site: $1,660/yr for Cupertino HO-3 (primary residence) and SF landlord policy (placeholder — landlord policies typically cost more). User estimate “around $1,500” is within the NAIC range. Action: get bound quotes for (a) 95014 HO-3 owner-occupied, (b) 94122 DP-3 landlord, (c) Cupertino DP-3 if rented. Wildfire, roof age, and replacement cost drive ZIP-level variation more than the statewide average.

Do not budget $1,500 as a quote. Budget $1,660 as a planning midpoint and replace it with a bound quote before deciding. Flagged F-11.

Maintenance & repairs

No official ZIP-specific maintenance figure exists. This site publishes no maintenance number (blank, Flagged F-09) and shows break-evens at 0.5%/1.0%/1.5% of market value as illustrative sensitivities only (Decision Analysis). To replace the blank: obtain (a) 5-year repair history, (b) roof/HVAC age + remaining life, (c) insurer/contractor reserve estimate. SF multi-unit stock should be budgeted above Cupertino single-family — quantify the difference privately; do not publish addresses or contractor PII.
