Scenarios: live, rent, sell

Eight options under five economic regimes. The 2040 transfer year is the headline; other transfer years are on the What-would-change page.

Reinvestment and opportunity risk. Selling and investing at once means you may invest just before a fall. Parking cash and phasing it in can reduce that, but only if the entry happens to be after the fall. The model's crash results are on one assumed path, and a phased investor who entered at the bottom would look better only in hindsight. Read the black-swan page before using these numbers.
