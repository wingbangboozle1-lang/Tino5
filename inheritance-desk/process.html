Trust process and deadlines

Step-by-step, from now to sale, with the dates that matter.

This is a planning checklist, not legal advice. Trust and estate steps depend on the document and the state. Confirm with an estate attorney.

Timeline

Now
Gather the trust document, the deed, and the last three assessment notices. Ask the attorney which trust type holds Property A.
At death
Get death certificates. Notify the trustee. The trust (not the estate) usually holds the house. Do not change the title yet.
Within 9 months
If an estate tax return is needed, file Form 706 with the IRS. Request a 6-month extension before the due date if the estimated tax is not paid in full (see the IRS filing guide).
Within 1 year
If the heir will live in the house, move in and claim the homeowners' exemption. Prop 19 requires the house to become the child's principal residence within 1 year (BOE).
Within 3 years
File BOE-19-P to claim the Prop 19 parent-child exclusion, or before any third-party transfer (BOE Prop 19 page).
Each year
Property tax bills: secured tax installments are due Nov 1 and Feb 1; delinquent after Dec 10 and Apr 10 (Santa Clara County Tax Rate Book).
At sale
Basis is generally the value at death, which limits capital gains tax. Check the Form 8971 value from the executor.
