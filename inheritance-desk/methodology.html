Methodology and model inputs

How the scenario results are built, and every input's status.

Method

Monthly simulation from transfer to 2076, all results converted to 2026 dollars. Live-in and rent options count the property's value, rent and costs. Phased options park cash in T-bills, then buy stocks over 12 months. Mixed options switch at a set date. A Prop 19 cap is applied to live-in only (and to the mixed live option).

Grid

The full 120-cell grid (3 transfer years × 5 regimes × 8 option variants) is in data/model.json. The model script is _src/model.py.

Limits

  • Not a forecast. Single deterministic path per regime.
  • Avoided-rent credit assumes the heir would otherwise pay rent of the same value.
  • No state income tax, no heir's own tax, no trust-level tax, no gift strategy.
