Glossary

Plain-language meanings for the terms used in this desk.

TermMeaningSource
BasisThe tax value used to measure gain when property is sold. For inherited property it is generally the value at death.irs_p551
Prop 19California rule letting a parent-child transfer keep a lower assessed value if the child moves in and makes it their main home.boe_prop19
Assessed valueThe value on which property tax is charged. In California it resets on a change in ownership unless an exclusion applies.rtc51
Basic exclusion amountThe amount a person can pass free of federal estate or gift tax (2026: $15,000,000).irs_estate
Form 706Federal estate tax return.irs_filing
Form 709Federal gift tax return.irs_filing
Net investment income taxA 3.8% tax on some investment income above a threshold.irs_tc559
T-billA short-term US Treasury bill. The model uses a 3-month T-bill for parked cash.treasury
Phased entryMoving cash into stocks in equal instalments over time, instead of all at once.Model definition (no external source)
Break-even rent yieldThe gross rent (as a share of value) at which living in the house and renting it out give the same result.Model definition (no external source)
RegimeA set of economic assumptions (inflation, stock return, housing growth) applied to the model.Model definition (no external source)
