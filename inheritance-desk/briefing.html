Briefing for attorney and CPA. Not advice. Property A and Property B. Full detail on the desk pages.

Question

Property A is expected to pass by trust around 2040–2050. Choose: live in it, rent it out, or sell and invest. Mixed paths also modelled.

Headline (2040 transfer, base, 2026 dollars)

Live (rent avoided counted) $7,161,050 · Rent $6,398,070 · Sell and invest at once $12,056,130. Break-even avoided rent about 3.54% of value a year.

Rules to confirm

  • Federal basic exclusion 2026 $15,000,000 (published); gift exclusion $19,000 (published). Form 706 due 9 months after death (IRS).
  • Inherited basis generally the value at death (Pub 551).
  • Prop 19 parent-child: move in within 1 year; claim within 3 years (BOE). Cap wording conflicts (F03).
  • California estate or inheritance tax: none confirmed from primary source yet (F05).
  • Capital gains: 0% bracket to $49,450 single; NIIT 3.8% above $200,000 single (IRS).

Open items

  • Composite tax rate (F01); insurance (F06); owner inputs unverified (F02); crash-regime proxy (F07).

Questions for counsel

  • Trust type and the trustee's powers over sale or occupancy.
  • Whether the heir may occupy the house under the trust terms.
