What would change the answer

The answer depends on a few inputs. The sensitivities below show how much each one moves the result.

Break-even avoided rent: living in the house (counting avoided rent) matches renting it out when the avoided rent is worth about 3.54% of value a year (2040 transfer, base regime). The model assumes 4.00%. Cash-only living (A0) matches renting out at a gross yield of about 0.46%.

Questions that change the answer

  • Would the heir otherwise pay rent of the same value? If not, the avoided-rent credit does not apply and A0 is the right comparison.
  • Is the heir likely to move in within 1 year of transfer? That is required to keep the Prop 19 parent-child benefit (BOE rules, Federal and CA rules).
  • Does the trust hold the property, and is it a revocable or irrevocable trust? (Not modelled; see questions.)
