California Legislature Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2 (Proposition 19) Principal residence of the transferor must become the principal residence of the transferee within one year; transferee must file for the homeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption within one year; exclusion is removed when the transferee is no longer eligible; new taxable value = prior taxable value plus the amount by which FMV exceeds prior taxable value plus $1,000,000 (biennially adjusted); claim within 3 years or before transfer to a third party, whichever is earlier; a claim is not a public document; a transfer through an inter vivos or testamentary trust is a transfer. official California Legislature

What is deliberately not cited

No secondary commentary, blog, brokerage page or news article is cited as authority for any figure. Where such a page was consulted to find a primary document, the primary document is what appears here. Several widely repeated figures — including some parcel tax estimates for Measure H that do not match the ordinance's own schedule — were discarded for that reason.

This is a planning document, not advice. It is prepared for review by a licensed California attorney and a CPA before any step is taken. No legal, tax or investment advice is given or implied. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly and marked; every other figure is an assumption, an owner-supplied anchor, or arithmetic on those.

Nothing here identifies a person, a street address, an assessor's parcel number, or a ZIP code. Owner-supplied figures are approximate and unverified. Money is rounded to the nearest $10 except where a statute or official table states an exact amount.
