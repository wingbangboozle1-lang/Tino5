The spread dominates the ranking. This chart is the strongest argument for not treating the recommendation as settled. In three of five regimes the lowest pure choice still leaves more than the highest pure choice does in another. A household that cannot tolerate the spread should not be making a twenty-year decision on a point estimate at all.
