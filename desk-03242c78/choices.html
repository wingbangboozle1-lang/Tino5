All choices, compared at the same dates and horizons

Nine paths, three transfer dates, twenty years, identical tax treatment for identical facts. Only the decisions differ. Every figure below is the same model with one or more switches changed, so a difference between two rows is a difference between two decisions and nothing else.

How to read a twenty-year rank. These are twenty-year projections. A ranking produced by a twenty-year projection is not a prediction of the future; it is a statement that, if these particular assumptions held, this path would leave more. The distribution of outcomes under each path is on projection charts, and it overlaps heavily.

Two paths are missing from this table on purpose. A like-kind exchange of A for other rental property is not modelled as a path because it is a decision that needs counsel on facts not in the file; the considerations are set out on like-kind exchange decision. A partial sale or a fractional-interest gift is likewise not modelled. Nothing is implemented without approval; see suggestions for review.

This is a planning document, not advice. It is prepared for review by a licensed California attorney and a CPA before any step is taken. No legal, tax or investment advice is given or implied. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly and marked; every other figure is an assumption, an owner-supplied anchor, or arithmetic on those.

Nothing here identifies a person, a street address, an assessor's parcel number, or a ZIP code. Owner-supplied figures are approximate and unverified. Money is rounded to the nearest $10 except where a statute or official table states an exact amount.
