Suggestions for review

Nothing on this desk has been implemented. These are the proposals that follow from the analysis, in the order they should be considered, each with what it would cost and what it would change. Any of them can be rejected without affecting the others.

How to use this page
Read each proposal, choose a path, and nothing happens until you say so.

Every suggestion is a decision the household could take. None has been taken. The desk recommends the first three; the rest are options that follow from the analysis and are listed so the choice is visible rather than implicit.

Proposals, with what each costs and what it would change.
Suggestion What it would cost What it would change Desk's view Status
1 Write the investment policy before the transfer. A short document fixing the cash reserve, the phasing schedule, the asset mix, the spending rule and who may override it. A few hours of the household's time, plus a review by the CPA. The largest controllable risk in the whole decision. Recommended derived
2 Obtain a current tax bill for Property A. It carries the tax rate area and the exact rate, which is the weakest verified figure in the model. Free; it is already in the household's records. Replaces a 1.15%-1.41% band with one number. Recommended derived
3 Obtain three written rental comparables for Property A. Free from most local managers. Converts the letting paths from a floor to an estimate. Recommended derived
4 Commission appraisals of both properties. A few hundred dollars each. Replaces both value anchors with defensible figures. Optional derived
5 Obtain the trust instrument and have counsel summarise its distribution terms. Counsel's time. Could change what is inherited, before any number matters. Optional, but ahead of the numbers derived
6 Ask the CPA whether a portability election was made at the first death. A phone call. Potentially the largest single item in the file. Optional, high value derived
7 Commission building inspections of both properties. A few hundred dollars each. Replaces the maintenance reserve assumption. Optional derived
8 Adopt the phased-entry schedule: 24 months parked, 24 monthly slices. About $978,350 of expected return at a 2045 transfer. A defined answer to a crash in the first two years. Optional derived
9 Re-run the model every year and record each decision with its date. An hour a year. Keeps the recommendation honest as inputs change. Recommended derived
10 Do not complete a like-kind exchange on these facts. Nothing. Avoids forfeiting the section 121 exclusion and the state exclusion. Recommended, as a decision not to act derived

What approval looks like

Approval is a sentence in the decision log on the annual re-run page: the item, the date, and who approved it. Until then the item stays open, and the desk's recommendation stays a recommendation.

Nothing in this repository acts on its own. There is no automation that moves money, files a form or contacts anyone. The site is static text and the model is arithmetic.

This is a planning document, not advice. It is prepared for review by a licensed California attorney and a CPA before any step is taken. No legal, tax or investment advice is given or implied. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly and marked; every other figure is an assumption, an owner-supplied anchor, or arithmetic on those.

Nothing here identifies a person, a street address, an assessor's parcel number, or a ZIP code. Owner-supplied figures are approximate and unverified. Money is rounded to the nearest $10 except where a statute or official table states an exact amount.
