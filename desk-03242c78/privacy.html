Privacy statement

This desk is published so that it can be reviewed. It is built so that it cannot be used to identify anyone.

What is not here

The categories removed, and how.
Category Treatment Status
Names of any person None appear. The household is referred to as 'the household' or 'the heir' throughout. derived
Street addresses None appear. The properties are described by county only. derived
Assessor's parcel numbers None appear, in any form. derived
ZIP codes or postal codes None appear. derived
City or neighbourhood names Do not appear. The desk names only California, Santa Clara County and San Francisco County, because the tax rules differ at county level and the comparison would be misleading without them. derived
Account numbers None. No financial account, loan, or policy number is referenced. derived
Assessed values exact to the dollar Rounded to $10,000 and $5,000 before entering the model. An exact assessed value matches one parcel in an open assessment roll. derived
The household's year-by-year tax and assessed-value history Deliberately not stored in this repository. Only the rate band it implies is kept, as three numbers. derived
Photographs None. The desk is text and one stylesheet. derived

Why the county names stay

Property tax is administered by county and the rules genuinely differ: the transfer tax, the recording fees, the assessment calendar and the rent ordinance are all local. Removing the county names would make the desk unusable without meaningfully protecting anyone, because a county of a million people does not identify a household. The parcel-level facts — value, address, assessment history — are what would identify it, and none of them appear.

What the site does not do

  • It sets no cookies and stores nothing in the browser.
  • It loads nothing from any other server: no fonts, no scripts, no images, no analytics.
  • It has no forms, no comments, and no way for a reader to submit anything.
  • It contains no email address or phone number.
  • It links to official documents only, and none of those links carries a query parameter that could identify a reader.

If you find something that identifies someone

That is a defect of the same severity as a wrong figure, and more urgent. The page it appears on should be removed from publication immediately, before the figure is corrected. The flagged for review page is where such an item would be recorded.

This is a planning document, not advice. It is prepared for review by a licensed California attorney and a CPA before any step is taken. No legal, tax or investment advice is given or implied. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly and marked; every other figure is an assumption, an owner-supplied anchor, or arithmetic on those.

Nothing here identifies a person, a street address, an assessor's parcel number, or a ZIP code. Owner-supplied figures are approximate and unverified. Money is rounded to the nearest $10 except where a statute or official table states an exact amount.
