Questions for the CPA

  1. What is the filing status, and does the section 121 exclusion double to $500,000?
  2. Have prior taxable gifts used part of the exclusion? If so, how much remains, and are the gift tax returns available?
  3. Is a portability election available and was a Form 706 filed at the first death? If not, can a late election still be made?
  4. What is the trust's income tax status, and who reports income during administration?
  5. Will the household be a California resident at the time of sale? If not, how is the California-source gain computed?
  6. What allocation between land and building should be used for depreciation if the house is let, and is an appraisal needed to support it?
  7. What is the expected state and federal tax on the parking-window interest while proceeds sit in Treasuries?
