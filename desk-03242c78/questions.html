What the desk did not attempt

  • Reading the trust. Not available, and reading it is the attorney's job, not this desk's.
  • Appraising either property. Not a licensed appraiser.
  • Quoting insurance. Not an insurer or broker.
  • Forecasting house prices or equity returns. No one can, and the desk presents ranges and regimes rather than a forecast.
  • Advising on specific securities. Out of scope; see the policy page.
  • Modelling the rental market for Property A. No comparables were obtained; the rent is a labelled assumption.
Each of these is a boundary the desk states rather than crosses. A projection that pretends to know any of them would be less useful, not more.

This is a planning document, not advice. It is prepared for review by a licensed California attorney and a CPA before any step is taken. No legal, tax or investment advice is given or implied. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly and marked; every other figure is an assumption, an owner-supplied anchor, or arithmetic on those.

Nothing here identifies a person, a street address, an assessor's parcel number, or a ZIP code. Owner-supplied figures are approximate and unverified. Money is rounded to the nearest $10 except where a statute or official table states an exact amount.
