# Decision analysis — Cupertino house (inheritance ~2040–2050 via trust)

All figures rounded to nearest $10. Projections use labeled assumptions: assessed value +2%/yr (Prop 13 cap), market value +3%/yr, effective tax rates 1.335% (Cupertino) / 2.135% (SF) derived from user-provided bills, Prop 19 $1M adjustment growing 3%/yr from the official $1,044,586 (Feb 2025–Feb 2027).

## 2045 projected snapshot (inheritance year)
- Cupertino market value ≈ $5,435,870; factored base year value ≈ $802,030; Prop 19 adjustment ≈ $1,831,690.
- **A. Live in it (Prop 19 exclusion)**: taxable value = min(market, FBYV + adjustment) ≈ **$2,633,720** → property tax ≈ **$35,170/yr**.
- **B. Rent it out (no occupancy)**: full reassessment to **$5,435,870** → property tax ≈ **$72,580/yr**.
- **C. Sell**: step-up in basis = date-of-death FMV → ≈ $0 taxable gain; net of 6% selling costs ≈ **$5,109,720**; at 6%/yr → ≈ **$6,837,950 by 2050** (≈ $273,520/yr at a 4% withdrawal assumption).
- Living in the house saves ≈ **$37,410/yr** in property tax vs. renting it out (2045 projection).

## Recommendation
1. Decide occupancy first: the Prop 19 exclusion requires primary-residence occupancy within 1 year + homeowners' exemption + BOE-19-P within 3 years.
2. If the heir can live in Cupertino → keep & live in it (lowest tax, no taxable sale; SF triplex can be fully rented).
3. If staying in SF → sell & reinvest (≈ tax-free sale due to step-up; invested proceeds likely outperform the rental option after the ≈ $72,580/yr reassessed tax, unless verified Cupertino market rent is exceptionally high — rent is UNVERIFIED/flagged).
4. Renting the Cupertino house is tax-disadvantaged (full reassessment) — only if verified rent comfortably exceeds the reassessed tax plus insurance/maintenance/vacancy/management.
5. Estate tax: 2045 projected federal exemption ≈ $26,302,590 (assumption); two houses ≈ $8,592,490 projected 2045 ($5,435,870 Cupertino + $3,156,620 SF) — below the single-person exemption, but the cash portion (internal-only) must be added. CA: no estate/inheritance tax.
