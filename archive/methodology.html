Methodology

How every modelled number was calculated, what was assumed, and what was deliberately not modelled. If you cannot reproduce a number from this page + the cited official inputs, treat it as an error and flag it.

Rounding & labels

  • Dollars: rounded to nearest $10 everywhere (e.g., $550,540 → $550,540; $1,044,586 → $1,044,590). Rates/percentages shown as published (e.g., 1.18162182%). Totals may differ by $10 from the sum of rounded components.
  • Labels: Official = direct from government source (link in row). Modelled = calculated from stated assumptions + official inputs (formula below). User-provided = supplied in brief, reproduced as given. Flagged = blank / needs review.
  • Privacy: placeholders only ([DECEDENT], [BENEFICIARY], [TRUSTEE], [TRUST NAME], [APN REDACTED]). No names, street addresses, APNs, or account numbers.

What was not modelled (and why)

  • Estate-tax bill: not computed — cash amount blank (F-01). Only thresholds shown.
  • Cupertino rent, SF maintenance/vacancy actuals: blank (F-05/F-09). Illustrative % shown only as sensitivity.
  • House-price forecast: scenarios only. No official forecast published.
  • Insurance ZIP quote: planning midpoint only (F-11). Replace with bound quote.
  • Future law: modelled as enacted today. “Fix Prop 19” repeal not modelled (F-08).
  • Income tax on rent / reinvestment: excluded from cash-flow tables except where noted. Add CPA computation for a full after-tax model.

Reproducibility

All modelled tables can be reproduced with a hand calculator from the formulas above + the official inputs linked in each row. Chart data on the Projections page plots the same rounded table values.
