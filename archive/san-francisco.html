Why SF matters for the Cupertino decision

  • If you live in Cupertino, SF rent ($85,800/yr gross) can cover Cupertino’s post-Prop 19 tax (≈ $27,450/yr) + insurance + water with room to spare — before maintenance/vacancy/tax.
  • If you rent Cupertino instead, you pay Cupertino tax and housing cost elsewhere, while SF rent must cover both — the math tightens sharply.
  • If you sell Cupertino, SF rent continues, but you must fund housing from reinvestment returns — sequence risk (market drop early) matters.
