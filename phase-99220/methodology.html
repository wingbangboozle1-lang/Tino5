Method notes

  • Break-evens are the input values at which keep and sell are equal. They are computed by bisection in the cbe model.
  • The economic basis adds the rent avoided by living in the home. The cash basis does not.
  • The replay is monthly. Phased strategies hold the parked money at the cash rate and buy equal parts; the lump sum buys all at the peak.
  • All money is rounded to the nearest $10. Percentages are shown to two decimal places.
