How to read the mixed rows

  • Live then sell and rent then sell use the model's own year-by-year cash rows for the years before the sale, then sell at that year's modelled value less 6.0% selling costs, and grow the proceeds at the net return.
  • Park then phase in is the mechanics the brief asked for: the proceeds sit in 3-month Treasury bills at 4.32% for the parking period, then are bought in equal monthly parts over 12 months (the 12-month buying period is an assumption). It has no market crash. The crash-entry test is on the replay page.
  • Choosing a mixed path does not change the Proposition 19 rule: moving out and renting makes the house reassessable. Check the timing on the timeline.
