Deadlines as a timeline

Dates run from the day of death, not the day of distribution. Dates that the official pages confirm are shown as confirmed; anything not yet read is flagged. This page is not legal advice; check each date with the trust attorney and the CPA.

  1. Day 0Date of death. For property held in a trust, the date of death is the change in ownership for tax purposes, even if the deed to the heir is recorded later.S7 State Board of Equalization, Taxpayers' Rights Advocate Office
  2. Within 90 daysIf the home is held in an LLC, partnership or corporation, file BOE-100-B with the State Board of Equalization.S7 State Board of Equalization, Taxpayers' Rights Advocate Office
    S8 State Board of Equalization, Legal Entity Ownership Program
  3. Within 150 daysFile BOE-502-D (Change in Ownership Statement, Death of a Real Property Owner) with the County Assessor. The trustee files it for trust property. If probate applies, it is due before or at the inventory filing instead.S7 State Board of Equalization, Taxpayers' Rights Advocate Office
  4. Within 9 months (if Form 706 is required)Form 706 due date for estates above the filing threshold. The 2026 threshold is $15,000,000. Due-date wording is not yet confirmed from the official instructions.S5 Internal Revenue Service
    S6 Internal Revenue Service
    Flag F04: due-date text not yet read
  5. 6 months after the 706 due dateAutomatic extension to file (Form 4768). The extension does not extend time to pay. Not yet confirmed from an official page.Flag F04
  6. Within 1 year of the change in ownershipFor the parent-child exclusion (Proposition 19), the home must become the heir's principal residence within one year of the change in ownership. Claim form BOE-19-P goes to the County Assessor.S7 State Board of Equalization, Taxpayers' Rights Advocate Office
  7. Claim filing deadline (BOE-19-P)Not confirmed from an official page in this session. Do not rely on any date until verified.Flag F05
  8. 10 DecemberDelinquency date for the first property tax installment. A 10% penalty applies after it.S7 State Board of Equalization, Taxpayers' Rights Advocate Office
  9. 10 AprilDelinquency date for the second property tax installment. A 10% penalty applies after it.S7 State Board of Equalization, Taxpayers' Rights Advocate Office
