California State Board of Equalization
LTA 2026/026 — Proposition 19 intergenerational transfer exclusion FAQ		Definitions: the excluded amount is the factored base year value immediately before transfer plus the adjusted one-million-dollar amount; the excess amount is full cash value at transfer minus the excluded amount; the new taxable value is the factored base year value plus the excess. Scope. A decision aid for one household question: when the inherited house arrives, should the heir live in it, let it out, or sell it and invest? Every figure is a model output from a round modelling base — not either property's value — rounded to $10. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published and marked. Nothing here is legal, tax or investment advice.

Privacy. No names, streets, parcel numbers, postcodes, cities or neighbourhoods appear on this site, and no figure from the household's own records is published at any precision. The conclusions are published as break-evens, rankings and rates, which move only slightly across a tenfold range of property values — the small movement is caused by a fixed statutory cap and is tested and stated on the method page.
