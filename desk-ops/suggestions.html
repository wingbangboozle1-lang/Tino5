Suggestions, for review before anything is implemented

The owner asked for a section where suggestions can be reviewed and the best path chosen before any change is made. Nothing on this page has been implemented. Each item carries what it would cost, what it would change, and the measure that would show whether it worked.

How to use this page. Read the "what it changes" column first. An item that does not change a break-even, an exposure or a deadline is decoration, and there are three of those at the bottom marked as such. The rest are ordered by how much of the decision they unlock per unit of effort.

What this desk would do first, if it were the household

  1. Two measurements. A date-of-death appraisal and three rental comparables. These are the only items that convert an assumption into evidence.
  2. One written question to the county. Rate area, roll value, and the claims it wants. Free, and it closes F01 and half of Q03.
  3. One conversation with the attorney, on the briefing sheet. The trust's terms decide whether the exclusion is available at all.
  4. One written page from the household. What would make them choose each path, written before the numbers are final. The owner asked for this and it is the single highest-value item on the page: without it, the projection picks the answer.
Nothing on this page is a change to the site. The site you are reading is the proposal. Each item above becomes a change only after it is chosen, and the chosen ones are recorded with a date on the requirement register.

Scope. A decision aid for one household question: when the inherited house arrives, should the heir live in it, let it out, or sell it and invest? Every figure is a model output from a round modelling base — not either property's value — rounded to $10. Statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published and marked. Nothing here is legal, tax or investment advice.

Privacy. No names, streets, parcel numbers, postcodes, cities or neighbourhoods appear on this site, and no figure from the household's own records is published at any precision. The conclusions are published as break-evens, rankings and rates, which move only slightly across a tenfold range of property values — the small movement is caused by a fixed statutory cap and is tested and stated on the method page.
