Costs and tax

Inheritance tax, property tax and carrying costs

Two different tax questions. The tax on inheriting is almost certainly zero in California; the tax on owning the house afterwards is not, because the Proposition 13 base is broken by the transfer. Most of the money in this decision sits in the second question.

2. The headroom calculation, and why it must be re-run

Headroom is the federal exclusion minus the gross estate. The gross estate is not the same as the value of A: it is everything the transferor owns. We know A's value and we do not know the rest, so the table below is an upper bound on the headroom, not a figure to plan against.
