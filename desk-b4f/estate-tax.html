Two High-Dollar Estate Traps Flagged for Trust Counsel:
1. Non-Grantor Trust Tax Compression (37% at $16,500): After the Settlor dies, an irrevocable administrative trust pays the 37% ordinary + 20% LTCG + 3.8% NIIT + 13.3% CA rate on retained income above just $16,500–$16,750 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35). Distribute Property A or income cleanly to the Beneficiary rather than accumulating income inside the post-death trust.
2. DSUE Portability & Property B Boundary: Property B is already owned by the Beneficiary and sits outside the Parent/Trust's taxable estate — however, once inherited, A + liquid corpus + B all enter the Beneficiary's eventual estate. If a predeceased parent died within the last 5 years without filing Form 706, check Rev. Proc. 2022-32 (5-year late portability window) immediately!
