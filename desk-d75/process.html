Trust process, step by step

Death to recorded title, with the deadline that attaches to each step and the authority behind it.

stepdeadlinewhat happenswho does itauthority

9Within one year of the transferfile for the homeowners' exemption on the heir's own claim - this is a separate filing from the exclusion claim and it is the condition for itheirsection 63.2(a)(1)(B)
10Within three years, and before any sale or any end of occupancyfile the Proposition 19 claim with the assessor. A late claim is considered but only from the lien date of the year it is filed, so the years you lose are not recoveredheirsection 63.2(f)
11Same windowrecord the trustee's deed transferring title, and pay the recording charges; a transfer by trust is normally outside the documentary transfer taxtrustee and title companycounty clerk-recorder
12Next rollthe assessor enrols the value: either the factored base plus the excess over the adjusted amount, or market value with no exclusionassessorBOE LTA 2026/026
13Ongoingkeep the exemption alive: the exclusion is removed in the year the heir stops qualifying, and the base that would have applied at transfer is then enrolledheirsection 63.2(a)(1)(C)
Two of these deadlines are the whole game. Steps 8 and 9 - live there within a year, and claim the exemption within a year - decide whether a seven-figure tax base survives. Step 10 files the claim, and its three-year window closes early if the house is sold or stops being occupied, which is the trap in the sequence: a plan to let it first and occupy later can forfeit both.
