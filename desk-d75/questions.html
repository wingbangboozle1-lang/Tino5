The two that matter most. Q04 (how large the cash side of the estate is) decides whether 40% of anything is payable, which outweighs every operating line in this desk put together. Q05 (what the tax bills actually contain) decides whether the cost base of the already-owned house - the engine funding everything else - is right or roughly double. Everything else refines.

Order of work

  1. Read the trust instrument and pull the recorded deed (closes Q01, Q02, Q03, Q17).
  2. Photograph both current tax bills, all lines, and both assessor notices (closes Q05, Q06, Q07).
  3. Get the two insurance declarations pages and the utility account's interconnection letter (closes Q08, Q09).
  4. Ask the trustee in writing what the instrument requires on distribution (closes Q14 and the sale-authority question).
  5. Commission a written rent analysis for the inherited house and a rent roll for the other (closes Q10, Q12, Q13).
  6. Only then re-run the decision page with the replaced numbers; the build takes seconds.
