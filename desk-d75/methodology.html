Rounding and privacy

Every household amount is rounded to the nearest $10, so that no figure on this site can be matched to a bill, an assessment or a listing by searching. Statutory and published amounts are quoted exactly and marked exact, because rounding a legal threshold would misstate it. No name, address, parcel or account number, postcode, city, county or neighbourhood appears; the two houses are referred to by their role. Your year-by-year assessment and tax history is not published at any rounding: a rounded series still identifies one parcel in the open roll, so it is verified here and then left out. The verifier that enforces all of this is part of the build.

The arithmetic, in words

  • Market value. Value in year y = owner's 2026 value grown at D1, or a replayed sequence in the black-swan regimes. Today: $3,100,000.
  • Enrolled base with no transfer. Assessed value grown at the Proposition 13 cap of 2%: $550,540 in 2026 to $1,481,830 in 2076.
  • Base after a transfer where the heir occupies. max(factored base, market value at transfer minus the adjusted amount for that year), grown at 2% after. At a 2045 transfer that is $4,206,640.
  • Base after a transfer with no exclusion. Market value at the transfer, grown at 2% after.
  • Property tax. (enrolled base − homeowners' exemption if owner-occupied) times the rate, plus the residual other charges on the bill grown at D5. The residual is not invented: it is your bill minus the rate times your assessed value, which comes to $1,090 for the inherited house and $1,090 for the other.
  • Carrying cost. insurance, water and sewer, net electricity (until the tariff ends), and maintenance as a share of value.
  • Rental path. gross rent at the yield assumption, less vacancy, management, tax, insurance and maintenance; depreciation on 75% of the transfer value over 27.5 years shelters income, and is recaptured at up to 25% on the sale, which is why letting is punished at the end.
  • Sale. market value at the sale year, less 6%, less capital gains on the increase since the stepped-up basis, with the principal-residence exclusion applied when the heir actually lived there.
  • Everything invested at the after-tax return until 2076, then compared. Real figures are the nominal total divided by cumulative inflation at D5.

How to re-run this with better numbers

python3 desk-d75/_src/build.py     # regenerates every page from the registers
python3 desk-d75/_src/verify.py    # the contract: rounding, sources, links, privacy
python3 desk-d75/_src/build.py --check   # fail if the committed pages are stale

To change an input, edit the constant or the row in _src/data.py and rebuild. Nothing is hard-coded in the prose, which is what makes the desk safe to re-run when the documents arrive.
