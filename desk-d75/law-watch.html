The basis adjustment at death, Internal Revenue Code section 1014unchanged by the 2025 actthe act that raised and indexed the exclusion did not amend section 1014: the rumour that it repealed the step-up in basis is not correct, and this desk states that explicitly because it was the most repeated claim of the 2025 debate. Bills that would end or limit the adjustment have been introduced in past Congresses and none has received a hearing. Gifts keep the donor's basis under section 1015, and where community property is involved both halves adjust under section 1014(b)(6).the sell path's tax-free exit rests on this.
