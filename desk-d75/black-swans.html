What survives every regime in that table. Sell it at transfer, reinvest the money finishes first in 6 of 8, Rent it out, live at the other house finishes first in 1 of 8, Live in it ten years, then sell finishes first in 1 of 8. The direction of the answer is robust to history being replayed at us; the size of it is not, with the gap between living in the house and selling it swinging by $44,722,430 between the deflation decade and the rate shock. Protect the exclusion, which is worth about the same in every one of these worlds, and leave the sale to a year you choose rather than one you are given.
