How to read the gaps

The four open items are not failures of research. Two are private account documents (insurance declarations, utility statements), one is a bill line item, and one is the trust instrument. Each has a named discovery action on the questions page and a row on Flagged for review.

A decision aid built from public law and the owner's own figures, not tax, legal or investment advice. Every table row links the document it came from, or says plainly that no official source supports it. Where a value could not be confirmed the cell is blank and the item is listed on Flagged for review.
