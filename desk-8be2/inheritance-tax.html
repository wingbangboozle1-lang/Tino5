Federal estate tax

There is no federal inheritance tax on the heir; the estate tax (if any) is paid by the estate before distribution.

California

California imposes no state estate tax and no inheritance tax. Inherited assets are not taxable income to the heir at the state level. Rent from inherited property and gain on a later sale are taxed (gain measured against the stepped-up basis).

Trust nuance

A transfer through an inter vivos or testamentary trust can qualify for the Prop 19 parent-child exclusion (R&TC 63.2(e)(9)); an interest in a legal entity does not (63.2(e)(8)); and a claim under the section is not a public document (63.2(c)). Whether a particular trust qualifies must be confirmed with an attorney — this desk states the rule, not a conclusion about your trust.

Inheritance allowed before tax. For a 2026 death, roughly the first $15,000,000 per person is shielded from federal estate tax (amounts above are taxed at graduated federal rates up to 40%). California adds nothing. If the estate (including Property A, Property B and the considerable cash inheritance described by the owner) approaches or exceeds the exclusion, the estate should file Form 706 and consider portability — see an estate attorney and CPA.
