Method

  • Rounding: all money rounded to the nearest $10 unless it is a statutory amount quoted verbatim and marked.
  • Sourcing: every table row carries an official source link or an explicit “no official source — unverified” marker; blank values mean no source was found.
  • Privacy: no names, addresses, parcel details, ZIP codes or city/neighbourhood names. Year-by-year owner tax/assessment history is not published. Owner inputs are labelled unverified.
  • Projections: illustrative only; constant growth rates are assumptions, not forecasts. Uneven real histories are in Black swan.
  • Mixed paths: the desk models pure options and mixed combinations; results depend on unknowable timing.
