What would change the answer: (1) proceeds return vs ~5.20% break-even; (2) transfer year; (3) house-price growth vs ~5.58% break-even; (4) Prop 19 occupancy within 1 year; (5) verified market rent for Property A (avoided-rent yield break-even ~4.68%).

Owner actions before the money arrives: written investment policy for sale proceeds; written valuation of Property A at date of death; confirm Prop 19 occupancy intent; estate liquidity plan if estate > $15,000,000; portability and prior taxable gifts; §1031 decision before selling.
