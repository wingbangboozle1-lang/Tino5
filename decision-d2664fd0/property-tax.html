Property tax & assessment

California property tax under Prop 13 and Prop 19. Assessed values and tax bills for the owner's parcels are not published on this desk — a rounded series still matches one parcel against open assessor rolls. Statutory rates and rules are quoted exactly as published.

RuleWhat it saysSource
