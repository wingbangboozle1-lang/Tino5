Suggestions to discover missing information. (1) Engage a licensed appraiser for written valuations of both properties at the relevant dates. (2) Obtain insurance quotes from multiple carriers for both primary-residence and rental policies. (3) Request rate schedules from the water and electric providers (general schedules only; ZIP-specific bills are not public). (4) Review lease copies and rent rolls for Property B. (5) Confirm Prop 19 eligibility and BOE-19-P deadlines with the county assessor. (6) Consult an estate-planning attorney and CPA before any sale or transfer.
