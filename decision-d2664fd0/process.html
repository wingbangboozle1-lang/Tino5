Step-by-step trust process

From death to recorded title for Property A (held in a trust, expected transfer 2040–2050). Property B is already owned and is not part of this process. Deadlines are shown as a timeline on the next page.

  1. Confirm the trust and the trustee. Verify the trust document names the successor trustee and identifies Property A. No personal identifiers are recorded on this desk.
  2. Obtain a written valuation of Property A at the date of death. This sets the stepped-up basis (§1014) and the Prop 19 factored base year value. [question Q14] · [flag F04]
  3. File Form 706 (federal estate tax) if required. Due 9 months after date of death (6-month extension available). Only needed if the estate exceeds the $15,000,000 per-person exclusion. [IRS Form 706] · Inheritance & estate tax
  4. File Prop 19 claim (BOE-19-P) with the county assessor. Due within 3 years of transfer or before sale (whichever first). The heir must occupy as primary residence within 1 year to keep the capped reassessment. [BOE Prop 19] · Property tax & assessment
  5. File homeowner's exemption (BOE-266) if occupying as primary residence. Provides the $7,000 assessed-value reduction. unverified: confirm with county assessor
  6. Record the transfer of title. The trustee conveys title to the heir; record with the county recorder. Transfer tax and recording fees are priced into the sell path [flag F10].
  7. Decide the path: live in, rent out, or sell. Record the decision and the date next to the item so a future reader knows what was chosen and why. Scenarios · Mixed paths
  8. Write an investment policy for sale proceeds before the money arrives. If selling, decide the phased-investing approach (Treasuries 24–36 months, then equities) in advance. Black swan & phased investing · [question Q10]
  9. Prepare an estate liquidity plan if the estate may exceed the federal exclusion. Inheritance & estate tax · [question Q19]
  10. Ask now about portability and prior taxable gifts. [question Q20]
Prop 19 eligibility. Claim the Proposition 19 exclusion only if the heir will occupy the home long term (move in within one year and make it a principal residence). If the heir will not occupy, the property is fully reassessed to market value — do not claim the exclusion. [BOE Prop 19] · [question Q13]
Like-kind exchange. Decide whether a §1031 like-kind exchange is wanted, and take advice before selling. §1031 applies to real property held for investment or business, with strict identification and exchange timelines. unverified: CPA/attorney to confirm · [question Q22]
