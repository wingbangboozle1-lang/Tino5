Key distinction

Property A is inherited; Property B is already owned. Property A receives a step-up in basis at death and may qualify for Prop 19's parent-child exclusion (if the heir occupies within one year). Property B is outside the inheritance entirely — its sale would not trigger a step-up and its retention does not depend on Prop 19 occupancy. This distinction drives the entire analysis.
