# Integration audit — 11 November 2026

**Status: needs review; not a publication sign-off.** This is a targeted audit, not a line-by-line verification of the repository. It deliberately omits the household's supplied figures and other identifying details.

## Release blockers

1. **Privacy:** the GitHub API reports this repository is public and GitHub Pages publishes the root of `main`. Existing site pages and tracked source files combine location, property characteristics, and household-specific financial data. Names and street addresses are not necessary for re-identification; rounding to $10 and using aliases does not guarantee anonymity. The root `README.md` claim that no personal data is stored is therefore not a reliable privacy assurance. Historical Git commits may remain accessible even after a normal deletion. An integration release must inventory *all* deployed paths, generated assets, source files and public history; moving details off the landing page or adding `noindex` does not secure them. Privacy should be addressed before claiming the requested privacy standard is met. [GitHub: removing sensitive data](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/keeping-your-account-and-data-secure/removing-sensitive-data-from-a-repository).
2. **Unsupported certainty:** the root landing page says estate tax will very likely never apply, although the total taxable estate, lifetime taxable gifts, deductions and other inputs are not established. A threshold alone cannot establish the likelihood or amount of tax. The IRS says the basic exclusion for 2026 is $15,000,000 and the filing calculation includes adjusted taxable gifts; portability and deductions may matter. [IRS Form 706 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706).
3. **Assessment is not income-tax basis:** the root landing page computes a supposed erased gain from a property-tax assessment. An assessed value is not proof of adjusted income-tax basis; improvements, ownership interests and trust terms matter. Inherited property basis is generally fair market value at death or, where properly elected, the alternate valuation date, subject to exceptions. A trust transfer does not by itself establish a full basis adjustment. [IRS inherited-property FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/faqs/interest-dividends-other-types-of-income/gifts-inheritances/gifts-inheritances), [IRS Publication 551](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p551).
4. **Prop 19 overstatement:** statements that moving in simply keeps the old assessment overlook the value limit, the transferor's qualifying principal-residence requirement and the applicable filing requirements. The exclusion can be partial; the value above the indexed limit is added to taxable value. The transferee must occupy within a year, and filing timing affects when relief applies. [California BOE fact sheet](https://www.boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub801.pdf).
5. **Verification coverage:** `node scripts/verify.mjs` reported 209 rows, zero errors and 53 warnings; `python3 scripts/verify.py` reported 3,633 checks and passed. These are useful structural tests, but neither result demonstrates that every claim in every published version was independently checked against an official source, that assumptions are verified facts, or that a privacy threat assessment was passed. The exposed examples above are sufficient to show a release-wide sign-off would be premature.

## Implications for a corrected analysis

- Treat the inherited house and the already-owned home differently: a transfer of the first does not itself mean the second is inherited or automatically reassessed. Do not presume a sale of either property.
- Show results as conditional model outputs, not verified future facts. Official links can establish a rule or a historical series, but cannot verify a private utility bill, insurance premium, market valuation, future rent or future return. Keep unverified official-value cells blank and flag them; do not publish private household inputs or input-derived fingerprints.
- Compare sell, rent and occupy using the same horizon, tax timing and cash-flow conventions. Explain the effect of the existing residence, tenancy restrictions, vacancy, repairs, insurance, transaction costs and tax basis; test adverse sequences as well as smooth growth. A reliable winner cannot be asserted without resolving material inputs and trust eligibility.
- Inheritance-law future thresholds for the proposed transfer window are not enacted dollar amounts. For planning, explicitly label inflation scenarios and distinguish an estate's potential federal liability from a beneficiary's own subsequent income or capital-gains tax. Amount remaining before the threshold is unknown without the estate and gift information.
- No public source can establish an individual premium from a ZIP code alone. Insurance and utility assumptions need private quotes and bills; solar bill credits depend on the actual tariff and true-up, not a guaranteed cash profit. The California Department of Insurance offers consumer guidance: https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/95-guides/03-res/.
