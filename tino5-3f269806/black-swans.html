Black-swan economics

Stress cases for 50 years: stagflation, slump, deflation, insurance shock and rule change. Use them to test the decision, not to predict it.

Why a 50-year plan needs black-swan cases

Inflation, home prices, rents, insurance and interest rates do not move in a straight line. The Fed targets 2% inflation (S14), but actual inflation has run far above target for stretches. The BLS table shows that the December-to-December change in consumer prices was above 8% in five of the nine years from 1973 to 1981 (S15).
