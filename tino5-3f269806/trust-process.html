Trust inheritance steps

The inheritance of Property A, step by step, with the official rule behind each step.

How the trust inheritance of Property A would run

Steps are in the order they usually occur. Each row shows the official source and whether it was verified.

StepActionWhat to doStatusSource (official link)
1Date of deathChange in ownership occurs on the date of death, even if distribution to the heir is later. Reassessment is measured from that date.VerifiedS10
2Within 150 daysThe trustee (or transferee) files BOE-502-D with the county assessor for each parcel.VerifiedS10
3Claim the parent-child exclusionFile BOE-19-P if the heir qualifies. Occupancy must start within one year of the death. Claim within three years or before any transfer to a third party.VerifiedS08, S10
4Homeowners' exemptionIf the heir occupies the home as a principal residence, file for the $7,000 homeowners' exemption within one year of transfer.VerifiedS08, S11
5Federal estate tax checkForm 706 is needed only if the gross estate exceeds $15,000,000 in 2026 (S02). Estimate the total estate before the filing deadline.VerifiedS02
6Mailing address and billsAsk the assessor to update the tax-bill address. Santa Clara: first installment due 1 Nov, delinquent after 10 Dec; second due 1 Feb, delinquent after 10 Apr (S12).VerifiedS10, S12
7Supplemental billA reassessment after transfer can produce a supplemental bill. Budget for it (S12, S13).VerifiedS12, S13
8Decide live, rent or sellLiving keeps the exclusion; renting loses it; selling triggers the sale analysis (basis is value at death, S05).VerifiedS08, S05
9Trust income taxThe trust may owe income tax on income it keeps. Brackets are compressed: 37% above $16,000 of taxable income in 2026 (S03).VerifiedS03
10Insurance and utilitiesMove insurance and utilities into the heir's name. Insurance premium, water provider and solar terms are not verified; get quotes (S17, S16).Unverified - blankS17, S16

Deadlines are as stated by the BOE and IRS. The 'unverified' step covers items not checked against an official source. This is a checklist, not legal advice.
