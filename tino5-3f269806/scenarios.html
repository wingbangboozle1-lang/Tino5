Live, rent or sell

The three paths for Property A, compared at transfers in 2040, 2045 and 2050. Every figure is derived from labelled inputs.

Best path on current evidence: renting Property A is clearly the weakest path, because it forfeits the parent-child exclusion. Between living and selling, the answer turns on comparable rent and the return you can earn on the proceeds. Both are unknown today.

What to do with the answer

  • Without a comparable-rent figure, the base case (4% after-tax return) favours selling over living in A, because the carry is larger than the return on the proceeds.
  • If comparable housing rents for more than the 2040 threshold, living in A is better. Test with the grid above.
  • Do not rent Property A out without the numbers in path C.
