What would change the answer

  • Repeal of step-up in basis: would add capital-gains tax to sales of inherited property. Basis of both properties is unknown, so it cannot be sized (Q19).
  • Narrowing of Prop 19: would remove the reason to live in A for tax reasons. Verified no bill found.
  • Lower federal exclusion: does not reach a known estate at owner values; matters only if the decedent holds more assets.
