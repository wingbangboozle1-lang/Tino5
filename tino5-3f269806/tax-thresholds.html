Tax and inheritance thresholds

Federal, California and local thresholds with the headroom each one leaves.

Threshold: how much can pass tax-free

The federal estate tax falls on the decedent's estate, not on the heir. The threshold is the decedent's total taxable estate plus lifetime taxable gifts, against the exclusion for the year of death. California has no estate or inheritance tax. Heirs owe no federal inheritance tax.

Local property tax on inheritance (Santa Clara County and San Francisco)

The main local cost of inheriting is property tax. Under Prop 13 the base resets on transfer. For a transfer to a child, the parent-child exclusion keeps taxable value to the base plus the add-on, but only if the child occupies the home. Without it, the home is taxed at market value. The projections and scenarios show both cases.
