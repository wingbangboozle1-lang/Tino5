Reading the table

  • Property A market value grows at the base assumption (2% a year), so market value roughly doubles about every 35 years.
  • Without a transfer, A's assessed value grows only at the lesser of CPI or 2%, so its tax grows slowly. A transfer resets it.
  • If the heir lives in A, the taxable value is the base plus the add-on, which is far below market but well above today's assessed value.
  • If the heir rents A, the taxable value is the market value, which is several times higher.
  • Property B's net operating income uses owner inputs for rent, utilities and insurance, and assumptions for vacancy and upkeep. It excludes the owner-occupied unit's imputed rent.
