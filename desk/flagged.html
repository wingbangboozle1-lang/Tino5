Flagged for review

Every unverified claim and every model-output figure, with what would close it.

This page is required reading, not an appendix. Every item below is either a claim this desk could not confirm against an official source, or a figure that is model output rather than a sourced fact. They are published rather than hidden, and none of them blocks publication. Each names what would close it.

Codes beginning F are factual gaps in the owner-supplied data. T are procedural questions. M are model-output figures. X are scope, privacy, and rounding statements.

Every open item as of the date at the top of the page. Rows with an official link are still listed where the figure derived from that source is a calculation rather than a quotation.
