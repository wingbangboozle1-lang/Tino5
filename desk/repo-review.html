Line-by-line verification method

Every figure in this desk was traced to a primary source. Where a primary source could not be reached, the figure was left blank and flagged rather than filled from a secondary source.

The pattern is worth noticing. Every statutory, regulatory, and market figure is verified. Every figure that describes these two specific houses, other than their tax bills, is an owner estimate. That is not a failure of research. It is the boundary of what an outside analysis can know, and it is exactly why the questions page exists.

This desk is a research aid. It is not legal, tax, investment, or appraisal advice. Every figure is either cited to an official source, or labelled as model output, or left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Amounts are rounded to the nearest $10 so that no figure can be matched back to a specific bill.
