The five questions that would change the recommendation

  1. Is the house in the trust? If not, the route is probate, and the statutory fees are roughly 6% of value before any tax.
  2. Can it be legally split into more than one unit? This is the only lever that makes the rental path competitive, because the break-even rent is a total, not a per-unit figure.
  3. What is the actual market rent? A rent within about 15% of the break-even number would make the two paths close enough to decide on other grounds.
  4. Will the heir live in it? Not a modelling question but a life question, and it decides the largest tax item in the analysis.
  5. How large is the rest of the estate? A portfolio large enough to approach the federal threshold changes the estate-tax answer from "none" to "this needs an appointment".
