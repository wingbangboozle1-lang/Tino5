Deliberately not used

  • Commentary sites and news articles. Several were consulted while looking for leads. None is cited for a figure, because none is an authority.
  • Listing sites. These show asking prices, not values, and they are not a defensible basis for an appraisal or a rent.
  • Secondary summaries of statutes. They are frequently out of date. Every statutory statement here is cited to the legislature's own text.

Source policy

RuleWhy
Statutes are cited to the legislature's own text Secondary summaries drift out of date within a session
Thresholds are cited to the agency that administers them An amount can be statutory but indexed, and only the agency publishes the year's figure
Market data is cited to the producer, not a republication Republications drop footnotes and lag revisions
Where a figure could not be confirmed, it is blank and flagged The alternative is a number that looks sourced and is not

Links that moved during the research

Several official pages that existed earlier in this project have since been retired, and each was replaced with a live equivalent before being cited. Where a page had moved, the replacement is what appears above. The ones that failed outright are listed on Repo review so that a later pass does not chase them again.

This desk is a research aid. It is not legal, tax, investment, or appraisal advice. Every figure is either cited to an official source, or labelled as model output, or left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Amounts are rounded to the nearest $10 so that no figure can be matched back to a specific bill.
