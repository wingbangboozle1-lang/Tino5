T1 The "1% tax above one million dollars" in the owner's brief is a California income-tax surcharge on California taxable income above $1,000,000. The official 2025 booklet renamed the Mental Health Services Tax to the Behavioral Health Services Tax and points to Form 540 line 62. It is not an estate, inheritance or property tax, and it does not add a separate rate for capital gains. name and line verified; rate and threshold: from secondary guides FTB 2025 Form 540 booklet (opened; the renaming and line 62 are in the "What's New" section). The 1% rate and $1,000,000 threshold were read from secondary guides only; the line 62 instruction was not opened. Open the line 62 instruction and confirm the rate and threshold. Until then the sensitivity should carry it as a surcharge on income above the threshold, not as an estate-tax item. Capital gains are taxed as ordinary income in California (secondary guides; FTB capital-gains page not opened).
T2 California has no state estate tax and no inheritance tax. The last state estate-tax return requirement ended with deaths on or after 2005. secondary California State Controller's Office, home page (opened 2026-11-19; the estate-tax page was not opened). Secondary guides cite the Controller, e.g. a probate-lending guide. Confirm on the Controller's estate-tax page before relying on it.
T3 Federal estate-tax filing threshold for a death in 2026: $15,000,000 per person. This is the basic exclusion amount. verified IRS, Estate tax: filing threshold table, 2026 row (opened) Use $15,000,000 for 2026 deaths. Later years and any change in law need a new check (the law-watch page tracks this).
