# Private inheritance planning dashboard

A privacy-first, static GitHub Pages decision-support site for a future California property inheritance. It uses public official sources for law and process, distinguishes law from projections, and does not publish user-supplied property values, addresses, rent, bills, or other identifying financial details.

## Important limitations

- The public site intentionally calls the homes **Property A** and **Property B** and omits city, ZIP, parcel, property values, rent, utility bills, and household details.
- Property-specific estimates supplied by the user are not independently verified against assessor records, bills, leases, inspections, or insurance quotes. They are not published and are listed as unverified categories on the review page.
- No market-value, operating-cost, property-tax-dollar, or investment-return projection is filled with a fabricated or unverified number. 2040–2050 law and values cannot be certified from 2026 official sources.
- Legal and tax rules are summarized, not individualized legal, tax, insurance, or investment advice. Recheck all rules at the actual transfer date.

## Site map

- `index.html` — executive summary and decision dashboard
- `pages/property-tax.html` — California assessment rules and Prop 19
- `pages/estate-tax.html` — federal and California transfer-tax rules
- `pages/decision.html` — live in, rent, or sell comparison
- `pages/inheritance-process.html` — inheritance / trust administration checklist
- `pages/flagged-review.html` — unverified inputs and open questions

The source of each factual table row is linked directly to an official government source. Costs, when a public official amount is relevant, are shown rounded to the nearest $10; the linked source provides the exact published amount. No personal data or third-party analytics are used.
