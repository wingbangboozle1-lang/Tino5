Bottom line: At a ~$3,100,000 Property A value the estate is well below the $15,000,000 per-person federal exclusion, so no federal estate tax is expected on the house alone (unless the "considerable amount of money" in the inheritance pushes the total estate above $15,000,000). California adds no estate or inheritance tax. The main tax lever is the stepped-up basis (minimizes gain if sold soon after death) and, if the heir keeps and later sells, §121 after living in the home 2 of 5 years.
