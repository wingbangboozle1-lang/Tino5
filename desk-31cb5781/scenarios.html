Reinvestment risk (beside the headline)

Selling converts property risk into market risk. If the heir sells at a market peak and invests everything into equities right before a 1929- or 2008-style drawdown, the reinvested portfolio can fall sharply while a kept house would not show a marked-to-market loss. The phased-investing path (Black Swan page) addresses this: hold sale proceeds in 3-month to 2-year Treasuries for 24–36 months, then phase into stocks so a single bad entry year does not determine the outcome.
