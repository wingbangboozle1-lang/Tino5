Open questions for the adviser

  1. Does the total inheritance (house + "considerable amount of money") exceed the $15,000,000 federal exclusion?
  2. Will the heir occupy Property A within 1 year of transfer (Prop 19 eligibility)?
  3. What is the parent's factored base year value of Property A? (county assessor / latest tax bill)
  4. What are the binding homeowner (A) and landlord DP-3 (B) insurance quotes?
  5. Recommended path: sell + phased investing, rent, occupy, or mixed?

Recommended next steps

  • Order date-of-death appraisal at transfer (sets stepped-up basis).
  • File BOE-19-P (within 3 years) and confirm occupancy within 1 year if keeping the low base.
  • If selling, use phased-investing path: Treasuries 24–36 months, then phase into equities.
  • Re-check Prop 19 allowance and federal exclusion before any 2040–2050 transfer.
