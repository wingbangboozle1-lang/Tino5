Property tax projection — Property A (baseline: full reassessment at transfer; $10 rounding)

Assumes the heir does not qualify for the Prop 19 exclusion (full reassessment to market at transfer). Property tax = 1% of assessed value. Growth assumption: $0 — see range table below.

Property A's current assessed value (parent's base) is owner/private information and is not published. Post-transfer tax under full reassessment ≈ 1% of market value at transfer. Under the Prop 19 cap (if heir moves in), the increase is limited to the allowance above the parent's factored base year value — worth roughly $19,930 in year one on the cbe desk's illustrative numbers, not the whole gap.
