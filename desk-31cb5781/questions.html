Suggestions to discover missing information: (1) Order a date-of-death appraisal when the transfer occurs — it sets stepped-up basis. (2) Request a Prop 19 preliminary assessment from the county assessor before deciding to occupy or sell. (3) Get binding insurance quotes (homeowner + landlord) before budgeting carrying costs. (4) Have a CPA model the estate against the $15,000,000 exclusion and the stepped-up-basis gain on sale. (5) Record the heir's occupancy intent in writing — it is the single most consequential deadline (1 year under Prop 19).
