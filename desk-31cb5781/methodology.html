Methodology & privacy

How this desk is built and how privacy is protected.

Methodology & privacy

  • Sources: every table row links an official/primary source, or is blank and marked unverified/owner-supplied. See Source register.
  • Rounding: money rounded to nearest $10 unless a statutory/agency figure is quoted exactly (tagged exact).
  • Privacy: no names, street addresses, ZIP codes, neighbourhoods, parcel numbers, account numbers, phones or emails. Properties are "Property A" (Santa Clara County) and "Property B" (San Francisco County) at county level only.
  • Model: projections are derived from labelled assumptions (growth 3%–6%; tax at 1% of value for illustration). They are not predictions.
  • Not advice: not tax, legal or investment advice. Re-check the then-current law before acting.
