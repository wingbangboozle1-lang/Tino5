Law watch

A decision made for a 2045 transfer has to survive twenty years of legislation. This page separates what is in force today, what the 3 November 2026 ballot did (unofficial until certification), and what would actually change the answer if it happened.

The certification date matters

First results were published on the night of 3 November 2026 and county counting runs to 11 December 2026. Every "unofficial" line on this page must be re-checked at the Secretary of State's results page after certification. Nothing here is treated as law in the model.
