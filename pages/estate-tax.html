Category 02 · Federal & California transfer taxes

Estate threshold, gift context, and inherited basis

“Estate tax,” “inheritance tax,” income tax on trust earnings, property tax reassessment, and tax on a later sale are different questions. A large inheritance amount alone does not tell you whether federal estate tax is due.

Federal estate-tax basic exclusion$15,000,000 per person for calendar-year 2026 under current federal law; the exclusion amount is inflation-adjusted under current law.This is not a separate per-beneficiary inheritance allowance. Estate size, lifetime adjusted taxable gifts, deductions, ownership, and any deceased-spousal unused exclusion (DSUE) matter. Federal filing threshold may be exceeded even if final tax differs.IRS, What's New—Estate and Gift Tax · IRS estate-tax FAQs
Federal Form 706 filing triggerFor a U.S. citizen or resident decedent, filing is generally required if the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and specific gift-tax exemption is more than the applicable filing threshold for year of death. For 2026, the threshold is $15,000,000.Inventory all assets under applicable inclusion/valuation rules and review prior gifts. Do not compare only the inherited cash or the two homes to the threshold.IRS estate-tax FAQs
Annual gift exclusion (not inheritance limit)$19,000 per donee for present-interest gifts in 2026 (exact official amount; already a $10 increment). This is distinct from the lifetime estate/gift exclusion.Do not use the annual gift exclusion as the maximum amount someone may inherit tax-free. Gifts above the annual exclusion may require reporting and may use lifetime exclusion; a gift is not automatically taxed at the excess amount.IRS gift-tax FAQs, 2026 annual exclusion table
California estate returnCalifornia State Controller guidance says no California Estate Tax Return is required for deaths on or after Jan. 1, 2005. The State Controller page identifies legacy inheritance-tax collections only for deaths before June 8, 1982.This does not eliminate federal estate tax, California income-tax returns, or property-tax reassessment. No separate local inheritance-tax figure is assumed.California State Controller, Estate Tax

San Francisco local transfer-tax watchSan Francisco Election Department’s preliminary Nov. 2026 results show Measure J passed; its stated scope is a transfer tax on certain property transfers to a lender due to or to avoid foreclosure. Threshold is not “tax-free inheritance amount.” The 2026 federal basic exclusion is a lifetime transfer-tax amount; the estate’s filing and any tax depend on gross estate, prior taxable gifts, deductions and available DSUE. No conclusion about the actual estate’s size or tax exposure is posted here.

Tax workpaper needed before deciding to sell

Have the fiduciary and tax professional reconcile the final estate/trust inventory, date-of-death appraisals, recorded ownership and trust terms, prior taxable gifts, any portability election, inherited basis, post-death improvements, depreciation and likely selling costs. Then calculate an estimated after-tax sale result rather than using gross sale proceeds. All these personal financial inputs are intentionally excluded from the public site.
