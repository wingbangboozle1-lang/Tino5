Verification standard applied

  • Legal rules and current thresholds were checked against the linked IRS, California BOE, State Controller, California FTB, local assessor, city and regulator sources.
  • Every factual table row on the public pages includes an official-source link. A link to an official process or tool does not mean a private amount was verified.
  • All private property figures and estimates were excluded instead of repeated. Unknown values are blank, and no purported 2040–2050 numeric forecast was manufactured.
  • No login, analytics, externally hosted font, user submission form, or personal document upload is included.

Flagged means unverified or incomplete—not a finding of error or tax liability. Reconcile privately with official records and qualified advisers.
