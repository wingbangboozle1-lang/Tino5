Method and privacy

  • Source links are primary government sources (IRS, California BOE, county assessors, state regulator / utility agencies and California Courts).
  • Where no official source validates a property-specific number, the value is blank and the item appears on Flagged for review.
  • Information supplied privately about property values, rent, utilities, tax history and insurance estimates is not reproduced on this public website.
  • Every displayed dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10. The Prop 19 allowance is displayed as $1,044,590, rounded from the official $1,044,586.
  • Current source statements are not predictions of future law or outcomes. Consult the relevant authority and licensed professional at the decision date.
