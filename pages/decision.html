DECISION PATHS

Keep the options open until the net numbers are verified.

The optimal choice depends on after-tax, after-maintenance cash flow—not headline property value, gross rent, or an assumed market return. Public pages intentionally omit personal property amounts.

i
Use one comparable period and one consistent tax treatment.

For each path, model annual operating cash flow plus one-time sale proceeds or initial investment, then compare the present value across a common horizon. Leave all unknown inputs blank. Do not treat a gross rent figure as profit.

RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE

Decision gates

  1. 01
    Resolve legal rights and timing.

    Read the executed trust, deeds, amendments, beneficiary terms, and any occupancy or sale restrictions with the trustee/estate attorney.

  2. 02
    Get the tax base, not a guess.

    Obtain death-date valuation, assessor change-in-ownership notice, current tax bill, and a written county determination on any Prop 19 claim.

  3. 03
    Price insurance and major systems.

    Request comparable written quotes for actual use (owner-occupied vs rental/mixed-use), replacement-cost coverage, liability, and exclusions; obtain inspection and repair reserve estimates.

  4. 04
    Build after-tax alternatives.

    Use a CPA/EA to model sale and rental tax; then compare against an investment plan using risk, fees, and a range of explicit return assumptions—not a single promised return.

  5. 05
    Commit only after deadlines are protected.

    Check Prop 19 occupancy and filing clocks immediately. Do not assume a later move-in restores an exclusion retroactively.
