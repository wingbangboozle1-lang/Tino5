Category 03 · Decision analysis

Live in it, rent it, or sell?

The objective is after-tax, risk-adjusted value—not maximum gross rent or sale price. The three paths use the same private facts and must be compared on the same horizon.

Evidence-based result today: no option can be ranked reliably. A live-in recommendation could depend on Prop 19 eligibility and avoided rent elsewhere; a rental recommendation depends on verified net cash flow and lease law; a sale recommendation depends on date-of-death basis, transaction costs, taxes, reinvestment risk and the other property's net cash flow. The inputs supplied to the project are private and unverified, so no invented net dollar figure or profit ranking is published.

Provisional recommendation

Preserve flexibility rather than sell or commit to a long lease based on the current unverified inputs. If a future parent-to-child transfer appears eligible, prioritize prompt assessor advice and Prop 19 deadlines; this is a potentially time-limited legal choice, not a guaranteed tax saving. Obtain private records and model all three paths on the same annual and long-horizon basis. Rank only when the after-tax cash flows and personal use value are documented.

The public site cannot determine the family's preferences, exact eligibility, future law, or risk tolerance and is not a personalized financial recommendation.
