Category 01 · California property tax

Assessment rules and the inheritance fork

A low existing assessment is not automatically inherited. The property’s title, transferor/transferee relationship, family-home status, date-of-death value, and timely claims determine the outcome.

For 2040–2050: today's statute and allowance are not a forecast of the law or assessed value at that time. Recheck the BOE and county assessor rules before relying on any current amount.

Prop 13 baselineCalifornia’s basic ad valorem property-tax rate is limited to 1% of assessed value, plus the rate needed for local voter-approved bonded indebtedness. Annual assessed-value inflation adjustments are capped at 2% when no reassessment event changes the base; separate direct assessments or charges may also appear on a bill.Do not apply 1% to a market estimate as if it were the entire bill; local voter-approved charges and parcel-specific assessment matter. County statements are required for a property-specific bill.CA BOE, Property Taxes: A Guide for Property Owners
Reassessment eventChange in ownership or new construction generally establishes a new base-year value at current market value; legal exclusions may apply.A trust inheritance can be a change in ownership for property-tax purposes even if beneficiaries receive title later. A Prop 19 exclusion is conditional, not automatic.CA BOE, Prop 19 Fact Sheet (June 2025)
Parent-to-child family homeCurrent BOE guidance permits a qualifying family-home exclusion if the home was the transferor’s principal residence and the transferee occupies it as their principal residence within one year; relationship and filing requirements apply.Renting or selling immediately may not meet the family-home condition. Check legal relationship, prior occupancy, title and trust terms with the assessor before assuming any exclusion.CA BOE, Prop 19 Fact Sheet
One-year requirementTo receive exclusion from the transfer date, the transferee must file a homeowners’ or disabled-veterans’ exemption claim within one year. For inherited property, the transfer date is the date of death, including property held in trust.Calendar this from date of death—not the later trust distribution date. Late claim relief is prospective, and does not waive the move-in requirement.CA BOE, Prop 19 Fact Sheet
BOE-19-P filing windowThe parent-child exclusion claim is generally due within three years of transfer and before transfer to a third party; a special six-month window after certain assessment notices is also described by BOE.Do not wait for a tax bill or sale decision. Confirm the exact deadline and currently required forms with the county assessor.CA BOE, Prop 19 Fact Sheet
Current value-limit incrementFor transfers Feb. 16, 2025–Feb. 15, 2027, the increment is about $1,044,590 (rounded to the nearest $10), added to the transferor’s factored base-year value. If market value exceeds the limit, the excess is added to that factored value.Need the actual date-of-death market value and factored base-year value to calculate the new taxable value. The increment adjusts every two years and will not be assumed for 2040–2050.CA BOE, Prop 19 Fact Sheet
Illustrative formula (not a result)new taxable value = factored base-year value + max(0, date-of-transfer market value − (factored base-year value + applicable Prop 19 increment))This is only the qualifying family-home value-limit computation. All parcel-specific inputs are intentionally blank and excluded from this public site.CA BOE, Prop 19 Fact Sheet
Supplemental tax billA change in ownership may produce a supplemental assessment and bill in addition to the regular annual bill; a county notice describes the assessed-value change.Budget for a possible additional bill after a transfer. No individual bill or parcel record is published here.Santa Clara County Assessor, supplemental assessment FAQ · San Francisco Assessor-Recorder
Official record checkCounty assessors maintain the official assessed-value roll and provide portals to search parcel-specific assessment information.Obtain private copies of the latest secured tax bills, notices, parcel history, exemptions, supplemental bills and recorded vesting; do not publish parcel identifiers.Santa Clara County Assessor · San Francisco Assessor-Recorder
Future assessment schedule2040–2050 dollar values: left blank; no verified parcel values or future transfer-date market values.The annual up-to-2% cap applies to an eligible existing taxable base, not a prediction of market value and not necessarily the post-inheritance tax base.CA BOE property-tax guide
