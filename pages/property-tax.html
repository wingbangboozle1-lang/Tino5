Property tax

The largest cost this analysis can actually control. Proposition 13 fixes the levy and caps assessment growth; Proposition 19 decides whether a death resets the assessment to market value. For Property A the difference is roughly $21,300 a year — and unlike the federal estate tax, it applies every year.
