Do not publish private records

The public site excludes names, exact addresses, ZIP codes, parcel IDs, exact home values, rent, utility bills, ownership documents and beneficiary/estate amounts. Keep those in a private, access-controlled working record; share only with the trustee, qualified tax/legal professionals, insurer and relevant county offices.
