Operating costs

Water, power and repairs can be checked against published tariffs and they largely reconcile with what the owner reports. Insurance cannot be checked publicly, and both of the owner's $1,500 estimates are almost certainly too low for buildings of this value. The model prices insurance as a rate on replacement cost and shows the sensitivity rather than inventing a quote.
