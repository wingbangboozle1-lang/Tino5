The inheritance process, step by step

Inheriting by trust is mostly not a legal problem — it is a calendar problem. Five deadlines decide most of the money, and four of them fall inside the first twelve months. This page is organised as the sequence you would actually execute, with the statutory authority for each step.

Two documents that must exist before any of this works

A funded trust (the deed shows the trustee) and a trust that is included in the grantor's estate (for the step-up). If either is missing, the process above still runs — it just costs far more. See flags F-14 and F-13.
