Inheritance tax

There is no California inheritance tax and no California estate tax. The federal estate tax exists but its exclusion is $15,000,000 per person for 2026 and indexed thereafter, so it is unlikely to apply to this family at all. The taxes that do apply to an inheritance are income tax (on later gain), property tax (the Prop 19 reassessment), and the income-tax benefit of the step-up in basis — which is the item actually worth planning around.
