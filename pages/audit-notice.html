Reviewed 11 November 2026. These issues affect the existing published dashboards; an automated pass is not proof of privacy or accuracy. Do not use the existing recommendation as a decision or tax calculation.

Privacy: public exposure

The existing public Pages site and repository include detailed property histories and unrounded owner-provided figures, including in non-HTML assets. The combination of location, property characteristics and financial history can be identifying even when names and addresses are absent. Merely rounding does not guarantee anonymity. The public Git history can retain prior versions even after files are changed; search-engine caches and copies may also remain. See GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub guidance on removing sensitive data.

Material decision and tax issues

  • Already-owned property: At least one companion analysis treats the already-owned property as part of the other person's inherited estate and assumes a new basis on it. This is inconsistent with the brief; estate inclusion depends on the decedent's actual ownership or other includible interest, not simply the beneficiary's ownership. IRS estate tax; IRS Publication 551.
  • Estate tax cannot be ruled out: the size and nature of the other estate assets, lifetime taxable gifts, marital facts and trust provisions are not known. A basic exclusion is not proof that any particular estate is exempt. Filing and tax calculations are distinct. IRS estate tax.
  • Basis and sale tax: a date-of-death basis adjustment is not automatic for every trust asset; a sale can have post-death appreciation, selling expenses, or other tax consequences. The quoted benefit and zero-tax assertions are not verified. IRS Publication 551; IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-2.
  • Property tax: the intergenerational exclusion is conditional, and a value above the applicable limit can still be added to the taxable value. Future law and the applicable indexed limit are unknown. California Board of Equalization: Proposition 19.
  • Model certainty: numerical outcomes depend on unverified property values, rents, insurance, trust terms, tax assumptions, and long-term returns. Some tables display owner-provided or modeled figures as though they were independently confirmed, and some rows lack their own source. Official sources cannot verify the user's specific private records. No recommendation or 50-year forecast can be certified on this evidence. SEC: stocks and investment risk.
