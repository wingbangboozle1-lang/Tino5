REFERENCE / CURRENT LAW

Tax & inheritance rules

Verified legal rules available as of 10 November 2026. Dollar figures are rounded to the nearest $10. A rule in force today is not a projection of the law in 2040–2050.

Important distinction. U.S. federal estate tax is generally imposed on the taxable estate, not as a tax bill on a beneficiary simply for receiving an inheritance. Income earned later (rent, interest, dividends, or taxable gain on sale) can be taxable to its recipient. A trust does not automatically exempt assets from estate tax or county reassessment.

CALIFORNIA & COUNTY PROPERTY TAX

Assessment, inherited homes, and Proposition 19

TopicVerified rule / valuePlanning implicationStatusOfficial source
California inheritance / estate taxFor decedents dying on or after 1 January 2005, California estate-tax return filing is no longer required under the State Controller’s stated rules; the FTB says gifts/inheritances are not individual taxable income, while later income may be taxable.Do not confuse absence of California inheritance/estate tax with federal estate tax, reassessed property tax, or post-inheritance income tax.VERIFIED · CURRENT STATE GUIDANCECalifornia State Controller ↗ · California FTB ↗
Prop 13 baselineGenerally, base property tax is 1% of assessed value plus voter-approved bonded indebtedness. Annual factored base-year value increase is generally capped at the lower of inflation or 2%; a qualifying change in ownership or new construction can reset the base.Actual parcel tax bill includes local voter-approved charges and any applicable exemptions; the 1% figure is not a complete local bill estimate.VERIFIED · GENERAL RULECalifornia BOE, Pub. 800-10 ↗
Prop 19 intergenerational family-home eligibilityFor qualifying parent-child transfers (and limited grandparent-grandchild transfers), the home generally must have been the transferor’s principal residence and become the transferee’s principal residence within 1 year; the claimant must file the homeowners’ or disabled-veterans’ exemption within 1 year to receive relief from the transfer date.Living in the inherited home may qualify; keeping it only as a rental or investment generally does not. Confirm family relationship, transferor occupancy, transferee occupancy, and county forms.CONDITIONALCalifornia BOE Prop 19 ↗
Prop 19 value cap, transfers 16 Feb 2025–15 Feb 2027Allowance is $1,044,586 plus the home’s factored base-year value at transfer; displayed value rounded to nearest $10 as $1,044,590. If market value exceeds the combined limit, the excess is added to the taxable value.This current allowance is not an estimate of the amount applicable to a transfer around 2040–2050. BOE adjusts the amount every 2 years; a transfer at that future date requires the then-current figure.VERIFIED · DATE-LIMITEDCalifornia BOE, 2025 notice ↗
When inherited property changes ownership for assessmentFor California property-tax purposes, death generally triggers change-in-ownership review on date of death, even if property remains in a trust and is distributed later; an exclusion may apply.Do not assume trust distribution date controls the assessment date. File the death statement and applicable exclusion claims with each county assessor.VERIFIED · GENERAL RULECalifornia BOE, death of owner guidance ↗
