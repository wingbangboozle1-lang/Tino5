COSTS & PROJECTIONS

Every future dollar is blank until its inputs are supportable.

No future value, tax bill, premium, repair reserve, water cost, power credit, or investment return is stated as fact. Current home-specific amounts have been removed from the public site for privacy, and user-supplied figures have not been independently verified.

!
Projection integrity rule

California assessed values are not a simple forecast of market prices. Proposition 13 assessment increases are generally capped at the lower of inflation or 2% while ownership is unchanged; a change in ownership or new construction can establish a new base-year value, subject to exclusions. A future inheritance could therefore cause a large reassessment or qualify for a partial exclusion. We do not extend today’s rules through 2040–2050 as if they were guaranteed.

i
Future estate-tax threshold

The 2026 federal basic exclusion is $15,000,000 per person. Under current federal law it is inflation-adjusted for 2027 and later years, so the 2040–2050 nominal threshold is currently unknown; Congress can change law again. The exclusion applies to the taxable estate and adjusted taxable gifts, not just inherited cash. For a death-year calculation, use that year’s IRS threshold and qualified estate-tax advice.
