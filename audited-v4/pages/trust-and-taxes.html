Step-by-Step Trust Real Property Inheritance Process

Strict statutory deadlines govern Prop 19 retroactivity, DSUE estate portability, and IRC §1014 basis documentation.

Phase 0: Now (2026–Pre-Death)
Verify Trust Funding, Title Vesting & Avoid LLC Disqualification

Confirm both Cupertino (95014) and San Francisco (94122) deeds are recorded in the [Revocable Living Trust] (never inside an LLC or corporation—per BOE LTA 2026/026 Q32–Q35, properties held by legal entities cannot claim Prop 19). Document California Community Property status so the surviving spouse receives a 100% double basis step-up (IRS Pub. 523). Evaluate annual exclusion gifting ($19,000 in 2026; $20,000 in 2027 per recipient per parent per Rev. Proc. 2026-35) if combined assets approach federal estate thresholds.

Days 1–60 Post-Death
Trustee Notice (Probate Code §16061.7), EIN & Date-of-Death Valuations

When the trust becomes irrevocable upon death, [Beneficiary / Successor Trustee] must serve formal written Probate Code §16061.7 notice on all beneficiaries and heirs within 60 days, triggering the 120-day contest window (CA Prob. Code §16061.7). Obtain an IRS EIN for the irrevocable administrative trust, order certified death certificates, and commission licensed MAI/certified date-of-death appraisals for both properties to establish stepped-up IRC §1014 / CA R&TC §18031 tax basis (CA FTB Sched. D).

Within 150 Days
Record Affidavit of Death of Trustee, BOE-502-D & BOE-502-A (PCOR)

Record an Affidavit of Death of Trustee with the Santa Clara County Clerk-Recorder and SF Assessor-Recorder, alongside Form BOE-502-D (Change in Ownership Statement — Death of Real Property Owner, due within 150 days of death per R&TC §480(b)) and Form BOE-502-A (Preliminary Change in Ownership Report). Claim transfer tax exemption as an inheritance/trust administration transfer (SF Transfer Tax Exemptions; Santa Clara Clerk-Recorder).

Within 9 Months (First & Second Death)
File IRS Form 706 (Even If Below $15M+ for DSUE Portability) & Trust Distribution Tax Check

Upon the first spouse's death, filing Form 706 within 9 months (plus automatic 6-month extension via Form 4768, or up to 5 years under Rev. Proc. 2022-32) preserves the deceased spouse's unused exclusion (DSUE portability), doubling the surviving parent's shield to $30.0M (2026) / $30.96M (2027) indexed (IRS Form 706 Instructions). Note: Trusts hit the 37% top federal bracket at just $16,000 (2026) / $16,500 (2027) of retained income—distribute income or property promptly rather than trapping rental cash inside an administrative trust (Rev. Proc. 2026-35).

Within 1 Year (Prop 19 Critical)
Occupy Chosen Principal Residence & File Form BOE-266 (Homeowners' Exemption)

To qualify for Prop 19 retroactive exclusion back to date of death, an eligible child must move into the parent's principal residence within 1 year of death AND file Form BOE-266 (Homeowners' Exemption, $7,000 AV reduction) within 1 year (BOE Pub. 801; BOE Pub. 800-6). Missing the 1-year BOE-266 deadline limits relief to prospective years only.

Within 3 Years
File Form BOE-19-P (Parent-Child Exclusion) & Execute Buy/Keep/Sell Plan

Submit Form BOE-19-P to the County Assessor within 3 years of date of death (or 6 months after supplemental/escape assessment notice, and before any third-party sale) (BOE LTA 2026/026; Santa Clara Assessor Prop 19). If selling either property via Strategy C, D, or E, execute sale promptly after appraisal so IRC §1014 basis equals sale proceeds ($0 capital gains tax).
