Five Critical Audited Corrections to Baseline Assumptions:
  • Prop 19 Primary-Residence Conflict: Only one parent primary residence qualifies—and per BOE LTA 2026/026 Q5, on a multi-unit building like SF, only the single occupied unit could ever qualify while rental units are 100% reassessed.
  • San Francisco Transfer Tax Cliff ($5.0M): Per SF Assessor Article 12-C, SF transfer tax triples from 0.75% ($1M–<$5M) to 2.25% on the entire price at $5,000,000+ (triggered around 2048 in High-Growth scenarios).
  • Insurance Underestimates ($1,500/yr): CDI 2025 data averages $2,340/yr ($3.38/$1k) statewide Homeowners + +32.8% rental dwelling rate hikes; scaled rebuild quotes are typically ~$4,230 (Cupertino) and ~$4,800 (SF 3-unit).
  • Cupertino Solar Policy Sunset: SVCE halved Net Surplus Compensation (2x→1x) in 2026, PG&E added a non-offsettable $24.15/mo Base Services Charge (CPUC D.24-05-028), and 20-yr NEM legacy expires onto NBT around 2037–2043.
  • SFPUC Utility Surge & Rent Control Squeeze: SFPUC's 10-Year Financial Plan projects +6.4%/yr water and +10.1%/yr sewer hikes while SF Rent Board caps annual increases at 1.6% (2026–27).
