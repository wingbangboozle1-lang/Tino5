Deep-Dive Reasoning: Live vs. Rent vs. Sell

Why Live in SF Unit 3 + Sell Cupertino (Plan A) Wins

Living in SF Unit 3 unlocks free Bay Area housing without sacrificing the $85,800/yr (2026$) income from Units 1 & 2. Selling Cupertino immediately post-inheritance uses IRC § 1014 to erase $2.5M+ of embedded capital gains tax, eliminates ~$73k–$117k/yr in non-owner-occupied Cupertino property taxes, and shifts concentrated real estate into liquid, compounding S&P 500 equities.

When "Sell Both" (Plan E) Makes Sense

If [Beneficiary / Heir Placeholder] prefers zero landlord management, wants to leave the Bay Area, or finds SF's pre-1979 rent control & SFPUC water escalations burdensome, selling both properties immediately at death (stepped-up basis on both!) delivers roughly equal after-tax wealth—provided SF is sold before crossing SF's $5,000,000 2.25% transfer tax cliff.

Why Pre-Death SF Move Boosts Plan A by ~$16,490/yr

Try toggling "Transferor Principal Residence → SF Unit 3" in the simulator above!
