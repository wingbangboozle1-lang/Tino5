Estate Tax Mitigation Playbook (If Total Estate Approaches Cap)

1. Always File IRS Form 706 at First Parent's Death (Portability): Even if the first spouse's estate is under $15M and owes $0 tax, failing to file Form 706 forfeits DSUE portability—cutting the family's combined tax-free shield from ~$50.70M down to ~$25.35M in 2045!
2. Gift High-Basis Cash, NEVER Low-Basis Cupertino/SF Real Estate: Real estate appreciation (~$7M–$14M combined gain) gets wiped out by IRC § 1014 step-up only if held until death. If liquid funds threaten the threshold, gift high-basis cash using the $19,000/yr (or $38,000/yr married) per-donee exclusion (and direct 529/tuition/medical payments, which are unlimited and tax-free!).
