Every statutory deadline and form cites its official government authority.

Phase 0 Pre-Death Audit
(2026–2040+)

Verify Trust Type, Titling & Pre-Death Principal Residence Strategy

  • Confirm both properties are deeded to [Family Trust Name Placeholder] as a revocable living trust (includible in gross estate for 100% IRC § 1014 step-up; avoid irrevocable grantor trust traps under IRS Rev. Rul. 2023-2).
  • If parents are married, verify Community Property characterization for 100% basis step-up on both halves at the first death (IRS Pub 551) and non-pro-rata distribution authority in the trust (BOE LTA 2026/026 Q41).
  • Confirm whether parent wants to establish primary residence in SF Unit 3 before death (if Plan A is chosen) or remain in Cupertino (if Plan C/D is chosen).
  • Verify solar PTO installation date on Cupertino and building certificate-of-occupancy date on SF 3-unit.
Phase 1 Days 1–30
Post-Death

Obtain Death Certificates, Trust EIN & Immediate Asset Protection

  • Order 8–12 certified Death Certificates (needed for both Santa Clara County & San Francisco County recordings, banks, brokerages, and IRS filings).
  • Obtain a new Trust EIN (post-death administrative trust becomes a separate taxpayer) and notify existing homeowners/landlord insurers immediately of trustee death to add [Successor Trustee Placeholder] / estate as named insured (preventing vacancy/unlisted-insured coverage gaps).
  • Keep secured property tax installments current (Dec 10 & Apr 10 delinquency deadlines; 10% late penalty under County Tax Rules).
Phase 2 Days 30–90
Appraisals & Notices

Date-of-Death Appraisals & Probate Code Trust Notices

  • Order certified Date-of-Death MAI/residential appraisals for [Cupertino Residence — ZIP 95014] and [San Francisco 3-Unit Property — ZIP 94122] (establishing new stepped-up basis under IRC § 1014 and consistent basis under § 1014(f); IRS Pub 551). Separate land vs. structure values for 27.5-yr rental depreciation.
  • Send California Probate Code § 16061.7 statutory Notification by Trustee to beneficiaries/heirs within 60 days.
  • File Affidavit of Death of Trustee (with certified death certificate) in Santa Clara County and San Francisco County Recorder offices to clear chain of title (BOE Deed Recording Guide).
Phase 3 Within 150 Days
Mandatory Filing

File Form BOE-502-D (Death of Real Property Owner) in Both Counties

  • Under CA Rev. & Tax. Code § 480(b), file separate Form BOE-502-D with Santa Clara County Assessor AND San Francisco Assessor-Recorder within 150 days of date of death (BOE Pub 800-9).
  • Note: Date of death—not the later trust distribution deed date—is the legal date of change in ownership for property tax reassessment.
Phase 4 Within 9 Months
Federal IRS Filing

File IRS Form 706 (Estate Tax & DSUE Portability) + Final 1040 / Trust 1041

  • File decedent's final personal income tax returns (Federal Form 1040 & CA Form 540) and Trust fiduciary return (IRS Form 1041 / CA Form 541).
  • If gross estate + adjusted taxable gifts exceeds the indexed OBBBA exclusion ($15M in 2026; ~$25.35M in 2045) OR if surviving spouse elects DSUE portability, file IRS Form 706 within 9 months (+ 6-mo extension; IRS Form 706 Instructions). Note Form 8971 consistent basis reporting if Form 706 is required.
Phase 5 Within 1 Year
Prop 19 Deadline

Establish Occupancy & File BOE-266 (Homeowners' Exemption) If Claiming Prop 19

  • If [Beneficiary / Heir Placeholder] is occupying a qualifying parent principal residence (Cupertino under Plan C/D, or SF Unit 3 if parent lived there under Plan A), move in and file Form BOE-266 within 1 year of date of death (BOE LTA 2026/026).
  • Remember: If executing Plan A (Sell Cupertino) while parent lived in Cupertino, do NOT delay Cupertino's sale—list and sell quickly while stepped-up basis equals sale price (~$0 gain/loss except closing costs!).
  • Record Trust Distribution Grant Deed + PCOR claiming documentary transfer tax exemption under RTC § 11930 (CA RTC § 11930) and SF Transfer Tax Affidavit (SF Assessor).
Phase 6 Within 3 Years
BOE-19-P & Operations

File Form BOE-19-P, Re-Title Utilities, Insurance & SF Rent Board

  • File Form BOE-19-P within 3 years of death (or within 6 months of supplemental/escape assessment notice, whichever is later) for any qualifying Prop 19 unit (BOE Pub 800-9).
  • Transfer PG&E (inheriting remaining 20-year NEM legacy period if keeping Cupertino; PG&E NEM FAQ), SJWC / SFPUC water accounts, and bind permanent owner-occupied + landlord insurance policies.
  • Update SF Rent Board Housing Inventory & pay annual $59/unit fee on rented SF units while claiming owner-occupied exemption on SF Unit 3 (SF Rent Board).
