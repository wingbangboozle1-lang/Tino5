Statute / Provision Current Verified Rule (2026) Impact on Cupertino / SF Decision (2040–2050) Status Official Source
OBBBA Estate & Gift Basic Exclusion (P.L. 119-21) $15,000,000 per individual in 2026 (up from $13,990,000 in 2025); indexed for inflation starting 2027. Top tax rate 40% ($5,945,800 basic credit). Married couples w/ DSUE portability: $30,000,000. Eliminated the 2026 TCJA sunset cliff. Real estate ($12.06M in 2045) fits under a single $25.35M indexed exemption unless liquid estate pushes total over threshold. Enacted Law IRS Estate & Gift
Annual Gift Tax Exclusion (IRC § 2503(b)) $19,000 per donee per year in 2025 & 2026 ($194,000 for non-citizen spouse). Married couples splitting gifts: $38,000/donee/yr. Allows gradual tax-free gifting of liquid cash/high-basis funds to trim estate size without using lifetime exemption or forfeiting real estate basis step-up. Verified 2026 IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32
Step-Up in Basis at Death (IRC § 1014 & § 1014(f)) Inherited property in gross estate resets basis to Date-of-Death FMV (100% step-up on both halves of CA community property). Consistent basis reporting required under § 1014(f). Enables immediate tax-free sale of Cupertino (~$7.71M in 2045) or SF with ~$0 federal/CA capital gains tax. Never gift appreciated houses during life. Verified Law IRS Pub 551

Rul. 2023-2) Assets in an irrevocable grantor trust that are not includible in the decedent's gross estate receive NO § 1014 basis step-up (keep carryover basis). Must verify [Family Trust Name Placeholder] is a revocable living trust (or estate-included trust), otherwise selling inherited Cupertino triggers ~$2.5M+ tax. IRS Ruling IRS Rev. Rul. 2023-2
Federal Long-Term Capital Gains & NIIT (2026) 0% up to $98,900 MFJ ($49,450 Single); 15% up to $613,700 MFJ ($545,500 Single); 20% above. Plus 3.8% NIIT over $250k MFJ / $200k Single = 23.8% top federal. Applies to post-inheritance appreciation & dividends if properties/stocks are sold later (prior to heir's own death). Verified 2026 IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32
Primary Residence Sale Exclusion (IRC § 121) Exclude up to $250,000 (Single) / $500,000 (MFJ) gain on main home owned & occupied 2 of last 5 years (subject to nonqualified use rules if rented first). Partially shields future growth if living in Cupertino or SF Unit 3 (for SF, applies only to the owner-occupied 1/3 unit, not the 2 rental units). Verified Law IRS Sec. 121 FAQ
SALT Deduction Cap Reversion (OBBBA § 70120) Raised temporarily to $40,400 in 2026 ($20,200 MFS; $40,000 in 2025) with MAGI phaseout to $10,000 floor; reverts to $10,000 in 2030. During 2040–2050, personal residence property tax ($51k–$84k Cupertino) is mostly non-deductible, whereas rental property taxes on Schedule E remain 100% deductible. Enacted Sunset IRS Pub. 6079
Residential Rental Depreciation & Recapture (Pub 527) 27.5-year straight-line MACRS GDS on stepped-up building structure basis (excluding land). Subject to max 25% Unrecaptured § 1250 tax upon sale. Resets depreciable basis on SF's 2 rented units (or Cupertino if rented) to inheritance-date building FMV, sheltering annual rental cash flow. Verified Law IRS Pub. 527
119th Congress Proposed Tax Bills (Watchlist) H.R. 2038 proposes lowering estate threshold & tightening grantor trust basis; H.R. 601 proposes 20% estate rate; H.Res. 206 supports preserving § 1014 step-up. None enacted as of Nov 2026 (all in committee), but future Congresses before 2040–2050 could reduce the $15M exclusion or limit § 1014 step-up. Proposed Only Congress.gov H.R. 2038

Documentary Transfer Tax Inheritance Exemption (RTC § 11930) Transfers by inter vivos gift, death, or trust distribution without consideration are 100% exempt from county/city documentary transfer taxes. $0 county/city transfer tax when inheriting Cupertino or SF properties via trust.
