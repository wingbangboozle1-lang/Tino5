From trust transfer to a decision

TopicFinding / actionStatusOfficial source
1 · Before transferPrivately review deed, trust amendments, successor trustee powers, beneficiaries, debt, ownership shares and inclusion in the transferor's estate. No document uploads to this site.Planning checklist, not legal instructionIRS · Estate tax
2 · At deathIdentify authorized trustee/executor, obtain records securely, secure property and notify insurer. Determine whether assets pass through trust or require probate.General checklistCalifornia Courts · Probate guide
3 · Inventory and valueInventory assets and liabilities; obtain defensible date-of-death valuation and preserve basis documentation.General checklistIRS · Inherited basis
4 · Determine estate filingsEvaluate gross estate, lifetime taxable gifts, deductions, marital provisions and any portability election; tax liability is not just inheritance × a penalty rate.General checklistIRS · Estate tax
5 · Review property-tax deadlinesContact assessor promptly about change-in-ownership reporting and supplemental assessment. If genuinely occupying and eligible, meet the one-year homeowners-exemption deadline.Verified timing; parcel process needs reviewBOE · Proposition 19
6 · Complete exclusion claimFile applicable family-home exclusion within three years or before third-party transfer; confirm residence, relationship and value-limit evidence.Verified summary; case-specific review neededBOE · Proposition 19 fact sheet (Pub 801)
7 · Settle and reserveAuthorized fiduciary addresses debts, expenses, taxes and distributions according to governing documents and applicable process. Keep funds for unsettled liabilities.General checklistCalifornia Courts · Probate guide
8 · Choose and documentCompare verified net sale proceeds to rental NOI and occupancy benefit; confirm policy changes and basis before executing leases, distribution or sale.Conditional decision gateIRS · Topic 703 (basis)
9 · Review annuallyRefresh insurance, leases, capital needs, tax bills and continued eligibility; re-evaluate law before the actual future transfer.Planning recommendationBOE · Proposition 19
