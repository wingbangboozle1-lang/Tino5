What changed: The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA, P.L. 119-21, signed July 4, 2025) cancelled the scheduled TCJA sunset that would have cut the exemption roughly in half in 2026, set $15,000,000 for 2026, made it permanent (no sunset), and indexed it for inflation starting 2027. Congress can still change it — that is the main federal risk to 2040s planning. Source: IRS; Rev. Proc. 2025-32.

Projection to 2040s: Exemption in 2040 = $15,000,000 × (cumulative inflation 2027–2040). At 2%/yr that is ≈ $19,740,000 (modelled). Houses at 3% growth to 2040 ≈ $7,411,690 combined (modelled, see Projections). Whether the estate exceeds the indexed exemption depends on the unknown cash amount (F-01). Do not assume “no tax” without a CPA computation at the time.
